# Text of House Amendment 37

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 37: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to compel a journalist or reporter to testify about 
     information or sources that the journalist or reporter 
     informs the Attorney General that he has obtained as a 
     journalist or reporter and that he regards as confidential.
```
