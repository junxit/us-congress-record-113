# Text of House Amendment 27

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Walberg

       Amendment No. 27: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the Investigative and Public Affairs Unit of the 
     Federal Bureau of Investigation except for the Ten Most 
     Wanted Fugitives, the Most Wanted Terrorists, and missing 
     children programs.
```
