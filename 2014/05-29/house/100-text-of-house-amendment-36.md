# Text of House Amendment 36

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 36: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to prosecute any person for violations of an online 
     service's user agreement or terms of service.
```
