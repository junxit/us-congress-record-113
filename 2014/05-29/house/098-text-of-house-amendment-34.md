# Text of House Amendment 34

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 34: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the National Institute of Standards and Technology 
     (``NIST'') to incorporate any weaknesses known to NIST into 
     encryption standards.
```
