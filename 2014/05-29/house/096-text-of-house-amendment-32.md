# Text of House Amendment 32

**Speaking:** Huffman, Jared (H001068) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Huffman

       Amendment No. 32: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to assess or collect the fee established by section 
     660.115 of title 50, Code of Federal Regulations.
```
