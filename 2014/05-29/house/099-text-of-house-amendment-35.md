# Text of House Amendment 35

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 35: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to imprison a person if that person has been 
     incarcerated continuously for 15 years or more and if the 
     sole basis for the incarceration is a conviction for a 
     nonviolent crime resulting in a pecuniary gain to the 
     prisoner of less than $1,000,000

[[Page H5025]]

     and a pecuniary loss to the victim or victims of less than 
     $1,000,000, as stated in the prisoner's sentencing report.
```
