                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, the Senate will resume consideration of the motion 
to concur in the House amendment to the Senate amendment to H.R. 3979, 
postcloture.

hour; Coburn, 1 hour; Murkowski, 30 minutes; Inhofe, 30 minutes, or 
their designees.
  Upon the use or yielding back of that time, at approximately 3 p.m.,

authorization bill and the Saperstein nomination.
