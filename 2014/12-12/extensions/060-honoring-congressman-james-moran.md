# HONORING CONGRESSMAN JAMES MORAN

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

```
                    HONORING CONGRESSMAN JAMES MORAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETTY McCOLLUM

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2014

  Ms. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to pay tribute to the career 
of Congressman James Moran. I had the pleasure of serving alongside Jim 
on the Appropriations Committee, including both the Defense and 
Interior-Environment subcommittees. Congressman Moran's experience and 
wisdom not only benefited his district and the nation, but myself as 
well.
  Congressman Moran has always brought passion to his work in Congress 
and that passion will be sorely missed next year.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring the service of Congressman 
James Moran in the United States House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
