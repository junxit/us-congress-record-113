# FEDERAL CORRECTIONAL OFFICER SCOTT J. WILLIAMS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5562

```
  FEDERAL CORRECTIONAL OFFICER SCOTT J. WILLIAMS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE 
                                BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5562) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 801 West Ocean Avenue in Lompoc, California, 
as the ``Federal Correctional Officer Scott J. Williams Memorial Post 
Office Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third 
time, and passed.
```
