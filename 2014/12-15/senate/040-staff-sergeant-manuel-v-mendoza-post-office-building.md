# STAFF SERGEANT MANUEL V. MENDOZA POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4416

```
         STAFF SERGEANT MANUEL V. MENDOZA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4416) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 161 Live Oak Street in Miami, Arizona, as the 
``Staff Sergeant Manuel V. Mendoza Post Office Building,'' was ordered 
to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
