# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3189, H.R. 3973, H.R. 4015

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:05 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3189. An act to prohibit the conditioning of any 
     permit, lease, or other use agreement on the transfer of any 
     water right to the United States by the Secretaries of the 
     Interior and Agriculture.
       H.R. 3973. An act to amend section 530D of title 28, United 
     States Code.
       H.R. 4015. An act to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to repeal the Medicare sustainable growth rate 
     and improve Medicare payments for physicians and other 
     professionals, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
