# TRIBUTE TO ALEX CARAMES

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ALEX CARAMES

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Alex Carames, a 
2013 summer intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.

a dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of his internship experience.

all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
