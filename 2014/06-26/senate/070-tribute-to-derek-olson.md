# TRIBUTE TO DEREK OLSON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO DEREK OLSON

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Derek Olson, an 
intern in

[[Page S4151]]

my Washington, DC office, for all the hard work he has done for me, my 
staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Derek is a graduate of Dakota Valley High School in North Sioux City, 
SD. Recently, Derek graduated from Iowa State University where he 
majored in political science. He is a dedicated worker who has been 
committed to getting the most out of his experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Derek for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
