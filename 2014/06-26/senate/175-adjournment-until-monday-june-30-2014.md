# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 30, 2014

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 494

```
                ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 30, 2014

  Mr. REID. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
provisions of S. Res. 494 as a further mark of respect to the memory of 
the late Senator Howard Baker of Tennessee.

Monday, June 30, 2014, at 12 noon.
```
