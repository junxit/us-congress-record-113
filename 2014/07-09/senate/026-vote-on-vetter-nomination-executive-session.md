# Vote on Vetter Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                       Vote on Vetter Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will be 2 
minutes of debate divided in the usual form.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I yield back the time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, all time is yielded back.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Darci L. Vetter, of Nebraska, to be Chief Agricultural Negotiator, 
Office of the United States Trade Representative, with the rank of 
Ambassador?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
