                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Strategic Forces of the Committee on Armed Services and 
the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be authorized to 
meet during the session of the Senate on July 16, 2014, at 9:30 a.m.
