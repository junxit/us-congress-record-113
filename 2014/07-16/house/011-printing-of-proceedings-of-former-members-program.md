# PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS OF FORMER MEMBERS PROGRAM

```
           PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS OF FORMER MEMBERS PROGRAM

  Mr. STIVERS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
proceedings during the former Members program be printed in the 
Congressional Record and that all Members and former Members who spoke 
during the proceedings have the privilege of revising and extending 
their remarks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
  The following proceedings were held before the House convened for 
morning-hour debate:


  United States Association of Former Members of Congress 2014 Annual 
                           Report to Congress

  The meeting was called to order by the Honorable Barbara Kennelly, 
vice president of Former Members of Congress Association, at 8:05 a.m.
```
