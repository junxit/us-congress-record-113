# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII,

           Mr. FATTAH (for himself and Mr. Wolf): introduced a 
             bill (H.R. 5158) to provide for the sealing or 
             expungement of records relating to Federal nonviolent 
             criminal offenses, and for other purposes; which was 
             referred to the Committee on the Judiciary, and in 
             addition to the Committees on Agriculture, and Ways 
             and Means, for a period to be subsequently determined 
             by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of 
             such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of
```
