# Text of House Amendment 34

**Speaking:** Richmond, Cedric L. (R000588) [D-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                        Offered By: Mr. Richmond

       Amendment No. 34: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to recover Community Development Block Grant disaster 
     recovery funds from any eligible homeowner recipient impacted 
     by Hurricane Katrina or Hurricane Rita who used such funds to 
     restore the homeowner's home to a habitable state and has 
     made an honest attempt to utilize the funds for their 
     intended purpose and comply with the covenants of the grant 
     agreement.
```
