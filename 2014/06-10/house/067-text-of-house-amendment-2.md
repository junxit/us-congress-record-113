# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Hartzler, Vicky (H001053) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4800

```
H.R. 4800

                       Offered By: Mrs. Hartzler

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to implement section 12106 of the Agricultural Act of 
     2014 (Public Law 113-79; 128 Stat. 980), section 11016(b) of 
     the Food, Conservation, and Energy Act of 2008 (Public Law 
     110-246; 122 Stat. 2130), or the amendments made by such 
     sections.
```
