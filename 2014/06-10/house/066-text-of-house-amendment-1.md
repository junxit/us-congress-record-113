# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Huelskamp, Tim (H001057) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 4800

```
H.R. 4800

                       Offered By: Mr. Huelskamp

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to finalize, implement, administer, or enforce the 
     proposed rule entitled ``Importation of Beef From a Region in 
     Brazil'' published by the Department of Agriculture in the 
     Federal Register on December 23, 2013 (78 Fed. Reg. 77370 et 
     seq.)
```
