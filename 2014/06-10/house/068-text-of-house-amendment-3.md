# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4800

```
H.R. 4800

                       Offered By: Mrs. Blackburn

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. (a) Each amount made available by this Act is 
     hereby reduced by 1 percent.
       (b) The reduction in subsection (a) shall not apply with 
     respect to the following mandatory accounts:
       (1) ``Federal Crop Insurance Corporation Fund''.
       (2) ``Commodity Credit Corporation Fund--Reimbursement for 
     Net Realized Losses''.
       (3) ``Child Nutrition Programs''.
       (4) ``Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program''.
```
