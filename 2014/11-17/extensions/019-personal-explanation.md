**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JAMES P. McGOVERN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 17, 2014

Salvador to commemorate the assassination of six Jesuit priests 25 
years ago. As a result, I was absent for rollcall votes 518 and 519. If
