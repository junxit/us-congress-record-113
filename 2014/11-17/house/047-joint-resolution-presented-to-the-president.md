# JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 120

```
              JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on September 16, 
2014, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following joint resolution:

       H.J. Res. 120 Approving the location of a memorial to 
     commemorate the more than 5,000 slaves and free Black persons 
     who fought for independence in the American Revolution.
```
