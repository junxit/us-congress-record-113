# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, November 17, 
2014, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bills:

       S. 898. An act to authorize the Administrator of General 
     Services to convey a parcel of real property in Albuquerque, 
     New Mexico, to the Amy Biehl High School Foundation.
       S. 1934. An act to direct the Administrator of General 
     Services to convey the Clifford P. Hansen Federal Courthouse 
     to Teton County, Wyoming.
```
