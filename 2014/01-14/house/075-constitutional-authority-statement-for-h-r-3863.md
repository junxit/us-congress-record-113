# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3863

**Measures:** H.R. 3863

```
            By Mr. BRADY of Texas:
        H.R. 3863.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution which 
     outlines Congress' authority ``to regulate commerce . . . 
     among the several states.'' This is where Congress derives 
     its regulatory powers.
```
