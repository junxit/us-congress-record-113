# BOYS TOWN CENTENNIAL COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2866

```
              BOYS TOWN CENTENNIAL COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 15, 2014

  Ms. FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in support of H.R. 
2866, The Boys Town Centennial Commemorative Coin Act. This legislation 
will create a coin to honor Boys Town's longstanding commitment to the 
needs of at-risk children and struggling families in communities across 
America.
  Driven by the belief that every child deserves the opportunity to 
succeed, Boys Town serves children of every race, color, and religion. 
In my district, South Florida Boys Town provides incredible 
opportunities to help children achieve success and pursue their dreams.
  I am pleased to honor Boys Town and I urge my colleagues to vote for 
this legislation and I yield back my time.

                          ____________________
```
