# CONGRATULATING RICKY REGAS

```
                       CONGRATULATING RICKY REGAS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 25, 2014

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Ricky Regas, a 
junior at Cinco Ranch High School, on bringing home a gold medal from 
the University Interscholastic League State Wrestling Championship. 
Ricky is one of the 28 talented wrestlers from Katy, TX, to qualify for 
the state tournament.
  Ricky captured this year's gold medal in the 182 lb. bracket after a 
hard-fought final match. Ricky finished the year with a record of 34-0, 
an undefeated season. I hope to continue to see great things from Ricky 
in his senior season.
  Texas is home to many outstanding athletes, and becoming a state 
champion is not an easy feat. On behalf of all residents of the Twenty-
Second Congressional District of Texas, I am honored to recognize 
Ricky's accomplishment. Our community is proud of Ricky Regas.

                          ____________________
```
