# PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 83

```
        PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 83

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 776, the House 
is considered to have adopted House Concurrent Resolution 122.
  The text of House Concurrent Resolution 122 is as follows:

                            H. Con. Res. 122

       Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate 
     concurring), That in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 83, the 
     Clerk of the House of Representatives shall amend the long 
     title so as to read: ``Making consolidated appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2015, and for other 
     purposes.''.

                          ____________________
```
