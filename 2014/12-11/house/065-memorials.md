# MEMORIALS

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       335. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 189, 
     urging congressional intervention to stop the proposal to 
     close or consolidate the Lansing mail processing and 
     distribution center; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Government Reform.
       336. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 395, 
     urging the Congress to direct the Comptroller General to 
     complete a full audit of the Board of Governors of the 
     Federal Reserve System and the Federal Reserve banks; jointly 
     to the Committees on Oversight and Government Reform and 
     Financial Services.
```
