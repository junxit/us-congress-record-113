# CREDIT UNION SHARE INSURANCE FUND PARITY ACT

```
              CREDIT UNION SHARE INSURANCE FUND PARITY ACT

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Banking, 
Housing, and Urban Affairs Committee be discharged from further 
consideration of H.R. 3468 and the Senate proceed to its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3468) to amend the Federal Credit Union Act to 
     extend insurance coverage to amounts held in a member account 
     on behalf of another person, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. DURBIN. I further ask unanimous consent that the bill be read a 
third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The bill (H.R. 3468) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
