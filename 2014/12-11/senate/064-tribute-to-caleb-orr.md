# TRIBUTE TO CALEB ORR

```
                          TRIBUTE TO CALEB ORR

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Caleb Orr, a 
2014 summer intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Caleb is a sophomore at Abilene Christian University, currently 
majoring in political science and sociology. He is a dedicated and 
diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.

for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
