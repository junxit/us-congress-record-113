# TRIBUTE TO ARI TEPLER

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ARI TEPLER

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Ari Tepler, a 
2014 summer intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Ari is a freshman at Yeshiva University, currently majoring in 
political science. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who has been 
devoted to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ari for 
all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
