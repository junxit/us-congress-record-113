# TRIBUTE TO WONGANI MTIKA

```
                        TRIBUTE TO WONGANI MTIKA

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Wongani Mtika, a 
2014 summer intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard 
work she has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.

internship experience.

for all the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in
```
