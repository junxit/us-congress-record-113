# TRIBUTE TO JOHN BURKE

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JOHN BURKE

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize John Burke, a 
2014 spring intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  John is a graduate of the George Washington University where he 
majored in international affairs. He is a dedicated and diligent worker 
who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to him for all the fine 
work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
