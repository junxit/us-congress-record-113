# TRIBUTE TO JONATHAN RIVERAS

```
                      TRIBUTE TO JONATHAN RIVERAS

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Jonathan 
Riveras, a 2014 summer intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of 
the hard work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State

who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.

for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
