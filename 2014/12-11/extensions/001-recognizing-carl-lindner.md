# RECOGNIZING CARL LINDNER

```
                        RECOGNIZING CARL LINDNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN A. BOEHNER

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2014

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a very special person 
to our local area, Carl Lindner, as the city of Cincinnati recently 
moved to adorn one of its downtown streets with Mr. Lindner's name.
  Mr. Lindner spent a lifetime making the Queen City the best place on 
earth to live and raise a family. I cannot think of a better place to 
honor him than on the streets of the city he loved and worked so hard 
to make better for us and for our children.
  He was a wonderful man, and, more importantly, a good friend to so 
many of us. It's for these reasons that our community is so very 
grateful, and I ask all Members of the House to join me as we celebrate 
Carl Lindner's legacy.

                          ____________________
```
