# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 5:30 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Vermont.
```
