**Speaking:** Stabenow, Debbie (S000770) [D-MI], Gillibrand, Kirsten E. (G000555) [D-NY], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
 NOMINATION OF COLLEEN BRADLEY BELL TO BE AMBASSADOR EXTRAORDINARY AND 
       PLENIPOTENTIARY OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA TO HUNGARY

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The assistant bill clerk read the nomination of Colleen Bradley Bell, 
of California, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of 
the United States of America to Hungary.

  The assistant bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum call 
be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
