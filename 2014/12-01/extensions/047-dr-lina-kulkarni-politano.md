# DR. LINA KULKARNI POLITANO

```
                       DR. LINA KULKARNI POLITANO

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, December 1, 2014

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Dr. Lina Kulkarni Politano for her inspirational leadership and giving 
back to her community.
  Dr. Politano is board certified and a Diplomat of the American Board 
of Orthodontics, a distinction which less than 30 percent of 
orthodontists attain. With a passion for Orthodontics she began 
building her practice from scratch. Today she serves as a member of the 
American Association of Orthodontists, the Rocky Mountain Society of 
Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Metropolitan 
Denver Dental Society.
  In addition to her work, Dr. Politano is proud to give back to the 
community where she grew up. She is a committed supporter of several 
schools' educational programs. This commitment extends to her alma 
mater D'Evelyn School, where she serves on the D'Evelyn Education 
Foundation's Board of Directors.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to Dr. Lina Kulkarni Politano for 
being honored by the West Chamber serving Jefferson County. I have no 
doubt she will exhibit the same dedication and character in all her 
future accomplishments.

                          ____________________
```
