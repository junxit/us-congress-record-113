# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 28, 2014

**Speaking:** McCarthy, Kevin (M001165) [R-CA]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 28, 2014

  Mr. McCARTHY of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet on Monday, July 28, 
2014, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m.

request of the gentleman from California?
```
