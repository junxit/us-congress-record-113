                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 135

  At the request of Mr. Vitter, the name of the Senator from Oklahoma 
(Mr. Inhofe) was added as a cosponsor of S. 135, a bill to amend title 
X of the Public Health Service Act to prohibit family planning grants 
from being awarded to any entity that performs abortions, and for other 
purposes.
