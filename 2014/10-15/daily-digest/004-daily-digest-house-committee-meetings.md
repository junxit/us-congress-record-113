# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
SERGEANT ANDREW TAHMOORESSI: OUR MARINE IN MEXICAN CUSTODY
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on the Western Hemisphere 
held a hearing on October 1, 2014, entitled ``Sergeant Andrew 
Tahmooressi: Our Marine in Mexican Custody''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
WHITE HOUSE PERIMETER BREACH: NEW CONCERNS ABOUT THE SECRET SERVICE
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Full Committee held a 
hearing on September 30, 2014, entitled ``White House Perimeter Breach: 
New Concerns about the Secret Service''. Testimony was heard from Julia 
Pierson, Director, Secret Service; and public witnesses.
GYM MEMBERSHIPS, GIFT CARDS, AND HAIR SALONS: EXAMINING THE MISUSE OF 
GOVERNMENT-SUPPLIED CREDIT CARDS
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on 
Government Operations held a hearing on October 14, 2014, entitled 
``Gym Memberships, Gift Cards, and Hair Salons: Examining the Misuse of 
Government-Supplied Credit Cards''. Testimony was heard from Anne 
Richards, Assistant Inspector General for Audits, Office of the 
Inspector General, Department of Homeland Security; Janet Kasper, 
Director, Contracts and Assistance Agreement Audits, Office of 
Inspector General, Environmental Protection Agency; Elliot Lewis, 
Assistant Inspector General for Audits, Office of Inspector General, 
Department of Labor; John Lyle, Deputy Assistant Secretary 
(Contracting), U.S. Air Force; and H.L. Larry, Deputy Director of Air 
Force Services, U.S. Air Force.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
