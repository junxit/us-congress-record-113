# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 112

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to the order of 
the House of today, the House stands adjourned until noon on Thursday, 
August 7, 2014, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 112, in which 
case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  There was no objection.
  Accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 6 minutes a.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Thursday, August 7, 2014, at noon, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 112, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.

                          ____________________
```
