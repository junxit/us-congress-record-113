# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 543

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       84. The SPEAKER presented a petition of The City of 
     Pleasantville, NJ, relative to Resolution No. 67-2014 urging 
     the House of Representatives and the President to pass and 
     sign the Homeowners Flood Insurance Affordability Act of 
     2014; to the Committee on Financial Services.
       85. Also, a petition of the City Council of Cincinnati, 
     Ohio, relative to Resolution No. 38-2014 recognizing the 
     importance of a unified Ireland; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Affairs.
       86. Also, a petition of Ontario County Board of 
     Supervisors, New York, relative to Resolution No. 265-2014 
     urging the adoption of H.R. 543; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.
```
