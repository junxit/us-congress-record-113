# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands 
adjourned until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow under the provisions of S. Res. 539, 
as a further mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable James M. 
Jeffords.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 6:53 p.m., adjourned until Wednesday, 
September 10, 2014, at 9:30 a.m.
```
