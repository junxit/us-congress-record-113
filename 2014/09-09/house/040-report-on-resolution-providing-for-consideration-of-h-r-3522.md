# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3522, EMPLOYEE HEALTH CARE PROTECTION ACT OF 2013

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3522, EMPLOYEE 
                   HEALTH CARE PROTECTION ACT OF 2013

  Mr. BURGESS from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-584) on the resolution (H. Res. 717) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3522) to authorize health insurance 
issuers to continue to offer for sale current group health insurance 
coverage in satisfaction of the minimum essential health insurance 
coverage requirement, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
