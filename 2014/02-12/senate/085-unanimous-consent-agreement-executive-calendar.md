# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at a time to be 
determined by me, with the concurrence of Senator McConnell, the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 561; that there 
be 20 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual form; that upon 
the use or yielding back of that time the Senate proceed to vote 
without intervening action or debate on the nomination; that the motion 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order; that 
any related statements be printed in the Record; and that the President 
be immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then 
resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
