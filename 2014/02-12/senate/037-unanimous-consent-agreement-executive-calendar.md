# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

**Measures:** S. 25

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that upon 
disposition of the House message with respect to S. 25, the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Calendar Nos. 497, 498, 493, 494, 495, 496, 531, and 534; that the 
Senate proceed to vote without intervening action or debate on the 
nominations in the order listed; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid on the table, with no intervening action or 
debate; that no further motions be in order; that any related 
statements be printed in the

[[Page S932]]

Record; that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action and the Senate then resume legislative session; further, that 
there be 2 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual manner prior 
to each vote and all votes after the first be 10 minutes in length.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. NELSON. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to speak as in 
morning business for 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
