# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, we will be in a period of morning business until 11 
o'clock this morning. Republicans will control the first half and 
Democrats the final half.
  At 11 a.m. this morning the Senate will proceed to executive session 
to consider Executive Calendar Nos. 525, 595, 527, and 529. These are 
all extremely important nominations. At 11:30 this morning there will 
be up to four rollcall votes on the confirmation of these nominations.
  We also hope to consider the debt limit legislation, military 
retirement pay, and, hopefully, additional nominations today.
```
