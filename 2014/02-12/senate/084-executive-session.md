  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 
597, 598, 601, 602, 603, with the exception of COL Mark A. Baird and 
COL Robert W. Stanley II; and Nos. 604, 605, with the exception of COL 
Andrew E. Salas; No. 606, with the exception of BG Jon K. Kelk; and 
Nos. 607, 608, 609, 610, and 611, and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force and Army; that the nominations be 
confirmed en bloc; the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order to any of the nominations; that any related 
statements be printed in the Record; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
