# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, February 13, through 
Monday, February 24, the majority leader and Senators Warner and Levin 
be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
