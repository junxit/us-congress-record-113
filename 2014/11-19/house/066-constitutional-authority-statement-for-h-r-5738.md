# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5738

**Measures:** H.R. 5738

```
           By Mr. BENTIVOLIO:
       H.R. 5738.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``All Bills for raising Revenue shall originate in the 
     House of Representatives; but the Senate may propose or 
     concur with amendments as on other Bills.''--U.S. 
     Constitution, Article I, section 7, clause 1
       ``No Money shall be drawn from the Treasury, but in 
     Consequence of Appropriations made by Law; and a regular 
     Statement and Account of the Receipts and Expenditures of all 
     public Money shall be published from time to time.''--U.S. 
     Constitution, Article I, section 9, clause 7
```
