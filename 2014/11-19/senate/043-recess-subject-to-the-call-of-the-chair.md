# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 4:46 p.m., recessed subject to the call of 
the Chair and reassembled at 6:31 p.m. when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Blumenthal).
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, what is the business before the body?

                          ____________________
```
