# SIGN THE KEYSTONE PIPELINE PERMIT

**Speaking:** Davis, Rodney (D000619) [R-IL]

```
                   SIGN THE KEYSTONE PIPELINE PERMIT

  (Mr. RODNEY DAVIS of Illinois asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. RODNEY DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I have seen reports that 
the President is going to come to this Chamber tomorrow and talk about 
avoiding dealing with Congress. He is going to pick up his pen and sign 
executive orders without coming to this institution and following our 
Constitution.
  Mr. President, if you are that willing to pick up your pen, then pick 
it up and sign the Keystone pipeline permit so that we can start 
creating jobs in this country and get 40,000 people off the 
unemployment rolls and into good-paying jobs that are careers.

                          ____________________
```
