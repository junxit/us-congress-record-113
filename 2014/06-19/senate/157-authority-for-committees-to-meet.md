# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      Committee on Armed Services

on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on June 19, 2014, at 9:30 a.m.

Senate on June 19, 2014, at 2:30 p.m.

the Dirkse,n Senate Office Building.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

Senate on June 19, 2014, at 11 a.m., to hold a hearing entitled
```
