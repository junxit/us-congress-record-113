# Text of House Amendment 39

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4870

```
H.R. 4870

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 39: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec.__. None of the funds made available by this Act may be 
     used to ``consult'', as that term is used in reference to the 
     Department of Defense and the National Security Agency in 
     section 20(c)(1) of the National Institute of Standards and 
     Technology Act (15 U.S.C. 278g-3(c)(1)), in contravention of 
     the provision therein which mandates:
       ``to assure--
       (A) use of appropriate information security policies, 
     procedures, and techniques, in order to improve information 
     security. . . ''.
```
