# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5091

**Measures:** H.R. 5091

```
           By Mr. YOHO:
        H.R. 5091.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, which grants Congress the Power ``To make 
     Rules for the Government and Regulation of the land and naval 
     Forces.''
```
