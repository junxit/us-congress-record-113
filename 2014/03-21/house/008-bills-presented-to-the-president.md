# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on March 18, 2014, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills:

       H.R. 3370. To delay the implementation of certain 
     provisions of the Biggert-Waters Flood Insurance Reform Act 
     of 2012, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2650. To allow the Fond du Lac Band of Lake Superior 
     Chippewa in the State of Minnesota to lease or transfer 
     certain land.
       H.R. 4076. To address shortages and interruptions in the 
     availability of propane and other home heating fuels in the 
     United States, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
