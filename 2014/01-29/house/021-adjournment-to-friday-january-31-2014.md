# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 31, 2014

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 31, 2014

  Mr. LUCAS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 3 p.m. on Friday, January 31, 
2014; and when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on 
Monday, February 3, 2014, when it shall convene at noon for morning-
hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
