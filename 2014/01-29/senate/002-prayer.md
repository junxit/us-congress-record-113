# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, ultimate judge of the universe, You have been our 
dwelling place in all generations, and we are sustained by Your 
steadfast love. Today, surround our Senators with the shield of Your 
favor as they labor to keep America strong. Lord, teach them to be 
obedient to Your commands, doing Your good will as Your presence fills 
them with joy. Manifest Your power through their labors so that this 
Nation will be exalted by righteousness. Help our Senators to put their 
trust in You and to recapture their trust in one another as Your angels 
guard them in all their ways.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
