# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                    SUBCOMMITTEE ON WATER AND POWER

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the

Klamath Basin Water Recovery and Economic Restoration Act of 2014.
```
