                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

     purposes (Rept. No. 113-176).

     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 974. A bill to provide for certain land conveyances in 
     the State of Nevada, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 113-
     178).
       By Ms. LANDRIEU, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with amendments:
       S. 1300. A bill to amend the Healthy Forests Restoration 
     Act of 2003 to provide for the conduct of stewardship end 
     result contracting projects (Rept. No. 113-179).
       By Ms. LANDRIEU, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 1301. A bill to provide for the restoration of forest
