# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  In these waning days of deliberation before Members leave to focus on 
the upcoming election, bless them with focus on the pressing matters of 
these days. May they be filled with wisdom and a spirit of goodwill and 
cooperation that good solutions to unfinished business might be arrived 
at together.
  Finally, bless our world with peace and all those seeking an end to 
violence.
  May all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
