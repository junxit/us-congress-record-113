# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, January 30, through 
Monday, February 3, the majority leader and Senators Warner and 
Rockefeller be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint 
resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
