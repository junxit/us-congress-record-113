# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  King of glory and peace, Your love sustains us. Each day You give us 
Your peace and joy, providing rest to the weary and renewing the 
strength of those exhausted by life's trials.
  In Your compassion lead our lawmakers to Your desired destination. 
When they cry to You for help, be their strength and shield. Lord, You 
are peace, joy, gladness, gentleness, beauty, and truth. Be our 
protector, guardian, and defender from this time forth even forever 
more.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
