# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1691

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


        Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs be authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on June 9, 2014, at 3:30 p.m. to 
conduct a hearing entitled ``Border Security: Examining the 
Implications of S. 1691, The Border Patrol Pay Reform Act of 2013.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
