# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Gingrey, Phil (G000550) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                   Offered By: Mr. Gingrey of Georgia

       Amendment No. 29: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to pay a Federal employee for any period of time 
     during which such employee is using official time under 
     section 7131 of title 5, United States Code.
```
