# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to authorize, approve, implement, or assist in any 
     way a toll on any segment of Interstate 4 in the State of 
     Florida.
```
