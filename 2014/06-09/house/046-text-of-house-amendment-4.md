# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                         Offered By: Ms. Waters

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. 4. __. None of the funds made available by this Act 
     may be used to require the relocation, or to carry out any 
     required relocation, of any asset management positions of the 
     Office of Multifamily Housing of the Department of Housing 
     and Urban Development in existence as of the date of the 
     enactment of this Act.
```
