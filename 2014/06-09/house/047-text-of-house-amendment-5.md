# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Royce, Edward R. (R000487) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                         Offered By: Mr. Royce

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used for the Housing Trust Fund established under section 
     1338 of the Federal Housing Enterprises Financial Safety and 
     Soundness Act of 1992 (12 U.S.C. 4568).
```
