# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4745

```
H.R. 4745

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to provide a per-passenger subsidy in excess of $250 
     under the Essential Air Service program.
```
