# CONGRATULATING HEATHER SHAKE

```
                      CONGRATULATING HEATHER SHAKE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 20, 2014

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Heather Shake 
for becoming the first woman golfer at the University of Houston-
Victoria to earn a conference title. As a freshman, Heather won the 
Association of Independent Institutions Conference Women's Golf 
Championship where she posted the low rounds of the day, 75-74 for a 7-
over 149.
  With this win, the former Dawson High School student earned an 
automatic bid to the National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics 
(NAIA) Women's Golf Championship in May. As only a freshman, I'm 
excited to see Heather's next accomplishments in both her educational 
and athletic endeavors.
  I wish Heather the best of luck at the NAIA Women's Golf 
Championship. On behalf of all residents of the Twenty-Second 
Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate Heather Shake on 
earning the University of Houston-Victoria's first conference title.

                          ____________________
```
