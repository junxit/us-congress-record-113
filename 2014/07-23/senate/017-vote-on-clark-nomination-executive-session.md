# Vote on Clark Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
                        Vote on Clark Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There will now be 2 minutes of debate prior to 
the vote on the Clark nomination.
  Who yields time?
  The Senator from Delaware.
  Mrs. SHAHEEN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to yield back 
all time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Julia Akins Clark to be General Counsel of the Federal Labor 
Relations Authority?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
