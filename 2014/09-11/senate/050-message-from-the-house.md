# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2678, H.R. 4751

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:30 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2678. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 10360 Southwest 186th Street 
     in Miami, Florida, as the ``Larcenia J. Bullard Post Office 
     Building''.
       H.R. 4751. An act to make technical corrections to Public 
     Law 110-229 to reflect the renaming of the Bainbridge Island 
     Japanese American Exclusion Memorial, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
