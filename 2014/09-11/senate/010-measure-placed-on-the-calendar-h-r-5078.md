# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 5078

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 5078

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 5078

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, H.R. 5078 is due for its second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title for a second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5078) to preserve existing rights and 
     responsibilities with respect to waters of the United States, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. REID. I would object to any further proceedings at this time on 
this matter.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The objection is heard. The bill 
will be placed on the calendar.
```
