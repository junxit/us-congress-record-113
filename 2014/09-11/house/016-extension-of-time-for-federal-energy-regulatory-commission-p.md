# EXTENSION OF TIME FOR FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION PROJECT INVOLVING AMERICAN FALLS RESERVOIR

**Measures:** S. 276

```
  EXTENSION OF TIME FOR FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION PROJECT 
                   INVOLVING AMERICAN FALLS RESERVOIR

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (S. 276) to reinstate and 
extend the deadline for commencement of construction of a hydroelectric 
project involving the American Falls Reservoir.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Kentucky (Mr. Whitfield) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
