# EPS SERVICE PARTS ACT OF 2014

```
                     EPS SERVICE PARTS ACT OF 2014

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Rice of South Carolina). The unfinished 
business is the question on suspending the rules and passing the bill 
(H.R. 5057) to amend the Energy Policy and Conservation Act to permit 
exemptions for external power supplies from certain efficiency 
standards, and for other purposes, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Kentucky (Mr. Whitfield) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
