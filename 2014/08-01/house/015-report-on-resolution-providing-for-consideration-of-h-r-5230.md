# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5230, SECURE THE SOUTHWEST BORDER ACT OF 2014; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5272, PROHIBITIONS RELATING TO DEFERRED ACTION FOR ALIENS; AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5230, SECURE 
 THE SOUTHWEST BORDER ACT OF 2014; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 
    5272, PROHIBITIONS RELATING TO DEFERRED ACTION FOR ALIENS; AND 
      PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-571) on the resolution (H. Res. 710) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5230) making supplemental 
appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2014, and for 
other purposes; providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5272) to 
prohibit certain actions with respect to deferred action for aliens not 
lawfully present in the United States, and for other purposes; and 
providing for consideration of motions to suspend the rules, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
