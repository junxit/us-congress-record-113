# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Friday, August 1, through 
Monday, September 8, the majority leader and Senators Rockefeller, 
Reed, Levin, Coons, and Cardin be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
