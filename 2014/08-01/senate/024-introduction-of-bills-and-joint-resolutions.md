# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2774, S. 2775, S. 2776

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SCHUMER:
       S. 2774. A bill to require all recreational vessels to have 
     and to post passenger capacity limits and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. BROWN (for himself and Mr. Portman):
       S. 2775. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to exempt aircraft management services from the ticket tax; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. WALSH:
       S. 2776. A bill to establish a Carbon Capture and 
     Sequestration Deployment Acceleration Fund to promote the 
     establishment of not fewer than 10 commercial-scale carbon 
     capture and sequestration units in the United States during 
     the next 10 years, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Natural Resources.

                          ____________________
```
