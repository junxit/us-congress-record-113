# CHAPLAIN BARRY BLACK

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                          CHAPLAIN BARRY BLACK

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, may I say, while our good chaplain is still 
in the Chamber, he has such a way with words. I am so impressed with 
the prayer this morning, where he recognized the way to get things 
done--as little as it is. If we could do more, we would get more, with 
this good staff. So I really appreciate the prayer and the Chaplain.
  I extend my appreciation for the hard work of this staff. Everyone in 
this room works hard, whether they are police officers or enrolling 
clerks. They do everything. I am really grateful for what they do to 
help us all look better. I wish we did more so we would look better, 
but that is the way it is.

                          ____________________
```
