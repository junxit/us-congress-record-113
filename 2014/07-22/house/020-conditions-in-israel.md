# CONDITIONS IN ISRAEL

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

```
                          CONDITIONS IN ISRAEL

  (Mr. GOHMERT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GOHMERT. Mr. Speaker, in Israel right now, there is a battle for 
peace. They are being embattled by a group who teach their children, in 
the educational materials we help pay for, to hate Jews, to hate 
Israelis. They teach the people to hate Israelis as well. They name 
streets and holidays after people who kill innocent people.
  It is time to cut off every dime of American money going to anyone 
who has any kind of relationship with Hamas or those killing in the 
Middle East, and especially in Israel.
  It is time to bomb Iran's nuclear capabilities. It is time for the 
United States, if we are not going to stop Iran's nukes, then let 
Israel do it. A friend will not put another friend in this kind of 
jeopardy.

                          ____________________
```
