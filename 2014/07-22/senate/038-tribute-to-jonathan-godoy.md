# TRIBUTE TO JONATHAN GODOY

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JONATHAN GODOY

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Jonathan Godoy, 
a 2013 summer intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.

dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jonathan 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
