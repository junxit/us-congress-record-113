# SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                         SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Friday, April 11 through 
Monday, April 28, Senators Levin, Rockefeller, Reed, and Gillibrand be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
