# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Father, be with us not only in great moments of experience but 
also during mundane and common tasks of life. Through the power of Your 
Spirit, may our Senators run without weariness and walk without 
fainting. Give them the wisdom to be patient with others, prompt to 
appreciate the virtues of their colleagues. Rule in their hearts, 
keeping them from sin and sustaining their loved ones. Lord, surround 
our Senators with the shield of Your favor, accomplishing in their 
lives more than they can ask or imagine. We pray in Your sovereign 
Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
