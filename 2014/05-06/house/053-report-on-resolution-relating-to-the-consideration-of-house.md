```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION RELATING TO THE CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE REPORT 113-
415 AND AN ACCOMPANYING RESOLUTION, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF 
  H. Res. 565, APPOINTMENT OF SPECIAL COUNSEL TO INVESTIGATE INTERNAL 
                            REVENUE SERVICE

groups by the Internal Revenue Service, which was referred to the House
```
