# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JASON CHAFFETZ

                                of utah

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 11, 2014

  Mr. CHAFFETZ. Mr. Speaker, I attended the funeral of a slain deputy 
sheriff in Utah on Feb. 2, 2013 and missed rollcall votes on H.R. 3590, 
The Sportsmen's Heritage and Recreational Enhancement (SHARE) Act of 
2013, as well as votes on amendments to that bill.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``no'' on all of the 
amendments. I also would have voted ``no'' on the Motion to Recommit 
with Instructions.
  Most importantly, I would have voted ``yes'' on final passage of the 
bill.
```
