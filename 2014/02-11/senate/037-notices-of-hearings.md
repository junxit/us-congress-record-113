# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                 Committee on Rules and Administration

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet at 10:30 a.m., on February 12, 2014, 
to conduct a business meeting to consider the nominations of Thomas 
Hicks and Myrna Perez to be members of the Election Assistance 
Commission.


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources will meet on February 13, 2014, at 10 
a.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building. The purpose 
of the Business Meeting is to consider the following nominations.
  Rhea S. Suh, to be the Assistant Secretary for Fish and Wildlife and 
Parks; and Janice M. Schneider, to be an Assistance Secretary of the 
Interior, Land and Minerals Management.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record should send it to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, Washington, DC 
20510-6150, or by email to <span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="5122303c0e373e263d342311343f342336287f22343f3025347f363e27">[email protected]</span>.
  For further information, please contact Sam Fowler at (202) 224-7571.
```
