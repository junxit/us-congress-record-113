# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4600

**Measures:** H.R. 4600

```
            By Mr. KING of Iowa:
        H.R. 4600.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The bill is enacted pursuant to Congress' powers to lay and 
     collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts, and Excises under Article I, 
     Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
```
