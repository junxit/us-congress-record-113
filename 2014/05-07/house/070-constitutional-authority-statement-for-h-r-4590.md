# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4590

**Measures:** H.R. 4590

```
           By Mr. LABRADOR:
       H.R. 4590.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This legislation has been written pursuant to Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 3, which gives Congress the authority ``To 
     Regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several 
     States, and with the Indian Tribes;''
```
