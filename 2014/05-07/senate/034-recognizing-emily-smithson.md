# RECOGNIZING EMILY SMITHSON

```
                       RECOGNIZING EMILY SMITHSON

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Emily Smithson for her hard 
work as an intern in my Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts 
and contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Emily is from San Marcos, CA. She is a recent graduate of Brigham 
Young University-Hawaii, where she earned a degree in political science 
and history. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made 
her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of her work is 
reflected in her great efforts during her time in my office.
  I thank Emily for the dedication she has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. I know 
she will have continued success with all of her future endeavors. I 
wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
