# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Miller, Jeff (M001144) [R-FL]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. MILLER of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. tomorrow; and 
when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on Tuesday, 
July 22, 2014, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate 
and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
