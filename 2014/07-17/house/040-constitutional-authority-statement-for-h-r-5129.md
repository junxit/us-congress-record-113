# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5129

**Measures:** H.R. 5129

```
            By Mr. SMITH of Nebraska:
        H.R. 5129.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 of the Constitution provides 
     that Congress shall have power ``To establish a uniform Rule 
     of Naturalization.''
```
