# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O divine Master, incline the hearts of our lawmakers to follow in 
Your way. May they seek to stay within the circle of Your providential 
plan for their lives, striving to please You as they live for Your 
glory. Lord, deliver them from crooked thoughts, careless words, and 
selfish hearts. Forgive them for the things undone that ought to have 
been done and the things done that ought not to have been done. Spirit 
of purity and grace, guide our Senators with Your power.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
