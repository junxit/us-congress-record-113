# TRIBUTE TO JENNESSA SCHOLL

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JENNESSA SCHOLL

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jennessa Scholl, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Jennessa is a graduate of Spearfish High School in Spearfish, SD. 
Currently, she is also a graduate of Black Hills State University, 
where she majored in mass communications. She is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of her experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jennessa for all of 
the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
