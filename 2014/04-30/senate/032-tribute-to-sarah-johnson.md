# TRIBUTE TO SARAH JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO SARAH JOHNSON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Sarah Johnson, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done 
for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Sarah is a graduate of Custer High School in Custer, SD. Currently, 
she is attending South Dakota School of Mines and Technology, where she 
is majoring in mining engineering. She is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of her experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Sarah for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
