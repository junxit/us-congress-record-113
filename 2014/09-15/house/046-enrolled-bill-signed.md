# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 120

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
a joint resolution of the House of the following title, which was 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.J. Res. 120. Joint resolution approving the location of a 
     memorial to commemorate the more than 5,000 slaves and free 
     Black persons who fought for independence in the American 
     Revolution.
```
