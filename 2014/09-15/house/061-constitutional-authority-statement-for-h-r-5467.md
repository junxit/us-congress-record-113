# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5467

**Measures:** H.R. 5467

```
           By Ms. FRANKEL of Florida:
        H.R. 5467.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 (Clauses 1, 3, 7, and 18) of the 
     United States Constitution, which grants Congress the power 
     to lay and collect taxes for the purpose of spending; to 
     regulate commerce between the several states; and to 
     establish post offices and post roads.
```
