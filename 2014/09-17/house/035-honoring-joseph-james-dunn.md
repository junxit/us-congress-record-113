# HONORING JOSEPH JAMES DUNN

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                       HONORING JOSEPH JAMES DUNN

  (Mr. DAINES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAINES. Madam Speaker, Montana is a State blessed with people who 
have a strong commitment to service. In fact, more than 100,000 
military veterans call our State home, and our dedicated law 
enforcement officials perform critical services each and every day.
  On August 14, 2014, we lost a great Montanan when Cascade County 
sheriff deputy and Marine Corps combat veteran Joseph James Dunn the 
First was killed in the line of duty.
  Joe put faith and family at the center of his life, going on multiple 
mission trips. He was described as a ``bold ambassador for Christ.'' No 
one was more important to Joe than his family: his dear wife, Robynn; 
son, Joey; and daughter, Shiloh.
  Joe will be dearly missed by all who knew him. He personified service 
to his country, to his State, and to his community. Joe's life is an 
inspiration for all Montanans.

                          ____________________
```
