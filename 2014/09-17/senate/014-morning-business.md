# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 12 p.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each and with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Delaware.

                          ____________________
```
