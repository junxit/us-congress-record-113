**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 
1009 through and including 1026 and all nominations placed on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, and Navy; that the nominations 
be confirmed en bloc; the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order to any of the nominations; that the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate 
then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:
