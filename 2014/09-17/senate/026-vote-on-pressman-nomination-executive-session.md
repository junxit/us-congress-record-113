# Vote on Pressman Nomination (Executive Session)

```
                      Vote on Pressman Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination of David Pressman, 
of New York, to be an Alternate Representative of the United States of 
America to the Sessions of the General Assembly of the United Nations, 
during his tenure of service as Alternate Representative of the United 
States of America for Special Political Affairs in the United Nations?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
