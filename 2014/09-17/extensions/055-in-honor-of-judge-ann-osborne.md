# IN HONOR OF JUDGE ANN OSBORNE

```
                     IN HONOR OF JUDGE ANN OSBORNE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PATRICK MEEHAN

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 17, 2014

  Mr. MEEHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Judge Ann Osborne 
of the Delaware County Court of Common Pleas in Pennsylvania. She is 
retiring after many years of serving her community.
  Judge Osborne has been a beacon for justice on the bench in Delaware 
County for the last 16 years. Before the bench, Judge

[[Page 15024]]

Osborne served as Sheriff of Delaware County from 1991 to 1998. 
Additionally, she taught comparative government, international law and 
criminal law at Temple University, Villanova University and Immaculata 
College. She left her mark on the Delaware County community and her 
contribution will not be forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Judge Ann Osborne for her long and honorable 
service. I applaud her successes and wish her the best of luck in her 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
