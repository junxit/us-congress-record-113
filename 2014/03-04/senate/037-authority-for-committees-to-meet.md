# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                    Select Committee on Intelligence

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Select Committee on Intelligence be authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on March 4, 2014, at 2:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                 Subcommittee on East Asia and Pacific

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session 
of the Senate on March 4, 2014, at 3 p.m., to hold an East Asia and 
Pacific subcommittee hearing entitled, ``Strengthening U.S. Alliances 
in Northeast Asia.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
