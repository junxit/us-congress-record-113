          COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS OF THE HOUSE

Representatives:

                                         House of Representatives,


                               Office of the Sergeant at Arms,

                                    Washington, DC, March 2, 2014.

     Speaker, House of Representatives,

     weather.
