# SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE DONALD W. NORCROSS, OF NEW JERSEY, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

```
 SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE DONALD W. NORCROSS, OF NEW JERSEY, AS A 
                          MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the gentleman 
from New Jersey, the Honorable Donald W. Norcross, be permitted to take 
the oath of office today.
  His certificate of election has not arrived, but there is no contest 
and no question has been raised with regard to his election.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
