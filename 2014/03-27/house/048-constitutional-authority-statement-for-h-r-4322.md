# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4322

**Measures:** H.R. 4322

```
            By Mr. McKINLEY:
        H.R. 4322.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       According to Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution: The 
     Congress shall have power to enact this legislation to lay 
     and collect duties and to regulate Commerce with foreign 
     nations.
```
