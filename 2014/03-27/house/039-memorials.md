# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 300, H.Res. 305

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       178. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Michigan, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 305 supporting a complete hydrologic 
     separation of the Great Lakes and Mississippi River Basins; 
     to the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
       179. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 300 
     memorializing the Congress and the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs to take a stronger role in investigating and 
     eliminating delays in veterans' health care; to the Committee 
     on Veterans' Affairs.
```
