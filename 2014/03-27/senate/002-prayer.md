# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, we would rest in You, who alone can bring order to our world. 
Reveal yourself to our Senators, guiding them on the path of peace. May 
they place behind them disappointed hopes as they lean on You for 
comfort and strength. Lord, rebuke their doubts, strengthen the good in 
them so that nothing may hinder the outflow of Your power in their 
lives. Direct them to make a commitment to work together for Your 
glory.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.
```
