# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2014

**Speaking:** McCarthy, Kevin (M001165) [R-CA]

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2014

  Mr. McCARTHY of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet on Monday, November 
17, 2014, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 
p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
