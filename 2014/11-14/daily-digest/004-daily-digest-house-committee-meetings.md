# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
THE FUTURE OF ENERGY IN AFRICA
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on Africa, Global Health, 
Global Human Rights, and International Organizations held a hearing 
entitled ``The Future of Energy in Africa''. Testimony was heard from 
Robert F. Ichord, Deputy Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Energy 
Resources, Department of State;

[[Page D1023]]

Eric G. Postel, Assistant to the Administrator, Bureau for Africa, U.S. 
Agency for International Development; Jonathan Elkind, Acting 
Assistant, Secretary, Office of International Affairs, Department of 
Energy; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
UKRAINE'S PARLIAMENTARY POLL
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission received a 
briefing on Ukraine's pivotal parliamentary poll, focusing on the 
conduct and results of the elections, as well as the potential for the 
newly elected parliament to confront the coming challenge of forging a 
democratic, secure, independent future for their strategically 
important country from Olha Aivazovska, Ukrainian Citizen Network 
OPORA, Kyiv, Ukraine; and Katie Fox, National Democratic Institute, 
Stephen Nix, International Republican Institute, and Gavin Weise, 
International Foundation for Electoral Systems, all of Washington, D.C.
```
