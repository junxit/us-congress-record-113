# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1963

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1963

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1963

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1963 is at the desk and 
due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by title for 
the second time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1963) to repeal Section 403 of the Bipartisan 
     Budget Act of 2013.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, could I ask who the sponsors of this 
legislation are? Who is sponsoring it?
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Senators Pryor, Hagan, Shaheen, and 
Begich.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to any further proceedings with 
respect to this bill.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the bill will 
be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
