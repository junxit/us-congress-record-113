# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  Under the previous order, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until 5 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up 
to 10 minutes each.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SESSIONS. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Hirono). Without objection, it is so 
ordered..
```
