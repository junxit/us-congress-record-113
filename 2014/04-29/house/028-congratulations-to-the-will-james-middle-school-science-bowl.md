# CONGRATULATIONS TO THE WILL JAMES MIDDLE SCHOOL SCIENCE BOWL TEAM

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
   CONGRATULATIONS TO THE WILL JAMES MIDDLE SCHOOL SCIENCE BOWL TEAM

  (Mr. DAINES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAINES. Mr. Speaker, today I am very proud to congratulate 
students from Will James Middle School in Billings, Montana, the town 
where Mom and Dad grew up and my grandma still lives today, for winning 
the best car design at the National Science Bowl Middle School Electric 
Car Competition.
  This weekend, five young Montana students traveled to the National 
Science Bowl in Washington, D.C., to compete against 47 other teams 
from around the Nation. I speak for all Montanans when I say that we 
are incredibly proud of their success.
  Under the guidance of science teacher Patrick Kenney, this team of 
five middle school students gained hands-on science and engineering 
experience in designing, building, and racing their model car.
  As Montana's Representative and a chemical engineer from Montana 
State University, I am incredibly proud Montana students like Madi, 
Sam, Tyler, Julianne, and Alex are leading the way in science and 
technology.
  Congratulations again to the Will James Middle School Science Bowl 
team.

                          ____________________
```
