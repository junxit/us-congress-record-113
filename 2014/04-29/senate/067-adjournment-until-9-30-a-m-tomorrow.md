# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Ms. HIRONO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate adjourn

30, 2014, at 9:30 a.m.
```
