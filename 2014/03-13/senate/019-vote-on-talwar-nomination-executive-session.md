# Vote on Talwar Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Burr, Richard (B001135) [R-NC]

```
                       Vote on Talwar Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will be 2 
minutes of debate equally divided in the usual form prior to a vote on 
the Talwar nomination.
  Who yields time? The Senator from North Carolina.
  Mr. BURR. I yield back the remaining time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, all time is yielded back.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Puneet Talwar, of the District of Columbia, to be an Assistant 
Secretary of State?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
