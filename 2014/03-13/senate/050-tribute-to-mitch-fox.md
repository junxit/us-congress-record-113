# TRIBUTE TO MITCH FOX

**Speaking:** Heller, Dean (H001041) [R-NV]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO MITCH FOX

● Mr. HELLER. Madam President, today I wish to honor Nevadan 
Mitch Fox for his dedication to journalistic fairness and quality of 
character.
  With almost 39 years of experience working for Las Vegas PBS, Mitch 
has come to be recognized as a journalist of integrity. Facilitating 
debate over a multitude of topics, Mitch has shown nothing but respect 
to his guests, always appreciating and inviting differences of opinion. 
Whether moderating a debate or a roundtable interview, the respect that 
Mitch commands encourages quality conversation and civil dialogue.
  Mitch's legacy of nonpartisan journalism has made him a go-to source 
for news coverage. He serves as a shining example within his 
profession.
  I will remember Mitch's welcoming and professional demeanor fondly, 
and I wish him luck on the next phase of his already distinguished 
career.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring this respectable Nevada 
journalist.●

                          ____________________
```
