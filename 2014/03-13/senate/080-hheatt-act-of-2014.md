# HHEATT ACT OF 2014

```
                           HHEATT ACT OF 2014

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to H.R. 4076, which was received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4076) to address shortages and interruptions 
     in the availability of propane and other home heating fuels 
     in the United States, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be read a third time 
and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and that 
any statements relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 4076) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time and passed.
```
