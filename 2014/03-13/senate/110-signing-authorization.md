# SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                         SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, March 13 through 
Monday, March 24, Senators King, Reed, Rockefeller, and Casey be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
