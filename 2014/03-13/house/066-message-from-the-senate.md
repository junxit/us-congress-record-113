# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1086, S. 2137, H.R. 3370

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3370. An act to delay the implementation of certain 
     provisions of the Biggert-Waters Flood Insurance Reform Act 
     of 2012, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed bills of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:


[[Page H2411]]


       S. 1086. An act to reauthorize and improve the Child Care 
     and Development Block Grant Act of 1990, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 2137. An act to ensure that holders of flood insurance 
     policies under the National Flood Insurance Program do not 
     receive premium refunds for coverage of second homes.

                          ____________________
```
