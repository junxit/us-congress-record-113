# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on May 22, 2014, she 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill:

       H.R. 1209. To award a Congressional Gold Medal to the World 
     War II members of the ``Doolittle Tokyo Raiders'', for 
     outstanding heroism, valor, skill, and service to the United 
     States in conducting the bombings of Tokyo.
```
