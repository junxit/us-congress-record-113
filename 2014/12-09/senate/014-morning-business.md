# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business, for debate only, until 10:30 a.m., 
with the time equally divided in the usual form.
  The Senator from Vermont.
  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I am looking at the clock, and I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate be able to continue in morning 
business for up to 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
