# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2990, S. 2991, S. 2992

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. CANTWELL (for herself, Ms. Collins, and Mrs. 
             Shaheen):
       S. 2990. A bill to establish a State Trade and Export 
     Promotion Grant Program; to the Committee on Small Business 
     and Entrepreneurship.
           By Mr. BEGICH:
       S. 2991. A bill to amend the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery 
     Conservation and Management Act to promote sustainable 
     conservation and management for the Nation's fisheries and 
     the communities that rely on them, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND:
       S. 2992. A bill to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
     reform procedures for determinations to proceed to trial by 
     court-martial for certain offenses under the Uniform Code of 
     Military Justice, and for other purposes; read the first 
     time.

                          ____________________
```
