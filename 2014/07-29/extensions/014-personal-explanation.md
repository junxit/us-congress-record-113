                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I submit the following 
remarks regarding my absence from votes which occurred on July 28,

flight from Columbia had not been delayed.
  Roll Number 455--H.R. 935--To amend the Federal Insecticide, 
Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act and the Federal Water Pollution Control 
Act to clarify Congressional intent regarding the regulation of the use 
of pesticides in or near navigable waters, and for other purposes--
``aye.''
  Roll 456--H.R. 3202--Essential Transportation Worker Identification 
Credential Assessment Act--``aye.''
  Roll 457--H.R. 3107--Homeland Security Cybersecurity Boots-on-the-
Ground Act--``aye.''
