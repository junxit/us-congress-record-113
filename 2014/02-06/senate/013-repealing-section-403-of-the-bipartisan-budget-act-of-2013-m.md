           Jeanne Shaheen, Jack Reed, Brian Schatz, Christopher A.

           Durbin, Tim Kaine, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Jeff Merkley, 
           Debbie Stabenow, Barbara Boxer, Kirsten E. Gillibrand
