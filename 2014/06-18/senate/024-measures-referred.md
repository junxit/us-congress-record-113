                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 43. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 14 Red River Avenue North in 
     Cold Spring, Minnesota, as the ``Officer Tommy Decker 
     Memorial Post Office''; to the Committee on Homeland Security 
     and Governmental Affairs.

     London, Ohio, as the ``London Fallen Veterans Memorial Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.

     States Postal Service located at 1 Walter Hammond Place in 
     Waldwick,

     Dublin, California, as the ``James `Jim' Kohnen Post

       H.R. 3998. An act to authorize the Administrator of General

       H.R. 4199. An act to name the Department of Veterans
