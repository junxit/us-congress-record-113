# NOMINATION OF HENRY J. AARON TO BE A MEMBER OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  NOMINATION OF HENRY J. AARON TO BE A MEMBER OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY 
                             ADVISORY BOARD

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the second nomination.
  The bill clerk read the nomination of Henry J. Aaron, of the District 
of Columbia, to be a Member of the Social Security Advisory Board for a 
term expiring September 30, 2020.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There is now 2 minutes of debate equally 
divided prior to a vote on the Aaron nomination.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that all time be yielded back.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Henry J. Aaron, of the District of Columbia, to be a Member of the 
Social Security Advisory Board for a term expiring September 30, 2020?
  The nomination was confirmed.

                          ____________________
```
