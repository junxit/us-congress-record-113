# NOMINATION OF ALAN L. COHEN TO BE A MEMBER OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
   NOMINATION OF ALAN L. COHEN TO BE A MEMBER OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY 
                             ADVISORY BOARD

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the Cohen nomination.
  The bill clerk read the nomination of Alan L. Cohen, of Virginia, to 
be a Member of the Social Security Advisory Board for a term expiring 
September 30, 2016.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There is now 2 minutes of debate equally 
divided prior to a vote on the Cohen nomination.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that all time be yielded back.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, all time is yielded back.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Alan L. Cohen, of Virginia, to be a Member of the Social Security 
Advisory Board for a term expiring September 30, 2016?
  The nomination was confirmed.

                          ____________________
```
