REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3193, CONSUMER 
 FINANCIAL PROTECTION SAFETY AND SOUNDNESS IMPROVEMENT ACT; PROVIDING

Protection, and for other purposes; providing for proceedings during

other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to
