# COMMEMORATING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MAYO CLINIC

**Speaking:** Begich, Mark (B001265) [D-AK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 339

```
         COMMEMORATING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MAYO CLINIC

  Mr. BEGICH. I ask unanimous consent that the HELP Committee be 
discharged from further consideration of S. Res. 339 and the Senate 
proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page S856]]

  The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 339) commemorating the 150th 
     anniversary of Mayo Clinic.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. BEGICH. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to, 
the preamble be agreed to, and the motions to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 339) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in the Record of 
Monday, January 27, 2014, under ``Submitted Resolutions.''

                          ____________________
```
