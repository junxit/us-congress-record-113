        Kirk, Mr. Wicker, Mr. Corker, and Mr. Graham):
  S.J. Res. 30. A joint resolution to disapprove a rule of the 
Environmental Protection Agency relating to greenhouse gas emissions 
from electric utility generating units; to the Committee on Environment 
and Public Works.

                              S.J. Res. 30

       Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
     United States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress 
     disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental 
     Protection Agency relating to new source performance 
     standards for emissions of carbon dioxide for new affected 
     fossil fuel-fired electric utility generating units 
     (published at 79 Fed. Reg. 1430 (January 8, 2014)), and such 
     rule shall have no force or effect.
