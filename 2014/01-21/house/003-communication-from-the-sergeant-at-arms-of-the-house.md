# COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS OF THE HOUSE

```
          COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Sergeant at Arms of the House of 
Representatives:

                                   Office of the Sergeant at Arms,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, January 21, 2014.
       Dear Mr. Speaker, As you are aware, the time previously 
     appointed for the next meeting of the House is 1 p.m. on 
     Tuesday, January 21, 2014. This is to notify you, pursuant to 
     clause 12(c) of rule I, of an imminent impairment of the 
     place of reconvening at that time. The impairment is due to 
     the weather.
           Sincerely,


                                               Paul D. Irving,

     Sergeant at Arms.

                          ____________________
```
