# MAYOR CLAY HENRY

```
                            MAYOR CLAY HENRY

  (Mr. GALLEGO asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GALLEGO. Madam Speaker, earlier I was on the floor and I 
mentioned

[[Page 4150]]

Clay Henry as one of the former elected officials in the district that 
I represent. I referred to him as the mayor of Terlingua; and you would 
think having grown up there and lived there all of my life, I would 
have remembered that he was actually at one time the mayor of Lajitas. 
Lajitas is a wonderful resort community in the Big Bend Country, right 
next to Terlingua. Perhaps I had too many visits with Mayor Clay Henry.
  I will tell you that one of the most beautiful parts of the world and 
one that I urge everyone to get familiar with is the Big Bend Country 
of west Texas: the Rio Grande River, the canyons. It is an amazing 
place. I urge everyone to get familiar with that part of the world, as 
there is really nothing like it in the entire United States.

                          ____________________
```
