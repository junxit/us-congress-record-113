# SENATE BILL REFERRED

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

       S. 1917. An act to provide for additional enhancements of 
     the sexual assault prevention and response activities of the 
     Armed Forces; to the Committee on Armed Services; in

     Infrastructure; and to the Committee on the Judiciary for a
```
