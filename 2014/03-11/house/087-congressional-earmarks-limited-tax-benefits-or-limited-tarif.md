# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 4015, H.R. 4138

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                          Offered By Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 4015, ``SGR Repeal and Medicare 
     Provider Payment Modernization Act of 2014,'' do not contain 
     any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI of the 
     Rules of the U.S. House of Representatives.

                        Offered By Mr. Goodlatte

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Judiciary in H.R. 4015 do not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as 
     defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                          Offered By Mr. Upton

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Energy and Commerce in H.R. 4015 do not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
       The amendment to be offered by Representative Conyers, Jr., 
     or a designee, to H.R. 4138, the Enforce Act, does not 
     contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or 
     limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
