    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4138, 
    EXECUTIVE NEEDS TO FAITHFULLY OBSERVE AND RESPECT CONGRESSIONAL 
 ENACTMENTS OF THE LAW ACT OF 2014, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF 
          H.R. 3973, FAITHFUL EXECUTION OF THE LAW ACT OF 2014

report (Rept. No. 113-378) on the resolution (H. Res. 511) providing

powers in the Constitution of the United States by ensuring that the 
President takes care that the laws be faithfully executed, and for 
other purposes, and providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3973) 
to amend section 530D of title 28, United States Code, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
