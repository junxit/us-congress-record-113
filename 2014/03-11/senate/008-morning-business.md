# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 11:30 a.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and the 
time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the majority controlling the first hour and the Republicans controlling 
the second hour.
  The Senator from California is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
