# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Blumenauer, Earl (B000574) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 4923

```
H.R. 4923

                       Offered By: Mr. Blumenauer

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act for 
     Project 99-D-143, Mixed Oxide Fuel Fabrication Facility, may
```
