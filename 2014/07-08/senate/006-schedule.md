# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2363

```
                                SCHEDULE

Republican leader, the Senate will be in a period of morning business

the motion to proceed to S. 2363, the Bipartisan Sportsmen's Act,

weekly caucus meetings.
```
