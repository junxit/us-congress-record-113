# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Be exalted, O God, above the highest Heavens, for we look to You each 
day for our protection and peace. Fulfill Your purposes by using our 
Senators as agents of Your grace. Lord, surround them with Your favor, 
as their labors bring honor to You. Deliver them from the traps set by 
their enemies. Give them hearts filled with confidence in Your 
prevailing providence, sustaining them with Your unfailing faithfulness 
and love.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
