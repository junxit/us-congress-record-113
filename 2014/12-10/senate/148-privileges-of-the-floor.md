```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. UDALL of New Mexico. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
Deepa Ghosh, a foreign affairs fellow in my office, and Kaveh 
Sadeghzadeh, a natural resources fellow, be granted floor privileges 
for the remainder of the Congress.

the 113th Congress: Military Fellow, Chief Master Sergeant Lavor 
Kirkpatrick; Interns Lee Kearns, Eleanor Murphy, Morgan Mena, and Joy 
Demmert.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection.
  Ms. LANDRIEU. I ask unanimous consent that Jonathon Burpee, a 
National Park Service fellow on the staff of the Energy and Natural 
Resources Committee, be granted floor privileges for the duration of 
the 113th Congress.
```
