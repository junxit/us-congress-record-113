# A TRIBUTE TO MARIE E. THOMPSON, AUDREY B. LESESNE, WILLIAM J. ``BILLY'' BARKER AND THE STAFF OF THE SEVENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

```
A TRIBUTE TO MARIE E. THOMPSON, AUDREY B. LESESNE, WILLIAM J. ``BILLY'' 
       BARKER AND THE STAFF OF THE SEVENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE McINTYRE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 10, 2014

  Mr. McINTYRE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to pay tribute to Marie E. 
Thompson, Audrey B. Lesesne, and William J. ``Billy'' Barker, my most 
senior staff, for their steadfast service, longevity of commitment and 
passionate performance in serving the constituents of the Seventh 
Congressional District of North Carolina.
  These individuals have served faithfully with me throughout my entire 
Congressional career, and their exemplary service has truly made a 
difference in the many lives which they have impacted by their 
professional, prompt, and persistent service.
  Marie Thompson's work on constituent services is unparalleled and has 
inspired other Congressional Offices to emulate her distinguished 
service.
  Audrey Lesense's mastery of multiple responsibilities, sharp insight, 
and valuable institutional knowledge have been greatly admired.
  Billy Barker's practical working knowledge and unfailing work ethic 
have been integral to the respect he has earned throughout our district 
by citizens everywhere.
  These three individuals have proudly and graciously given their very 
best in the service of their country and our district, and I am 
grateful for their work and their friendship. Indeed, they--along with 
the many others who have served in our Congressional office over these 
past 18 years--have proven that teamwork and loyalty demonstrate the 
true dedication and determination they possess and the devotion to the 
people that they have given.
  May God bless them and all of our staff, both past and present, for 
their strong, distinguished service to the people with purpose and 
passion--and for which our country, the people of our district, and I 
will forever be grateful.

                          ____________________
```
