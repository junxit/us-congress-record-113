# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy, holy, holy, Lord God almighty, who is and who was and who is to 
come, through Your wisdom all things are governed, and through Your 
grace all things are sustained. Give our Senators the power to serve 
You. As they labor to do Your will, provide them with the wisdom to 
discern Your precepts and obey Your commands. Lord, help them to see 
that to know You is life, to serve You is freedom, and to praise You is 
joy. Let them experience You in the center of their being, finding 
delight in Your presence.
  We pray in Your majestic Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
