# HONORING GWEN MOFFEIT'S DEDICATED SERVICE TO HENDERSON COUNTY

```
     HONORING GWEN MOFFEIT'S DEDICATED SERVICE TO HENDERSON COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JEB HENSARLING

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 9, 2014

  Mr. HENSARLING. Mr. Speaker, Gwen Moffeit has served Henderson County 
for over thirty-five years--twenty-four of them as County Clerk. She 
was elected to the position in 1990 as the first Republican candidate 
to win a county-wide election. During her tenure, she converted the 
process of recording documents from the traditional hardback books to 
the new technology of digital imaging. Gwen has had the pleasure of 
working with many elected officials during her tenure, including five 
County Judges: Winston Reagan, Tommy Smith, Aubrey Jones, David 
Holstein, and Richard Sanders.
  Gwen has been very active in the community and has recently served on 
the I.T. committee, the Henderson County Elections Committee, the Bail 
Bond Board, as well as many other county committees throughout the 
years. She has also been a member of the Athens Kiwanis for over twenty 
years--serving as secretary for almost ten of those years. She is a 
member of Lone Star Republican women, Henderson County Republican 
Women's Club and the Cedar Creek Republican Club.
  Ms. Moffeit has been married to her high school sweetheart 43 years. 
They have two daughters: Misty Wilmeth and Amy Cooper, with four 
grandchildren: grandson Bailey and granddaughters, Allie, Bradie, and 
Gracelyn. Ms. Moffeit and her husband are longtime members of 
Leagueville Baptist Church.

                          ____________________
```
