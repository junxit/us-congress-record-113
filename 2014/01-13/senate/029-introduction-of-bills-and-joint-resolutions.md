       S. 1913. A bill to make permanent the Payments in Lieu of

     Resources.

     Headquarters located at 99 New York Avenue

       S. 1915. A bill to permit health insurance issuers to offer 
     additional plan options to individuals; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
