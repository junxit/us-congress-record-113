# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. UDALL of Colorado (for himself and Mr. Bennet):
       S. 1913. A bill to make permanent the Payments in Lieu of 
     Taxes program; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
           By Mr. BROWN (for himself, Mr. Kirk, and Mr. Durbin):
       S. 1914. A bill to designate the Federal building housing 
     the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives 
     Headquarters located at 99 New York Avenue NE, Washington, 
     DC, as the ``Eliot Ness ATF Building''; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. FLAKE (for himself and Mr. Johnson of 
             Wisconsin):
       S. 1915. A bill to permit health insurance issuers to offer 
     additional plan options to individuals; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
```
