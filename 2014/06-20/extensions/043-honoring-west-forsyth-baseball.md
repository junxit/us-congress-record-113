# HONORING WEST FORSYTH BASEBALL

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
                     HONORING WEST FORSYTH BASEBALL

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. VIRGINIA FOXX

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 19, 2014

  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the West Forsyth 
baseball team, which recently won the North Carolina state 4A baseball 
championship. West Forsyth defeated Richmond in a best of three series. 
This was the Titan's first baseball state championship, and the 
school's first championship in any boys' sport.
  West Forsyth's victory was under the leadership of first year head 
coach Brad Bullard, a West Forsyth graduate and member of the Titan's 
2001 second place team, which was the last team to reach the state 
championship. This year, the Titans amassed a 29 and 6 record and won 
the West Regional Championship to earn their place in the final series.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend these young athletes and the coaches who led 
them on their winning campaign and wish the team continuing success in 
future seasons.
```
