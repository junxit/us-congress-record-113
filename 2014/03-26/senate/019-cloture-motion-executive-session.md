# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I have a cloture motion at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination of 
     John B. Owens, of California, to be United States Circuit 
     Judge for the Ninth Circuit.
         Harry Reid, Patrick J. Leahy, Patty Murray, Bill Nelson, 
           Robert P. Casey, Jr., Jack Reed, Tammy Baldwin, Jon 
           Tester, Tom Udall, Bernard Sanders, Michael F. Bennet, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Elizabeth Warren, Charles E. 
           Schumer, Sheldon Whitehouse, Richard Blumenthal, 
           Richard J. Durbin.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum under rule XXII be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
