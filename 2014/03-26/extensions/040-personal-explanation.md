# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Duckworth, Tammy (D000622) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1813, H.R. 3060

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TAMMY DUCKWORTH

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 26, 2014

  Ms. DUCKWORTH. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, March 24, 2014, I was 
unavoidably detained and missed the following votes:
  H.R. 3060--``To designate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 232 Southwest Johnson Avenue in Burleson, Texas, as 
the ``Sergeant William Moody Post Office Building''.'' Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``yes'' on this bill.
  H.R. 1813--``To redesignate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 162 Northeast Avenue in Tallmadge, Ohio, as the 
``Lance Corporal Daniel Nathan Deyarmin Post Office Building''. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on this bill.

                          ____________________
```
