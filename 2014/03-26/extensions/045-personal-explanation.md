# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Scott, Robert C. "Bobby" (S000185) [D-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2824

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ROBERT C. ``BOBBY'' SCOTT

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 26, 2014

  Mr. SCOTT of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I intended to vote 
``no'', but was recorded as having voted ``yes'', on H.R. 2824 
(rollcall 141), which would overturn Reagan-era protections for streams 
and communities in Appalachia. I would like the record to reflect my 
strong opposition to H.R. 2824.
```
