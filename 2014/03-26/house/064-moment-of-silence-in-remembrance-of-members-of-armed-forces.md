# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF MEMBERS OF ARMED FORCES AND THEIR FAMILIES

```
 MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF MEMBERS OF ARMED FORCES AND THEIR 
                                FAMILIES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Collins of Georgia). The Chair would ask 
all present to rise for the purpose of a moment of silence.
  The Chair asks that the House now observe a moment of silence in 
remembrance of our brave men and women in uniform who have given their 
lives in the service of our country in Iraq and Afghanistan and their 
families, and of all who serve in our Armed Forces and their families.

                          ____________________
```
