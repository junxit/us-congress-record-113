# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Energy and Mineral 
Resources held a hearing entitled ``The Science Behind Discovery: 
Seismic Exploration and the Future of the Atlantic OCS''. Testimony was 
heard from Walter Cruickshank, Deputy Director, Bureau of Ocean Energy 
Management, Department of Interior; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
Employment Situation
Joint Economic Committee: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
employment situation for December 2013, after receiving testimony from 
Erica L. Groshen, Commissioner, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Department 
of Labor.
```
