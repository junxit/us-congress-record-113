# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 667

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on January 9, 2014, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill:

       H.R. 667. To redesignate the Dryden Flight Research Center 
     as the Neil A. Armstrong Flight Research Center and the 
     Western Aeronautical Test Range as the Hugh L. Dryden 
     Aeronautical Test Range.
```
