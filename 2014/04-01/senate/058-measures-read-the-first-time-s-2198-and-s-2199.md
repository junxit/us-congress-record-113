# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2198 AND S. 2199

```
           MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2198 AND S. 2199

  Mr. REID. There are two bills at the desk due for their first 
reading.

[[Page 5294]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bills by title for the 
first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2198) to direct the Secretary of the Interior, 
     the Secretary of Commerce, and the Administrator of 
     Environmental Protection Agency to take action to provide 
     additional water supplies and disaster assistance to the 
     State of California and other Western States due to drought, 
     and for other purposes.
       A bill (S. 2199) to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
     1938 to provide more effective remedies to victims of 
     discrimination in the payment of wages on the basis of sex, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. REID. I ask for a second reading for both bills but object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bills will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
