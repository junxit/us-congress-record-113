# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 12:24 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
pro tempore (Mr. Wolf) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4302. An act to amend the Social Security Act to 
     extend Medicare payments to physicians and other provisions 
     of the Medicare and Medicaid programs, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Leahy).
```
