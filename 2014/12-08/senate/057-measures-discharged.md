# MEASURES DISCHARGED

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 78, H.R. 1707, H.R. 2112, H.R. 2223, H.R. 2678, H.R. 3534, H.R. 4939, H.R. 5030

```
                          MEASURES DISCHARGED

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Homeland Security and 
Governmental Affairs Committee be discharged from further consideration 
of the following measures, which are post office-naming bills, and the 
Senate proceed to their consideration en bloc: H.R. 4939, H.R. 1707, 
H.R. 78, H.R. 2112, H.R. 2223, H.R. 2678, H.R. 3534, and H.R. 5030.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills 
en bloc.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the bills be read a third time 
and passed en bloc and the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table 
en bloc with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
