# ELIZABETH L. KINNUNEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2223

```
               ELIZABETH L. KINNUNEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2223) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 220 Elm Avenue in Munising, Michigan, as the 
``Elizabeth L. Kinnunen Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
