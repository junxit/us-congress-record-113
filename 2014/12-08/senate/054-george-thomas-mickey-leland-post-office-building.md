# GEORGE THOMAS ``MICKEY'' LELAND POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
          GEORGE THOMAS ``MICKEY'' LELAND POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 78) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4110 Almeda Road in Houston, Texas, as the 
``George Thomas `Mickey' Leland Post Office Building,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
