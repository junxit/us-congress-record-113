# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the source of our being, on yesterday, December 7, we 
remembered how You sustain us even through unexpected tragedies. We 
recall the deaths, injuries, heroism, doubts, disbelief, and patriotism 
on that date that will live in infamy.
  Lord, continue to guide this land we love on its labyrinthine path to 
greatness. Protect it from dangers seen and unseen as You unite it for 
the common good. Use our Senators for Your glory as our Nation seeks to 
bring deliverance to captives and to let the oppressed go free.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
