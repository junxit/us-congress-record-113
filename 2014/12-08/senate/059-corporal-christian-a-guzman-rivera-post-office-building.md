# CORPORAL CHRISTIAN A. GUZMAN RIVERA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
        CORPORAL CHRISTIAN A. GUZMAN RIVERA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5030) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 13500 SW 250 Street in Princeton, Florida, as 
the ``Corporal Christian A. Guzman Rivera Post Office Building,'' was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
