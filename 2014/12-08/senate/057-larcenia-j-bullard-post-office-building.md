# LARCENIA J. BULLARD POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                LARCENIA J. BULLARD POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2678) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 10360 Southwest 186th Street in Miami, 
Florida, as the ``Larcenia J. Bullard Post Office Building,'' was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
