# JAMES R. BURGESS JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               JAMES R. BURGESS JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1707) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 302 East Green Street in Champaign, Illinois, 
as the ``James R. Burgess Jr. Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
