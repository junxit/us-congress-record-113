# NATIONAL CLANDESTINE SERVICE OF THE CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY NCS OFFICER GREGG DAVID WENZEL MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
  NATIONAL CLANDESTINE SERVICE OF THE CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY NCS 
            OFFICER GREGG DAVID WENZEL MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2112) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 787 State Route 17M in Monroe, New York, as 
the ``National Clandestine Service of the Central Intelligence Agency 
NCS Officer Gregg David Wenzel Memorial Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
