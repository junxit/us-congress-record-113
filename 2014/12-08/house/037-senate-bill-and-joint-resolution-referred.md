# SENATE BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION REFERRED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 45, S. 1447

```
               SENATE BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  A bill and a joint resolution of the Senate of the following titles 
were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as 
follows:

       S. 1447. An act to make technical corrections to the Navajo 
     water rights settlement in the State of New Mexico, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       S.J. Res. 45. Joint resolution providing for the 
     reappointment of David M. Rubenstein as a citizen regent of 
     the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution; to the 
     Committee on House Administration.

                          ____________________
```
