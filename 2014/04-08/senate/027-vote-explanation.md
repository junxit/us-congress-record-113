# VOTE EXPLANATION

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I regret having missed the April 7, 2014 
vote on passage of H.R. 3979, as amended, the Emergency Unemployment 
Compensation Extension Act of 2014.
  Had I been present, I would have voted for the passage of the 
Emergency Unemployment Compensation Extension Act of 2014 to support 
the 16,000 Louisianians awaiting the extension provided by this 
legislation.
```
