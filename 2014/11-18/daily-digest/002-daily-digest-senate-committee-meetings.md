# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
                  (Committees not listed did not meet)
TAX RELIEF AFTER A DISASTER
Committee on Finance: Subcommittee on Taxation and IRS Oversight 
concluded a hearing to examine tax relief after a disaster, focusing on 
how individuals, small businesses, and communities recover, after 
receiving testimony from Mayor Andy Berke, Chattanooga, Tennessee; 
Robert G. Loughery, Bucks County Commissioners, Doylestown, 
Pennsylvania; Vincent M. Ignizio, New York City Council, New York, New 
York; Sean T. Cronin, St. Vrain and Left Hand Water Conservancy 
District, Longmont, Colorado; Steve Ellis, Taxpayers for Common Sense, 
Washington, DC.; and Troy K. Lewis, The American Institute of Certified 
Public Accountants, Draper, Utah.
NOMINATION
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine the nomination of Earl L. Gay, of the 
District of Columbia, to be Deputy Director of the Office of Personnel 
Management, after the nominee, who was introduced by Representative 
Lewis, testified and answered questions in his own behalf.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
