# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1233, H.R. 4194

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 17, 
2014, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 1233. To amend chapter 22 of title 44, United States 
     Code, popularly known as the Presidential Records Act, to 
     establish procedures for the consideration of claims of 
     constitutionally based privilege against disclosure of 
     Presidential records, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4194. To provide for the elimination or modification 
     of Federal reporting requirements.

                          ____________________
```
