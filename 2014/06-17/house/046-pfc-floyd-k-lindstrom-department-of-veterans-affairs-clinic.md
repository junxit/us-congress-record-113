motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 3375) to designate

Springs, Colorado, as the ``PFC Floyd K. Lindstrom Department of 
Veterans Affairs Clinic,'' on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

gentleman from Colorado (Mr. Lamborn) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
