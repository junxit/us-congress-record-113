# NOTIFICATION OF THE DESIGNATION OF MEREDITH M. BROADBENT AS CHAIR AND DEAN A. PINKERT AS VICE CHAIR OF THE UNITED STATES INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION, EFFECTIVE JUNE 17, 2014--PM 44

```
 NOTIFICATION OF THE DESIGNATION OF MEREDITH M. BROADBENT AS CHAIR AND 
DEAN A. PINKERT AS VICE CHAIR OF THE UNITED STATES INTERNATIONAL TRADE 
               COMMISSION, EFFECTIVE JUNE 17, 2014--PM 44

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with the provisions of 19 U.S.C. 1330(c)(1), this is to 
notify the Congress that I have designated Meredith M. Broadbent as 
Chair and Dean A. Pinkert as Vice Chair of the United States 
International Trade Commission, effective June 17, 2014.
                                                        Barack Obama.  
                                        The White House, June 17, 2014.
```
