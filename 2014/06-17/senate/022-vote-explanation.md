# VOTE EXPLANATION

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. MORAN. Madam President, as a sponsor of the Veterans' Access to 
Care through Choice, Accountability and Transparency Act of 2014, I am 
pleased the Senate has risen to the occasion and come together on this 
critical legislation. Although I was still in Kansas attending to my 
family, I would have joined my colleagues in voting aye on Rollcall 
vote No. 187, a bipartisan plan to help make certain veterans receive 
the access to quality, timely care they deserve. I will continue to 
work to address the challenges and problems at the Department of 
Veterans Affairs so our Nation's heroes have a VA worthy of their 
service.

                          ____________________
```
