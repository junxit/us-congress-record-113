# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 5, H.Res. 340

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       180. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Ohio, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 340 commending Israel for its cordial and 
     mutually beneficial relationship with the United States and 
     Ohio; to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       181. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Arizona, relative to Senate Concurrent Memorial 1006 urging 
     the Congress to provide full, sustainable funding for the 
     PILT program; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       182. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of Ohio, 
     relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 5 urging the Congress 
     to propose a balanced budget amendment to the Constitution; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
