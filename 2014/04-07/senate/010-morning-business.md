# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 5 p.m. with 
Senators therein being permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  Mr. REID. I note the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Ms. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
