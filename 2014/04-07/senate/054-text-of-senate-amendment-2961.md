# Text of Senate Amendment 2961

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

```
  SA 2961. Mr. CRUZ proposed an amendment to the bill S. 2195, to deny 
admission to the United States to any representative to the United 
Nations who has been found to have been engaged in espionage activities 
or a terrorist activity against the United States and poses a threat to 
United States national security interests; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``A bill to deny admission 
     to the United States to any representative to the United 
     Nations who has been found to have been engaged in espionage 
     activities or a terrorist activity against the United States 
     and poses a threat to United States national security 
     interests.''.

```
