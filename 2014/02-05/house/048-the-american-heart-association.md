# THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Beatty, Joyce (B001281) [D-OH]

```
                     THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION

  (Mrs. BEATTY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mrs. BEATTY. Mr. Speaker, I rise this evening in support and honor of 
the American Heart Association. Today, women, Democrats and 
Republicans, stood together in honor of the American Heart Association 
because we understand that cardiovascular disease is the number one 
killer for women.
  The American Heart Association and Stroke Association asks us to wear 
red in support of educating and giving awareness to the American 
people. We asked all citizens this Friday, February 7, to wear red. 
Stand with us as we stand for educating and making our citizens aware 
of this killer disease.

                          ____________________
```
