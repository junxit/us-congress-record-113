# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4216

**Measures:** H.R. 4216

```
            By Mr. CONYERS:
        H.R. 4216.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I. Section 8. The Congress shall have power to lay 
     and collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States;
```
