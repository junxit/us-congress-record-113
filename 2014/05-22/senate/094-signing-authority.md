# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Friday, May 23, through 
Tuesday, June 3, Senators Rockefeller and Reed of Rhode Island be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
