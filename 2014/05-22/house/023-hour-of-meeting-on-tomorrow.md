# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Cantor, Eric (C001046) [R-VA]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. CANTOR. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 3 p.m. tomorrow; when the House 
adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at noon on Tuesday, May 27, 
2014; and when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
noon on Wednesday, May 28, 2014, for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for 
legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
```
