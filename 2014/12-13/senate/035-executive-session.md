```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

NOMINATION OF JORGE LUIS ALONSO TO BE UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE FOR 
                   THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF ILLINOIS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move to proceed to executive session to 
consider Calendar No. 1070.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Jorge Luis 
Alonso, of Illinois, to be United States District Judge for the 
Northern District of Illinois.


                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. REID. There is a cloture motion at the desk, and I ask that it be 
reported.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been presented 
under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination of 
     Jorge Luis Alonso, of Illinois, to be United States District 
     Judge for the Northern District of Illinois.
```
