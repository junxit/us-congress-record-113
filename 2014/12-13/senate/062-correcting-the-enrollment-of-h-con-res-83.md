# CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF H. CON. RES. 83

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 83, H.R. 83, H.Con.Res. 122

```
              CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF H. CON. RES. 83



 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S6814, December 13, 2014, in the first column, the 
following language appears: CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF H. CON. 
RES. 122
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: CORRECTING THE 
ENROLLMENT OF     H.R. 83


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of H. Con. Res. 122, correcting the enrollment of H.R. 
83, providing a new title; that the concurrent resolution be agreed to; 
and the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table 
with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 122) was agreed to.
```
