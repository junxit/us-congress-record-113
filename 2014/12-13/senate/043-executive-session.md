NOMINATION OF JOHN ROBERT BLAKEY TO BE UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE FOR 
                   THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF ILLINOIS

  Mr. REID. I now move to proceed to executive session to consider 
Calendar No. 1075.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The question is on agreeing to the motion.

  The clerk will report the nomination.

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate,

     Judge for the Northern District of Illinois.
