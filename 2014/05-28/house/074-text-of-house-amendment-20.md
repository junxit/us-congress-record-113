# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

```
H.R. 4660

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 20: Sec. __ None of the funds made available 
     by this Act may be used by the Federal Bureau of Prisons to

     government is required to provide a minimum number of inmates

     center.
```
