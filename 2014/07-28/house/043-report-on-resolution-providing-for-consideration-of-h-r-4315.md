# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4315, 21ST CENTURY ENDANGERED SPECIES TRANSPARENCY ACT

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4315, 21ST 
              CENTURY ENDANGERED SPECIES TRANSPARENCY ACT

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 113-563) on the resolution (H. Res. 693) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4315) to amend the 
Endangered Species Act of 1973 to require publication on the Internet 
of the basis for determinations that species are endangered species or 
threatened species, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
