# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, at 2:45 p.m. tomorrow we will have a 
rollcall vote on the confirmation of the McDonald nomination to be the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs, followed by several voice votes to 
confirm the nominations of Andre, Hoza, and Polaschik. We will then 
turn to consideration of the Highway Transportation Funding Act.
  Senators should expect five rollcall votes tomorrow evening in 
relation to the Wyden, Carper-Corker-Boxer, Lee, and Toomey amendments 
and on passage of H.R. 5021, as amended, if amended. Senators will be 
notified when those votes are scheduled.
```
