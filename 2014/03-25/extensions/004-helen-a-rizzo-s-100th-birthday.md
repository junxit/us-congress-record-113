# HELEN A. RIZZO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

```
                    HELEN A. RIZZO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOU BARLETTA

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 25, 2014

  Mr. BARLETTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Helen A. Rizzo of Jim 
Thorpe, Pennsylvania who is turning 100 years old on March 21, 2014.
  Mrs. Rizzo was born in Norristown, Pennsylvania on March 21, 1914. 
She worked as a seamstress and married Daniel G. Rizzo. Mrs. Rizzo has 
two children, Carmen and Marian, as well as five grandchildren and six 
great grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, as she turns 100, I wish Helen Rizzo a happy and healthy 
birthday.

                          ____________________
```
