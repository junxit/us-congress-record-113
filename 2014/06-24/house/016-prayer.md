# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Eternal God, we give You thanks for giving us another day. We pause 
in Your presence and ask guidance for the men and women of the people's 
House.
  On a day when voters in many States participate in congressional 
primaries, may Your spirit of wisdom be manifest among those who 
exercise their rights, rights for which so many struggled 50

[[Page 10754]]

years ago to secure for all American citizens.
  Here in Washington, may all Members realize that Your congregation is 
wider and broader than ever we could measure or determine. Help them, 
and help us, O Lord, to put away any judgments that belong to You and 
do what we can to live together in peace.
  Bless us this day and every day, and may all that is done within the 
people's House be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
