# SENATE RESOLUTION 435--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 435--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
    THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 435

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Andrew B. Willison 
     as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
