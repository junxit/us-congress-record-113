# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 5016

```
H.R. 5016

                       Offered By: Mrs. Blackburn

         Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill (before the 
     short title), insert the following:

[[Page H6298]]

         Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act 
     may be used to provide funds from the Hardest Hit Fund 
     program established by the Secretary of the Treasury under 
     title I of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 
     (12 U.S.C. 5211 et seq.) to any State or local government for 
     the purpose of funding pension obligations of such State or 
     local government.
```
