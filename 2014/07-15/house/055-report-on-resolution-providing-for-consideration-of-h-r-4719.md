# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4719, FIGHTING HUNGER INCENTIVE ACT OF 2014

**Measures:** H.Res. 670, H.R. 4719

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4719, FIGHTING 
                      HUNGER INCENTIVE ACT OF 2014

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 113-522) on the resolution (H. Res. 670) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4719) to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend and expand the 
charitable deduction for contributions of food inventory, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
