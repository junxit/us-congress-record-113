# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. King). The Senator from North Carolina.
  Mrs. HAGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that if cloture is 
invoked on either the Bay or LaFleur nomination the confirmation vote 
or votes occur at 3:15 p.m. with all other provisions of the previous 
order remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the request?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
