# ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

```
                          ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair will exercise its prerogative to 
express its thanks to the floor staff for their work for the Senate and 
for the Nation.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 11:25 p.m., adjourned sine 
die.

                          ____________________
```
