# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God of wisdom and order, who filled the universe with the mysteries 
of Your power; sustain Your lawmakers with the knowledge of Your mercy 
and providence. May they always look to You, the architect of 
destinies, for guidance in the precarious journey of defending freedom. 
Lord, help them to grow in grace and in a knowledge of Your will and 
purposes. Sustain them and their loved ones with Your everlasting arms. 
May Your hand lead our Senators and Your right hand protect them.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
