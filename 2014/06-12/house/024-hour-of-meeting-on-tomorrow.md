# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Frelinghuysen, Rodney P. (F000372) [R-NJ]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. FRELINGHUYSEN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, one, 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. tomorrow; 
and, two, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on 
Tuesday, June 17, 2014, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour 
debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
