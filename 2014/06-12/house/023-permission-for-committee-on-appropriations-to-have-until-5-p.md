# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 5 PM FRIDAY, JUNE 13, 2014, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2015

**Speaking:** Frelinghuysen, Rodney P. (F000372) [R-NJ]

```
 PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 5 PM FRIDAY, 
   JUNE 13, 2014, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2015

  Mr. FRELINGHUYSEN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations have until 5 p.m. on Friday, June 13, 2014, 
to file a privileged report on a bill making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2015, 
and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Yoho). Is there objection to the request 
of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
