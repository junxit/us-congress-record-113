# TRIBUTE TO BRADLEY PUFFENBARGER

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO BRADLEY PUFFENBARGER

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize Bradley 
Puffenbarger, a 2013 intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the 
hard work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Bradley is a graduate of Georgetown University in Washington, DC, 
where he majored in English. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who 
has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Bradley 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
