# TRIBUTE TO JAMES UTHMEIER

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JAMES UTHMEIER

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, today I recognize James Uthmeier, 
a 2013 legal extern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  James is a graduate of the University of Florida in Gainesville, FL. 
Currently, James is in his second-year of law school at Georgetown 
University in Washington, DC. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who 
has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to James 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
