# APOLOGIES TO PRESIDING OFFICER AND STAFF

```
                APOLOGIES TO PRESIDING OFFICER AND STAFF

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, from time to time I have to express my 
apologies to everyone--staff, the Presiding Officer--but I just can't 
come to the floor until we know what we are going to do tomorrow, and 
that takes a lot of time. That is what is going on while I am in my 
office.
  So I apologize to everyone. I am sorry that things take so long, and 
it appears we are doing nothing, but there are things being done.

                          ____________________
```
