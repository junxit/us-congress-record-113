# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4372

**Measures:** H.R. 4372

```
            By Mr. SCHIFF:
        H.R. 4372.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 and the Necessary and Proper Clause. 
     Additionally, the Preamble to the Constitution provides 
     support of the authority to enact legislation to promote the 
     General Welfare.
```
