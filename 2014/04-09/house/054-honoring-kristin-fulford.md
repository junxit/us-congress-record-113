# HONORING KRISTIN FULFORD

**Speaking:** Barrow, John (B001252) [D-GA]

```
                        HONORING KRISTIN FULFORD

  (Mr. BARROW of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BARROW of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Kristin 
Fulford, a member of my 12th District staff for the past 9 years. 
Kristin has been an invaluable part of our team, handling thousands of 
casework issues for folks all over my district.
  Kristin has made sure that folks received their Social Security and 
Medicare payments, helped high school students prepare to enter one of 
our Nation's military institutions, and has done almost everything else 
in between.
  You would be hard-pressed to find anyone in the 12th District who 
doesn't know someone that Kristin has helped. She has been a team 
player who, for nearly a decade, has taken pride in helping the folks 
in our communities.
  All of us in Congress know how hard our staffs work, and we know we 
wouldn't be here if it weren't for people like Kristin, who are our 
eyes, ears, and helping hands when we can't be home.
  I wish Kristin, her husband, Edward, and their daughter, Wren, all 
the best as she begins her new journey, and I know the folks in my 
district will miss her as much as I will.

                          ____________________
```
