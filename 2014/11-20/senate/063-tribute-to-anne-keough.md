# TRIBUTE TO ANNE KEOUGH

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ANNE KEOUGH

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Anne Keough, an 
intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Anne is a graduate of Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, Anne is attending George Washington University, where she is 
majoring in international affairs/security policy and Arabic. Anne is a 
dedicated worker who has been committed to getting the most out of her 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Anne Keough for all of 
the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
