# TRIBUTE TO KELSEY SAKOS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO KELSEY SAKOS

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Kelsey Sakos, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Kelsey is a graduate of Stevens High School in Rapid City, SD. 
Currently, Kelsey is attending Black Hills State University, where she 
is majoring in political science/social science. Kelsey is a dedicated 
worker who has been committed to getting the most out of her 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kelsey Sakos for all 
of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
