# TRIBUTE TO MEGAN REIFFENBERGER

```
                     TRIBUTE TO MEGAN REIFFENBERGER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Megan 
Reiffenberger, an intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the 
hard work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Megan is a graduate of Watertown High School in Watertown, SD. 
Currently, Megan is attending George Mason University, where she is 
majoring in English. Megan is a dedicated worker who has been committed 
to getting the most out of her experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Megan Reiffenberger 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●
```
