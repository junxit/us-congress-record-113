# TRIBUTE TO MARIA ECKRICH

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MARIA ECKRICH

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Maria Eckrich, an 
intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Maria is a graduate of Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, Maria is attending American University's School of 
International Service, where she is studying global governance, 
politics, and security. Maria is a dedicated worker who has been 
committed to getting the most out of her experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Maria Eckrich for all 
of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
