# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, June 5, through 
Monday, June 9, the majority leader be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
