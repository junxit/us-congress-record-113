# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at a time to be 
determined by me, with the concurrence of Senator McConnell, the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nomination: 
Calendar No. 512; there be 20 minutes of debate equally divided in the 
usual form; that upon the use or yielding back of the time the Senate 
proceed to vote, without intervening action or debate on the 
nomination; the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no further 
motions be in order; that any related statements be printed in the 
Record; that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
