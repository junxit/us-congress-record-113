# CORPORAL JUSTIN D. ROSS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1228

```
              CORPORAL JUSTIN D. ROSS POST OFFICE BUILDING

Postal Service located at 123 South 9th Street in De Pere, Wisconsin,
```
