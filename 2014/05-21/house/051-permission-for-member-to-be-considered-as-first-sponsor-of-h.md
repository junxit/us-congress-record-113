# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1189

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 1189

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1189

  Mr. HOLT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1189, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Markey of Massachusetts, for the purposes 
of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hultgren). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
