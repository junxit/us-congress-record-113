# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO SELECT COMMITTEE ON THE EVENTS SURROUNDING THE 2012 TERRORIST ATTACK IN BENGHAZI

**Measures:** H.Res. 567

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO SELECT COMMITTEE ON THE EVENTS SURROUNDING 
                 THE 2012 TERRORIST ATTACK IN BENGHAZI

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to section 2(a) of House Resolution 567, 113th 
Congress, and the order of the House of January 3, 2013, of the 
following Members to the Select Committee on the Events Surrounding the 
2012 Terrorist Attack in Benghazi:
  Mr. Cummings, Maryland;
  Mr. Smith, Washington;
  Mr. Schiff, California;
  Ms. Linda T. Saanchez, California;
  Ms. Duckworth of Illinois
```
