# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 685, H.R. 1209

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 1209. An act to award a Congressional Gold Medal to 
     the World War II members of the ``Doolittle Tokyo Raiders'', 
     for outstanding heroism, valor, skill, and service to the 
     United States in conducting the bombings of Tokyo.
       H.R. 685. An act to award a Congressional Gold Medal to the 
     American Fighter Aces, collectively, in recognition of their 
     heroic military service and defense of our country's freedom 
     throughout the history of aviation warfare.

                          ____________________
```
