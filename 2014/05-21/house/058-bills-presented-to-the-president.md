# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on May 21, 2014, she 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill:

       H.R. 685. To award a Congressional Gold Medal to the 
     American Fighter Aces, collectively, in recognition of their 
     heroic military service and defense of our country's freedom 
     throughout the history of aviation warfare.
```
