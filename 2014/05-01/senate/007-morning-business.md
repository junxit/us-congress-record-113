# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 11:15 a.m. with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, and with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The assistant majority leader.
```
