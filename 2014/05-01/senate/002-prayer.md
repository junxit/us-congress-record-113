# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God who brought light out of darkness causing the morning to 
appear, give to our Senators the vigor needed for today's tasks. Lord, 
protect them from every evil way, empowering them to live with 
integrity. Keep their bodies fit and healthy, their thinking straight, 
and their hearts pure. As they strive to serve You, may they accomplish 
their daily duties with simplicity, uprightness, and faithfulness. Give 
them the grace of faith by which they may lay hold of things unseen.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
