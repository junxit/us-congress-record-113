# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4553

**Measures:** H.R. 4553

```
           By Mr. McKINLEY:
       H.R. 4553.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       According to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the 
     Constitution: the Congress shall have the power to provide 
     for the general welfare of the United States.
       According to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the 
     Constitution: The Congress shall have power to enact this 
     legislation to regulate commerce with foreign nations, and 
     among the several states, and with the Indian tribes.
```
