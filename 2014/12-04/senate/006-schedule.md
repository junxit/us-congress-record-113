# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, the Senate will proceed to executive session, with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees.
  At 10 a.m. the Senate will proceed to five rollcall votes on 
confirmation of Franklin Orr to be Under Secretary for Science, 
Department of Energy; Joseph Hezir to be Chief Financial Officer for 
the Department of Energy; and cloture on the nominations of Gregory N. 
Stivers to be U.S. district judge for the Western District of Kentucky; 
Joseph F. Leeson to be U.S. district judge for the Eastern District of 
Pennsylvania; and Lydia Kay Griggsby to be a judge of the U.S. Court of 
Federal Claims.
  There will be another series of up to six rollcall votes at 1:45 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
