# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5659

**Measures:** H.R. 5659

```
           By Mr. STUTZMAN:
       H.R. 5659.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution which states, 
     ``Congress shall have the power...to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, and among the several states and among the 
     Indian Tribes.''
```
