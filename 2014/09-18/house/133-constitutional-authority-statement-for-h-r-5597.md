# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5597

**Measures:** H.R. 5597

```
           By Mr. GOHMERT:
       H.R. 5597.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under the 10th Amendment to the U.S. Constitution, ``The 
     powers not delegated to the United States by the 
     Constitution, nor prohibited by it to the States, are 
     reserved to the States respectively.'' The power to 
     completely control what school children eat for breakfast or 
     lunch is not an enumerated power of Congress and therefore a 
     decision best left to the States.
```
