# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, September 18 through 
Wednesday, November 12, the majority leader and Senators Rockefeller, 
Reed of Rhode Island, Carper, Coons, and Cardin be authorized to sign 
duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
