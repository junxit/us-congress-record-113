# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 488

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 14, 2014

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I would like to state that on March 4, 
2014, I would have voted in favor of H. Res. 488, supporting the people 
of Venezuela as they protest peacefully for democracy, a reduction in 
violent crime and calling for an end to recent violence. I am pleased 
that this resolution expressing support for basic human rights passed 
the House decisively.
```
