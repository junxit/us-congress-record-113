# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Compassionate and merciful God, we give You thanks for giving us 
another day.
  During the upcoming week of constituency visits, give the Members of 
this assembly insight, inspiration, and industry to work for the good 
of our country. Sustain our citizens with Your power, that they might 
be true to the highest and best they know and are able to achieve.
  As Members visit with those whom they represent, may solutions that 
work toward the betterment of all in our Nation emerge in open and 
respectful conversation.
  May the assurance of Your love and the presence of Your truth abide 
in all our hearts and all our homes.
  And may all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
