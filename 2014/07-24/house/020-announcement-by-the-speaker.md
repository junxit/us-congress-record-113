# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

```
                      ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

  The SPEAKER. The Chair will remind the House that on July 24, 1998, 
at 3:40 p.m., Officer Jacob J. Chestnut and Detective John M. Gibson of 
the United States Capitol Police were killed in the line of duty 
defending the Capitol against an intruder armed with a gun.
  At 3:40 p.m. today, the Chair will recognize the anniversary of this 
tragedy by observing a moment of silence in their memory.
```
