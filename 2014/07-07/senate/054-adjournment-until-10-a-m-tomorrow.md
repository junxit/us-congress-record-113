# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 496

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it adjourn under the provisions of S. 
Res. 496 as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Alan 
John Dixon, former U.S. Senator from the State of Illinois.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:09 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, July 8, 2014, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
