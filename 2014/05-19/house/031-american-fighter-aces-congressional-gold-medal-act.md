motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 685) to award a

country's freedom throughout the history of aviation warfare, as 
amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Huizenga) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
