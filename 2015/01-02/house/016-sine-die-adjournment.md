# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, in accordance with House 
Concurrent Resolution 125, the Chair declares the second session of the 
113th Congress adjourned sine die.
  There was no objection.
  Thereupon (at 1 o'clock and 7 minutes p.m.), the House adjourned.
```
