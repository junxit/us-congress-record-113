# TRIBUTE TO ELAINE BALSLEY

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ELAINE BALSLEY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Elaine Balsley, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past few 
months.
  Elaine is a graduate of Stevens High School in Rapid City, SD. 
Currently, she is attending Black Hills State University where she is 
majoring in mass communications. She is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Elaine 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
