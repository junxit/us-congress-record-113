# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 7, S.Res. 76

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. Leahy):
       S. Res. 76. A resolution designating room S-126 of the 
     United States Capitol as the ``Senator Daniel K. Inouye 
     Room'' in recognition of his service to the Senate and the 
     people of the United States; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. MORAN (for himself, Mr. Alexander, Ms. Ayotte, 
             Mr. Barrasso, Mr. Boozman, Mr. Burr, Mr. Chambliss, 
             Mr. Coburn, Mr. Cornyn, Mr. Crapo, Mr. Cruz, Mr. 
             Enzi, Mr. Flake, Mr. Grassley, Mr. Hatch, Mr. Heller, 
             Mr. Hoeven, Mr. Inhofe, Mr. Isakson, Mr. Johanns, Mr. 
             Paul, Mr. Portman, Mr. Risch, Mr. Roberts, Mr. Rubio, 
             Mr. Thune, Mr. Toomey, Mr. Vitter, and Mr. Wicker):
       S. Con. Res. 7. A concurrent resolution expressing the 
     sense of Congress regarding conditions for the United States 
     becoming a signatory to the United Nations Arms Trade Treaty, 
     or to any similar agreement on the arms trade; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.
```
