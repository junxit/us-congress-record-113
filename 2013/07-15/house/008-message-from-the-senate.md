# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 43, H.R. 2289

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill and a Concurrent 
Resolution of the House of the following titles:

       H.R. 2289. An act to rename section 219(c) of the Internal 
     Revenue Code of 1986 as the Kay Bailey Hutchison Spousal IRA.
       H. Con. Res. 43. Concurrent Resolution authorizing the use 
     of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for a 
     ceremony honoring the life and legacy of Nelson Mandela on 
     the occasion of the 95th anniversary of his birth.
```
