# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2190

**Measures:** H.R. 2190

```
           By Mr. MORAN:
       H.R. 2190.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This Bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution,

[[Page H2958]]

     which provides Congress with the power to regulate commerce 
     and relations between the United States and Indian Tribes, 
     and to pass all laws necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution the foregoing powers, as well as all other Power 
     vested by the Constitution.
```
