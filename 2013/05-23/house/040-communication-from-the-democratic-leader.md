# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, May 23, 2013.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker, U.S. Capitol, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to the National Foundation 
     on the Arts and Humanities Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 955(b) 
     note), I am pleased to re-appoint of The Honorable Betty 
     McCollum of Minnesota to the National Council on the Arts.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     Democratic Leader.
```
