# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 16, 2013.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. Capitol,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to Section 4(b) of the World 
     War I Centennial Commission Act (Pub. L. 112-272), I hereby 
     appoint Mr. Robert Dalessandro of Alexandria, Virginia, to 
     the World War I Centennial Commission.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     Democratic Leader.
```
