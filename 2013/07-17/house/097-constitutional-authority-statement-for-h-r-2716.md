# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2716

**Measures:** H.R. 2716

```
           By Mr. MORAN:
       H.R. 2716.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This Bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution which provides Congress with 
     the power to lay and collect taxes and regulate commerce 
     among the several states.
```
