# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Landrieu, Mary L. (L000550) [D-LA]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing scheduled 
before the Subcommittee on Water and Power of the Committee on Energy 
and Natural Resources has been postponed. This hearing was scheduled to 
be held on Tuesday, June 11, 2013, at 10 a.m., in room 366 of the 
Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the hearing was to receive testimony on the November 
6, 2012, referendum on the political status of Puerto Rico and the 
administration's response.
  For further information, please contact Allen Stayman at (202) 224-
7865 or Danielle Deraney at (202) 224-1219.


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet on Wednesday, June 12, 2013, in SR-
301, Russell Senate Office Building, at 10 a.m., to conduct a hearing 
on the nomination of Davita Vance-Cooks, of Virginia, to be the Public 
Printer.
  For further information regarding this hearing, please contact Lynden 
Armstrong at the Rules and Administration Committee, (202) 224-6352.


            committee on small business and entrepreneurship

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship will meet on June 13, 2013, at 10 
a.m. in room 428A, Russell Senate Office Building to hold a markup of 
pending legislation.
```
