# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that we now 
proceed to a period of morning business with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each, and we can do that until 7:30 tonight; 
and during that period of time, it will be for debate only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Iowa.
```
