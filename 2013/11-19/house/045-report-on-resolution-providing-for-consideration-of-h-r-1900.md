# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1900, NATURAL GAS PIPELINE PERMITTING REFORM ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 420, H.R. 1900

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1900, NATURAL 
                   GAS PIPELINE PERMITTING REFORM ACT

  Mr. BURGESS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-272) on the resolution (H. Res. 420) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1900) to provide for the timely 
consideration of all licenses, permits, and approvals required under 
Federal law with respect to the siting, construction, expansion, or 
operation of any natural gas pipeline projects, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
