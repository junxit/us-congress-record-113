# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 982, H.R. 3350

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Steve Cohen, 
     or a designee to H.R. 982, the Furthering Asbestos Claim 
     Transparency (FACT) Act of 2013, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                          Offered By Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 3350, ``Keep Your Health Plan Act 
     of 2013,'' do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9 of rule XXI of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
