# THOUGHTS AND PRAYERS FOR THE PEOPLE OF OKLAHOMA

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

```
            THOUGHTS AND PRAYERS FOR THE PEOPLE OF OKLAHOMA

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, before I begin, let me offer my thoughts 
and prayers for the people of Oklahoma, who are in the middle of a 
devastating disaster. We in New Jersey know what that kind of 
devastation can mean, and our hearts go out to the victims and their
```
