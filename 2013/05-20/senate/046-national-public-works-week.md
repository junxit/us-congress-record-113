# NATIONAL PUBLIC WORKS WEEK

```
                       NATIONAL PUBLIC WORKS WEEK

  Mrs. BOXER. I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of S. Res. 149, submitted earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 149) designating the week of May 19 
     through May 25, 2013, as ``National Public Works Week.''

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mrs. BOXER. I ask unanimous consent the resolution be agreed to, the 
preamble be agreed to, and the motions to reconsider be laid on the 
table, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 149) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')
```
