# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 130, S. 157, S. 256, S. 304, S. 459

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker announced his signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 130. An act to require the Secretary of the Interior to 
     convey certain Federal land to the Powell Recreation District 
     in the State of Wyoming.
       S. 157. An act to provide for certain improvements to the 
     Denali National Park and Preserve in the State of Alaska, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 256. An act to amend Public Law 93-435 with respect to 
     the Northern Mariana Islands, providing parity with Guam, the 
     Virgin Islands, and American Samoa.
       S. 304. An act to direct the Secretary of the Interior to 
     convey to the State of Mississippi 2 parcels of surplus land 
     within the boundary of the Natchez Trace Parkway, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 459. An act to modify the boundary of the Minuteman 
     Missile National Historic Site in the State of South Dakota, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
