# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1001

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 1001

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1001

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1001, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Bonner of Alabama, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprinting pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
