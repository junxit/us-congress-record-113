# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, JUNE 6, 2013, AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 161

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, JUNE 6, 2013, AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the

[[Page S3964]]

Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the provisions of 
S. Res. 161 as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
Senator Frank R. Lautenberg of New Jersey.

Thursday, June 6, and does so under the provisions of S. Res. 161 as a 
further mark of respect to the late Senator Frank R. Lautenberg of New 
Jersey.

6, 2013, at 9 a.m.
```
