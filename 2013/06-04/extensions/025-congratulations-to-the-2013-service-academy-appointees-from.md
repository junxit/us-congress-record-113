# CONGRATULATIONS TO THE 2013 SERVICE ACADEMY APPOINTEES FROM THE 21ST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF TEXAS

**Speaking:** Smith, Lamar (S000583) [R-TX]

```

Academy; John Edward Monday, Jr., Boerne--Samuel V. Champion High 
School, United States Military Academy; Clara Elizabeth Navarro, Rice 
University, United States Naval Academy; James Lyn Pazdral, Greystone 
Preparatory School at Schreiner University, United States Military 
Academy; Albert Dixon Patillo III, Heritage School, United States 
Military Academy; Rafael David Ramos-Michael, Brackenridge High School, 
United States Naval Academy; and Kirsten S. Redmon, United States 
Military Preparatory School/Sam Houston High School, United States

  Again, congratulations to these outstanding students. I know they
```
