# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 and clause 9 of rule 
XX, proceedings will resume on questions previously postponed. Votes 
will be taken in the following order:
  Ordering the previous question on House Resolution 83; adopting House 
Resolution 83, if ordered; and agreeing to the Speaker's approval of 
the Journal.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
