# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. BAUCUS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 26, the 
nomination of Jack Lew to be Secretary of the Treasury, with 8 hours 
for debate equally divided in the usual form; that upon the use or 
yielding back of time, the Senate proceed to vote without intervening 
action or debate on the nomination; that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, with no intervening action or 
debate; that no further motions be in order; that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
