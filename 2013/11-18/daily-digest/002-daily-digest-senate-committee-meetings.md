International Centre for Missing and Exploited Children, Alexandria, 
Virginia; Patrick Murck, Bitcoin Foundation, Washington, D.C.; Jeremy 
Allaire, Circle Internet Financial, Boston, Massachusetts; and Jerry 
Brito, George Mason University Mercatus Center, Arlington, Virginia.
