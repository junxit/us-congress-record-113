# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mrs. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1471. An act to authorize the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs and the Secretary of the Army to reconsider decisions 
     to inter or honor the memory of a person in a national 
     cemetery, and for other purposes.
       S. 1545. An act to extend authorities related to global 
     HIV/AIDS and to promote oversight of United States programs.
```
