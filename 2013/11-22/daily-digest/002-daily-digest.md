# Daily Digest/

**Measures:** S. 1217

```
Committee Meetings
HOUSING FINANCE REFORM
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine housing finance reform, focusing on developing a 
plan for a smooth transition, including S. 1217, to provide secondary 
mortgage market reform, after receiving testimony from James E. 
Millstein, Millstein and Co., Washington, DC.; John F. Bovenzi, Oliver 
Wyman, Alexandria, Virginia; Mark Zandi, Moody's Analytics, Malvern, 
Pennsylvania; and David K. Min, University of California Irvine School 
of Law.
```
