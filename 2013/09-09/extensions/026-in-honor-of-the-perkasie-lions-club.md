# IN HONOR OF THE PERKASIE LIONS CLUB

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Michael G. (F000451) [R-PA]

```
                  IN HONOR OF THE PERKASIE LIONS CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. MICHAEL G. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 9, 2013

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, for 75 years, the Perkasie Lions Club 
has dedicated their time to bettering both the lives of those in their 
community and disadvantaged individuals abroad. Currently meeting only 
five blocks from where it first assembled, and the club's reach and 
positive impact has only grown with its age. Since 1938, they have 
completed a variety of projects, with their main focus being assistance 
to the blind. Not only does the Lions Club provide medical equipment 
and glasses to those unable to afford them, but they also help enrich 
the lives of children within the community. The members have made 
comprehensive improvements to Lions Camp Kirby, where hearing and 
visually impaired children can take part in a variety of activities. 
From exploring the outdoors to toasting marshmallows around the camp 
fire, deserving young people are able to enjoy these opportunities 
regardless of financial standing. The Perkasie Lions Club has an 
impressive history in Bucks County, and I look forward to their 
continued contributions to the community.
```
