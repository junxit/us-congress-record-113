# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3068

**Measures:** H.R. 3068

```
           By Mr. BENISHEK:
       H.R. 3068.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8 which 
     allows Congress to regulate trade amongst the Indian Tribes. 
     This bill is enacted pursuant to treaties lawfully entered 
     into and ratified pursuant to the power granted to Congress 
     under Article II, Section 2, Clause 2. This bill is enacted 
     pursuant to Article III Section 2 which grants Congress power 
     to regulate jurisdiction in courts inferior to the United 
     States Supreme Court.
```
