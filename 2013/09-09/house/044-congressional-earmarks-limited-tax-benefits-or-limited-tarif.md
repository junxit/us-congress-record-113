# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                          Offered by Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 2775, to condition the provision of 
     premium and cost-sharing subsidies under the Patient 
     Protection and Affordable Care Act upon a certification that 
     a program to verify household income and other qualifications 
     for such subsidies is operational, and for other purposes, do 
     not contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, 
     or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI 
     of the Rules of the U.S. House of Representatives.

                          Offered by Mr. Upton

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Energy and Commerce in H.R. 2775 do not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
