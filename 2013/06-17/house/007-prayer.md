# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  God, our Father, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  Bless the Members of the people's House as they return to Washington.

[[Page 9249]]

May their energy to address our Nation's issues be renewed following 
their visits home for the Father's Day weekend.
  Continue to bless all who work in the Capitol. May our citizens be 
mindful of their generous service to the operations of government and 
supportive of them as they toil in relative anonymity day in and day 
out.
  We ask that what all those who work within these hallowed Halls do 
would be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
