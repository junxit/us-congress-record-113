# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to a period of morning business from now until 6:40 p.m. to allow a 
colloquy between Senator Brown and Senator Isakson.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. When that time is up, I ask unanimous consent to be 
recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Georgia.
  Mr. ISAKSON. I ask unanimous consent to be recognized along with 
Senator Brown of Ohio for up to 15 minutes and to engage in a colloquy.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
