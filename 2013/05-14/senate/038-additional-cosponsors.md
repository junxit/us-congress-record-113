                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 214

  At the request of Ms. Klobuchar, the name of the Senator from 
California (Mrs. Boxer) was added as a cosponsor of S. 214, a bill to 
prohibit brand name drug companies from compensating generic drug 
companies to delay the entry of a generic drug into the market.

Connecticut (Mr. Blumenthal) was added as a cosponsor of S. 217, a bill

improve the disability compensation evaluation procedure of the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs for veterans with mental health
