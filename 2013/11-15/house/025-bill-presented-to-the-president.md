# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 14, 
2013, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval the following bill.

       H.R. 2747. To amend title 40, United States Code, to 
     transfer certain functions from the Government Accountability 
     Office to the Department of Labor relating to the processing 
     of claims for the payment of workers who were not paid 
     appropriate wages under certain provisions of such title.
```
