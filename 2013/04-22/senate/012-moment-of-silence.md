# MOMENT OF SILENCE

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

```
                           MOMENT OF SILENCE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
moment of silence to honor the victims of the bombings in Boston, MA.
  (Moment of silence.)
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Wyoming.
  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I wish to thank the leader for that moment 
of silence. I hope everybody in America will keep the people of 
Boston--particularly those who were injured or lost family members and 
those who saw the pain and the tragedy--in their prayers. I hope we 
would keep all the people across America who witnessed that on 
television or saw the replays of it on television in our prayers, and I 
hope the recovery will bring Americans together, as happened on 9/11.

                          ____________________
```
