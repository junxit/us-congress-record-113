# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR OBJECTION

```
                      EXECUTIVE CALENDAR OBJECTION

  Mrs. McCASKILL. Mr. President, at this time, I am objecting to the 
Senate proceeding to the consideration of the nomination of Lt Gen. 
Susan Helms, USAF, Calendar No. 70 in order to provide additional time 
for myself and other Senators to gather additional information 
regarding Lieutenant General Helms' record of service, particularly her 
actions serving as a convening authority for military courts martial.
```
