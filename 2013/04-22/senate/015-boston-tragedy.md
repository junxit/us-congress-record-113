# BOSTON TRAGEDY

```
                             BOSTON TRAGEDY

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, before I begin, I want to take a moment to 
say my thoughts and prayers go out to the good people in Boston and 
other areas where they have had tremendously horrific events and 
attacks. I hope and pray that all those whose lives were impacted by 
these tragic events will have a swift and peaceful recovery.
  I want to commend all of the law enforcement agencies involved in the 
investigation that brought the hunt for the perpetrators to a 
successful end.

                          ____________________
```
