# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 11 a.m., with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, and 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The Senator from Illinois.
```
