# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       7. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Commissioner of 
     Gray County, Texas, relative to a Resolution affirming the 
     rights of our citizens under the 2nd Amendment; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       8. Also, a petition of the Board of Supervisors, Monterey 
     County, California, relative to Resolution No. 13-089 urging 
     the Congress to enact comprehensive immigration reform; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
       9. Also, a petition of the Pima County Board Supervisors, 
     Arizona, relative to Resolution No. 2013-19 urging the 
     Congress to enact comprehensive immigration reform; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```
