# RECOGNIZING BROUCK KUCZYNSKI

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING BROUCK KUCZYNSKI

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Brouck Kuczynski for her hard 
work as an intern in my Republican policy committee office. I recognize 
her efforts and contributions to my office as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Brouck is a native of Virginia and a graduate of Villanova University 
in Pennsylvania. She currently attends the University of Pittsburgh 
School of Law, where she is expected to graduate in a few weeks. She 
has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I thank Brouck for the dedication she has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. I know 
she will have continued success with all of her future endeavors. I 
wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
