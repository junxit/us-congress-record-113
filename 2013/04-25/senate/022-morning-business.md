# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the Senate proceed to a period of morning 
business, and during that period of time Senators be allowed to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Delaware.
  Mr. COONS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to enter into a 
colloquy with the Senator from Alaska for up to 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
