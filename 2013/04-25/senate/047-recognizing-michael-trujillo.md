# RECOGNIZING MICHAEL TRUJILLO

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MICHAEL TRUJILLO

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Michael Trujillo for his hard 
work as an intern in my Cheyenne office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Michael is a native of Laramie and a graduate of Laramie Senior High 
School. He currently attends the University of Wyoming, where he is 
majoring in political science and journalism. He has demonstrated a 
strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our 
office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over 
the last several months.
  I thank Michael for the dedication he has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I know 
he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I wish 
him all my best on his next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
