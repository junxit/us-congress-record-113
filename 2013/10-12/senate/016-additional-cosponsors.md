# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 395

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the name of the Senator from Hawaii 
(Mr. Schatz) was added as a cosponsor of S. 395, a bill to amend the 
Animal Welfare Act to provide further protection for puppies.


                              S.J. RES. 24

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from Alaska 
(Ms. Murkowski) was added as a cosponsor of S.J. Res. 24, a joint 
resolution to amend the Department of Defense Survivor Benefits 
Continuing Appropriations Resolution, 2014 to make continuing 
appropriations for death gratuities and related survivor benefits for 
survivors of deceased members of the Coast Guard.
```
