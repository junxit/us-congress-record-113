# HONORING MATT WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Smith, Adrian (S001172) [R-NE]

```
                         HONORING MATT WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ADRIAN SMITH

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                       Saturday, October 12, 2013

  Mr. SMITH of Nebraska. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my 
constituent Matt Williams of Gothenburg, Nebraska. Matt is the 
President of Gothenburg State Bank and has served as Chairman of the 
American Bankers Association for the last year.
  Matt brought to the position the perspective of a rural banker 
serving farmers, ranchers, and small businesses in rural America. It 
was a point of pride for Nebraska and the Third District for one of our 
own to serve in this position.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Matt Williams and his 
service to his community, Nebraska, and the American Bankers 
Association.

                          ____________________
```
