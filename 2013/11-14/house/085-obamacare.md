# OBAMACARE

**Speaking:** Posey, Bill (P000599) [R-FL]

```
                               OBAMACARE

  (Mr. POSEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. POSEY. Mr. Speaker, the President promised Mr. Griffin in my 
district and millions of other Americans that, if they wanted to, they 
could keep their own doctor. Unfortunately, he found out already that 
is not true.
  Americans were told that they could keep their own insurance company 
if

[[Page H7086]]

they liked it. Unfortunately, that is not true either. They were told 
it would cost less. Unfortunately, that is not true either. They were 
told it would not create a new tax. Unfortunately, that is not true 
either. They were told there would not be any rationing. Unfortunately, 
that is not true either.
  It is not right; it is not fair; and it is not good for the United 
States of America.
```
