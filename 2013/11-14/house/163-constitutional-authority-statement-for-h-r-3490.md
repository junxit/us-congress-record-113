# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3490

**Measures:** H.R. 3490

```
           By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
       H.R. 3490.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This Act is justified by the sixteenth amendment, which 
     grants Congress the power to lay and collect taxes on income.
```
