# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3350, KEEP YOUR HEALTH PLAN ACT OF 2013

**Measures:** H.Res. 413, H.R. 3350

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3350, KEEP 
                      YOUR HEALTH PLAN ACT OF 2013

  Mr. BURGESS (during the Special Order of Mr. Rohrabacher), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 113-265) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 413) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 3350) to authorize health insurance issuers to continue to offer 
for sale current individual health insurance coverage in satisfaction 
of the minimum essential health insurance coverage requirement, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.

                          ____________________
```
