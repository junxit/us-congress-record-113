# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3494

**Measures:** H.R. 3494

```
           By Mr. BLUMENAUER:
       H.R. 3494.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the United States Constitution 
     provides clear authority for Congress to pass legislation 
     regarding our national transportation program and safety 
     regulations within that program.
```
