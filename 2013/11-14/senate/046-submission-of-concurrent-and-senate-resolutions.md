            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

           By Mr. CASEY (for himself, Mr. Roberts, Mrs. Murray,

           By Mr. FRANKEN (for himself and Ms. Klobuchar):
       S. Res. 297. A resolution congratulating the Minnesota Lynx 
     women's basketball team on winning the 2013 Women's National 
     Basketball Association Championship; considered and agreed 
     to.
