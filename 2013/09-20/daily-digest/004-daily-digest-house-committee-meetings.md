# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
RESETTING THE FORCE FOR THE FUTURE: RISKS OF SEQUESTRATION
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Readiness held a hearing 
entitled ``Resetting the Force for the Future: Risks of 
Sequestration''. Testimony was heard from Lieutenant General William M. 
Faulkner, USMC, Deputy Commandant, Installations and Logistics; and 
Lieutenant General Raymond V. Mason USA, Deputy Chief of Staff for 
Logistics.
PREPARING TODAY'S STUDENTS FOR TOMORROW'S JOBS: A DISCUSSION ON CAREER 
AND TECHNICAL EDUCATION AND TRAINING PROGRAMS
Committee on Education and the Workforce: Subcommittee on Early 
Childhood, Elementary, and Secondary Education held a hearing entitled 
``Preparing Today's Students for Tomorrow's Jobs: A Discussion on 
Career and Technical Education and Training Programs''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
CATEGORICAL EXCLUSION UNDER THE NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ACT 
(NEPA) FOR ADDING SPECIES TO THE LACEY ACT'S LIST OF INJURIOUS WILDLIFE
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Fisheries, Wildlife, 
Oceans and Insular Affairs held a hearing entitled ``The Department of 
the Interior's proposal to use a Categorical Exclusion under the 
National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) for adding species to the 
Lacey Act's list of injurious wildlife''. Testimony was heard from 
David Hoskins, Assistant Director Fish and Aquatic Conservation, Fish 
and Wildlife Service; and public witnesses.
INFRASTRUCTURE: ENABLING DISCOVERY AND ENSURING CAPABILITY
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on Space held 
a hearing entitled ``Infrastructure: Enabling Discovery and Ensuring 
Capability''. Testimony was heard from Paul K. Martin, Inspector 
General, National Aeronautics and Space Administration; and Richard 
Keegan, Associate Deputy Administrator, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration.

[[Page D893]]



Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
