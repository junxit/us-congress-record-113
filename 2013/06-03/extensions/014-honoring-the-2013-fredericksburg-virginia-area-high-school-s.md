# HONORING THE 2013 FREDERICKSBURG, VIRGINIA AREA HIGH SCHOOL SENIOR MILITARY ENLISTEES

and eighteen Fredericksburg, Virginia area high school seniors who plan 
to enlist in the United States Armed Forces after graduation. These 
students have excelled in their academic and extracurricular activities 
and I offer my sincere congratulations upon their high school 
graduation.
