# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1848, H.R. 3204

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 21, 
2013, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 3204. To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic 
     Act with respect to human drug compounding and drug supply 
     chain security, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 1848. To ensure that the Federal Aviation 
     Administration advances the safety of small airplanes, and 
     the continued development of the general aviation industry,
```
