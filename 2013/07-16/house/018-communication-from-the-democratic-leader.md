# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic leader:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 15, 2013.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker, U.S. Capitol,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to section 13101 of the 
     Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical 
     Health (HITECH) Act (P.L. 111-5), I hereby reappoint Mr. Paul 
     Egerman of Weston, Massachusetts to the HIT Policy Committee
```
