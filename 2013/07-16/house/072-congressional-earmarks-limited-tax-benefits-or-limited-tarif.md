# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 2667, H.R. 2668

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                          Offered By Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 2667, ``Authority for Mandate Delay 
     Act,'' do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax 
     benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 
     of rule XXI of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives.

                          Offered By Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 2668, ``Fairness for American 
     Families Act,'' do not contain any congressional earmarks, 
     limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined 
     in clause 9 of rule XXI of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
