# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 251, H.R. 254, H.R. 588

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on July 12, 2013, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 251. To direct the Secretary of the Interior to convey 
     certain Federal features of the electric distribution system 
     to the South Utah Valley Electric Service District, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 254. To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
     facilitate the development of hydroelectric power on the 
     Diamond Fork System of the Central Utah Project.
       H.R. 588. To provide for donor contribution acknowledgments 
     to be displayed at the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Visitor 
     Center, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
