# GOLD STAR WIVES DAY

```
                          GOLD STAR WIVES DAY

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Judiciary 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of and the Senate 
now proceed to S. Res. 67.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report the resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 67) designating April 5, 2013, as 
     ``Gold Star Wives Day.''

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mrs. MURRAY. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed 
to, the preamble be agreed to, and the motions to reconsider be made 
and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 67) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in the Record of March 
5, 2013, under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
