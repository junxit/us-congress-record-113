# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 141

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Ms. JACKSON LEE. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 8 o'clock and 30 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 141, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, April 10, 2013, at 10 
a.m., for morning-hour debate, as a further mark of respect to the 
memory of the late Baroness Margaret Thatcher.

                          ____________________
```
