# Text of Senate Amendment 798

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
  SA 798. Mr. INHOFE submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 601, to provide for the conservation and development 
of water and related resources, to authorize the Secretary of the Army 
to construct various projects for improvements to rivers and harbors of 
the United States, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the end of title II, add the following:

     SEC. 20__. VERDIGRIS RIVER BASIN WATER SUPPLY STORAGE 
                   CONTRACTS.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any community 
     entity that is a party to a contract in effect on the date of 
     enactment of this Act for water supply storage on a 
     nonhydropower lake within the Verdigris River Basin shall be 
     required to pay not more than the contractual rate per acre-
     foot (as in effect on the date of enactment of this Act) in 
     entering into a contract for new water supply storage in a 
     nonhydropower lake within the Verdigris River Basin.
                                 ______
```
