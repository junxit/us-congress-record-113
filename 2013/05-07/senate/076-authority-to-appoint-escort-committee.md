# AUTHORITY TO APPOINT ESCORT COMMITTEE

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                 AUTHORITY TO APPOINT ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. BROWN. Madam President, I ask the President of the Senate be 
authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate to join a 
like committee on the part of the House of Representatives to escort 
Her Excellency Park Geun-hye, the President of South Korea, into the 
House Chamber for the joint meeting at 10:30 a.m., Wednesday, May 8, 
2013.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
