# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 807, FULL FAITH AND CREDIT ACT

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 807, FULL 
                          FAITH AND CREDIT ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-52) on the resolution (H. Res. 202) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 807) to require that the Government 
prioritize all obligations on the debt held by the public in the event 
that the debt limit is reached, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
