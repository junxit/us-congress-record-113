# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 11, 2013

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent in the House 
chamber for votes on Monday, June 3. I would like the record to show 
that, had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall vote 
184, and ``yea'' on rollcall vote 185. I was also inadvertently absent 
for the following votes and would like the record to show that, had I 
been present, I would have voted ``nay'' on rollcall vote 189, and 
``yea'' on rollcall vote 205.
```
