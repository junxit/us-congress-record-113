# PROVIDING FOR ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS TO H.R. 3304

```
           PROVIDING FOR ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS TO H.R. 3304

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 71 which was 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 71), providing for 
     corrections to the enrollment of the bill H.R. 3304.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. PRYOR. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution be 
read a third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 71) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
