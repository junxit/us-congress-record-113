# DISCHARGED NOMINATION

```
                         DISCHARGED NOMINATION

  On December 17, 2013, the Senate Committee on Homeland Security and 
Governmental Affairs was discharged from further consideration of the 
following nomination under the authority of the order of the Senate of 
January 7, 2009 and the nomination was placed on the Executive 
Calendar:

       *MICHAEL G. CARROLL, OF NEW YORK, TO BE INSPECTOR GENERAL, 
     UNITED STATES AGENCY FOR INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT.

  *Nominee has committed to respond to requests to appear and testify 
before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.
```
