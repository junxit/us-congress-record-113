# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3787

**Measures:** H.R. 3787

```
           By Mr. LANKFORD:
       H.R. 3787.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     found in Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1, ``The Congress shall 
     have Power to . . . provide for the common Defence and 
     general Welfare of the United States''.
```
