# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for debate only until 12 
noon, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I note the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. COATS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
