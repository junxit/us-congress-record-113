# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:43 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 307. An act to reauthorize certain programs under the 
     Public Health Service Act and the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act with respect to public health security and all-
     hazards preparedness and response, and for other purposes.
                                  ____

  At 4:20 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 
sections 5580 and 5581 of the revised statutes (20 U.S.C. 42-43), and 
the order of the House of January 3, 2013, the Speaker appoints the 
following Members of the House of Representatives to the Board of 
Regents of the Smithsonian Institution: Mr. Johnson of Texas and Mr. 
Cole of Oklahoma.

                          ____________________
```
