# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2013

**Speaking:** Westmoreland, Lynn A. (W000796) [R-GA]

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2013

  Mr. WESTMORELAND. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Friday, 
January 25, 2013, and when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn 
to meet at 1 p.m. on Tuesday, January 29, 2013.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
```
