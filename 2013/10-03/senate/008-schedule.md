# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

in a period of morning business until 2 p.m. this afternoon, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes. The first hour will

designees. The Republicans will control the first 30 minutes and the
```
