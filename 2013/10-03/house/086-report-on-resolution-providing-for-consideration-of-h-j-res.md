purposes; providing for consideration of motions to suspend the rules; 
waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to 
consideration of certain resolutions reported from the Committee on 
Rules; and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
