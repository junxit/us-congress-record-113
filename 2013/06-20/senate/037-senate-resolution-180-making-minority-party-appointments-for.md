# SENATE RESOLUTION 180--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 113TH CONGRESS

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 180--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 113TH 
                                CONGRESS

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 180

       Resolved, That the following be the minority membership on 
     the following committees for the remainder of the 113th 
     Congress, or until their successors are appointed:
       Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Mr. 
     Thune, Mr. Wicker, Mr. Blunt, Mr. Rubio, Ms. Ayotte, Mr. 
     Heller, Mr. Coats, Mr. Scott, Mr. Cruz, Mrs. Fischer, Mr. 
     Johnson of Wisconsin, and Mr. Chiesa.
       Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: 
     Mr. Coburn, Mr. McCain, Mr. Johnson of Wisconsin, Mr. 
     Portman, Mr. Paul, Mr. Enzi, Ms. Ayotte, and Mr. Chiesa.
       Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship: Mr. 
     Risch, Mr. Vitter, Mr. Rubio, Mr. Paul, Mr. Scott, Mrs. 
     Fischer, Mr. Enzi, Mr. Johnson of Wisconsin, and Mr. Chiesa.

                          ____________________
```
