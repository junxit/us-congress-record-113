# HONORING RAYMOND CLARK THOMPSON

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                    HONORING RAYMOND CLARK THOMPSON

  (Ms. FRANKEL of Florida asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to say that on 
Sunday, Vietnam war veteran Raymond Clark Thompson's name is being 
added to the Vietnam War Memorial Wall where he will be remembered for 
his valiant service in the Army and extraordinary sacrifice for our 
country.
  A native of Indiana and the oldest of six children, Ray served in the 
Vietnam War as a radio specialist.
  Mr. Speaker, I would like to waive my time, and I will try again in a 
few minutes.

                          ____________________
```
