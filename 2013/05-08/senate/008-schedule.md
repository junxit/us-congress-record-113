# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of Senator 
McConnell, the Senate will be in morning business until 10 a.m. At 10 
a.m., the Senate will recess until 11:30 to allow for the joint meeting 
of Congress with the President of the Republic of Korea. When the 
Senate reconvenes, we will resume consideration of S. 601, the Water 
Resources Development Act. At 2 p.m. there will be three rollcall votes 
in relation to amendments to the bill.
```
