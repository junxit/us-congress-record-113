# TRIBUTE TO HANNAH MUDD

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO HANNAH MUDD

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Hannah Mudd, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Hannah is a graduate of St. Vincent de Paul High School in 
Perryville, MO. Currently, she is attending Saint Mary's College, where 
she is majoring in political science and history. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Hannah for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
