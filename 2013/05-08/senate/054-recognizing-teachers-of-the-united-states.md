# RECOGNIZING TEACHERS OF THE UNITED STATES

```
               RECOGNIZING TEACHERS OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the HELP 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. Res. 126 and 
that the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 126) recognizing the teachers in the 
     United States for their contributions to the development and 
     progress of our country.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution be agreed to, the preamble be agreed to, and the motions to 
reconsider be made and laid upon the table, with no intervening action 
or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 126) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
