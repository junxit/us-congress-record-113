# TRIBUTE TO KARINA KIEWEL

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO KARINA KIEWEL

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Karina Kiewel, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Karina is a graduate of Dakota Valley High School in North Sioux 
City, SD. Currently, she is attending the University of Kansas, where 
she is majoring in political science and environmental studies. She is 
a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her 
internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Karina for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
