  Ms. SINEMA. Mr. Speaker, due to my attendance at the memorial service 
in Arizona for the Prescott Fire Department's Granite Mountain Hotshots 
who lost their lives in the Yarnell Fire, I will miss votes this 
evening, July 8, 2013. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' 
on all three measures, H.R. 1341--Financial Competitive Act of 2013, 
H.R. 1564--Audit Integrity and Job Protection Act, and H.R. 11711--FOR 
VETS Act of 2013, under consideration this evening.
  Mrs. KIRKPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, due to my attendance at the memorial 
service in Arizona for the Prescott Fire Department's Granite Mountain 
Hotshots, I will miss votes this evening, July 8, 2013. Had I been 
present, I would have voted the following way on these suspension 
votes: H.R. 1341 Financial Competitive Act of 2013--I would have voted 
``yes''; H.R. 1564 Audit Integrity and Job Protection Act--I would have 
voted ``yes''; H.R. 1171 FOR VETS Act of 2013--I would have voted 
``yes.''
