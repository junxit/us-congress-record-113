# Text of Senate Amendment 740

**Speaking:** Pryor, Mark L. (P000590) [D-AR]

```
  SA 740. Mr. PRYOR (for himself and Mr. Blunt) submitted an amendment 
intended to be proposed by him to the bill S. 743, to restore States' 
sovereign rights to enforce State and local sales and use tax laws, and 
for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:
  At the end, add the following:

     SEC. __. EXTENSION OF INTERNET TAX FREEDOM ACT.

       (a) In General. Section 1101 of the Internet Tax Freedom 
     Act (47 U.S.C. 151 note) is amended by striking ``November 1, 
     2014'' and inserting ``November 1, 2024''.
       (b) Grandfathering of States That Tax Internet Access.--
     Section 1104(a)(2)(A) of such Act is amended by striking 
     ``November 1, 2014'' and inserting ``November 1, 2024''.

                          ____________________
```
