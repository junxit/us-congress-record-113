# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2013

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 62

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2013

  Mr. GOHMERT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this order, it adjourn 
to meet at 10 a.m. on Friday, November 1, 2013, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in 
House Concurrent Resolution 62, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
