# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
November 7, 2013, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:
       AIR FORCE NOMINATION OF LT. GEN. SUSAN J. HELMS, TO BE 
     LIEUTENANT GENERAL, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON MARCH 19, 
     2013.
       BEN S. BERNANKE, OF NEW JERSEY, TO BE UNITED STATES 
     ALTERNATE GOVERNOR OF THE INTERNATIONAL MONETARY FUND FOR A 
     TERM OF FIVE YEARS, (REAPPOINTMENT), WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON APRIL 18, 2013.
       THOMAS EDGAR WHEELER, OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, TO BE A 
     MEMBER OF THE FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION FOR THE 
     REMAINDER OF THE TERM EXPIRING JUNE 30, 2013, VICE JULIUS 
     GENACHOWSKI, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON MAY 9, 2013.
```
