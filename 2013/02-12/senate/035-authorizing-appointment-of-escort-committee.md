# AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

```
              AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Presiding 
Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part 
of the Senate to join a like committee on the part of the House to 
escort President Obama into the House Chamber for the joint session to 
be held tonight at 9 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
