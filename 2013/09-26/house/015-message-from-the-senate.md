# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 252. An act to reduce preterm labor and delivery and the 
     risk of pregnancy-related deaths and complications due to 
     pregnancy, and to reduce infant mortality caused by 
     prematurity.

  The message also announced that the Senate concurs in the amendment 
of the House to bill (S. 793), ``An Act to support revitalization and 
reform of the Organization of American States, and for other 
purposes.''
```
