       CAIR has apparently been laundering money obtained from

     Registration Act.

     communications at Frank Gaffney's Center for Security Policy,

     money laundering operation. In addition to violating its 
     501(c)3 regulations, CAIR's undisclosed and hidden foreign 
     donations amount to violation of the Foreign Agent
