# TAIWAN OBSERVER STATUS ACT

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1151

```
                       TAIWAN OBSERVER STATUS ACT

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to Calendar No. 90, H.R. 1151.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1151) to direct the Secretary of State to 
     develop a strategy to obtain observer status for Taiwan at 
     the triennial International Civil Aviation Organization 
     Assembly, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the bill be read 
the third time and passed, and the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid on the table, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1151) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

```
