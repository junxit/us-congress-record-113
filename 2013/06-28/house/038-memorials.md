                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as

       72. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 175 memorializing the Congress to 
     codify into law a Department of Defense standard for 
     religious freedom that would be applied to all uniformed 
     services; to the Committee on Armed Services.

     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 91 
     memorializing the Congress to prevent unnecessary and

     the Committee on Financial Services.
