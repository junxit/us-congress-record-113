# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.J. Res. 96

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 96

```
            By Mr. McKINLEY:
        H.J. Res. 96.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       According to Article I, Section 9, Clause 7 of the 
     Constitution: No money shall be drawn from the Treasury but 
     in Consequence of Appropriations made by Law; and a regular 
     Statement and Account of the Receipts and Expenditures of all 
     public Money shall be published from time to time.
```
