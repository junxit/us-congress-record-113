# prayer

                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray. Immortal, invisible God only wise, we look to You today 
for wisdom as our lawmakers seek to do what is best for our Nation and 
world. Lord, we admit that our human intellects have limits, for we 
know not what tomorrow holds, but You do. So guide us, sovereign king. 
We are pilgrims on planet Earth. We are weak but You are mighty; lead 
us with Your powerful hand. Amen.
