# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 15, 2013

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 15, 2013

  Mr. REID. I now ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, July 15, 2013; 
that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that the 
majority leader be recognized; and following the remarks of the two 
leaders, the time until 5:30 p.m. be divided equally between the two 
leaders or their designees, with Senators permitted during that time to 
speak for up to 10 minutes; further, that at 5:30 p.m. I be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
