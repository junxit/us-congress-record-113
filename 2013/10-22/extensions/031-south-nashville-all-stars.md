# SOUTH NASHVILLE ALL-STARS

```
                       SOUTH NASHVILLE ALL-STARS

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. MARSHA BLACKBURN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2013

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. Speaker, Tennessee is home to many young talents. 
Whether in the music, technology, health care, or artistic communities, 
America's youth find success. I'm please today to rise and add one more 
group to the list of outstanding young talent: baseball players.
  The South Nashville All-Stars headed earned their place in the World 
Series with a 5-3 win over Henrico, VA in the southeast region final. 
While they did not leave the Series with the title, they returned home 
to Middle Tennessee to fans, friends, and family all proud of their 
outstanding accomplishments.
  I ask my colleagues to join with me in celebrating the hard work, 
perseverance, and dedication of: Austin Kasick #14, Ben Pickman #32, 
Blake Kirchenbauer #3, Blake Money #15, Bricen Russell #23, Christopher 
McElvain #7, Connor Smith #13, Knox Preston #24, Robert Hassell #9, 
Tanner Morgan #1, Trae McLemore #21, and Zane Denton #25.
  I join with their friends, parents, coaches, and supporters in 
celebrating their wonderful season.

                          ____________________
```
