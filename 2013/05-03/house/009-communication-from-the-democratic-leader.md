# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                      Washington, DC, May 3, 2013.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. Capitol,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to section 672(b) of the 
     National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2013 (Pub. 
     L. 112-239), I am pleased to appoint the following 
     individuals to the Military Compensation and Retirement 
     Modernization Commission:
       Mr. Christopher Carney, Dimock, PA.
       General Peter W. Chiarelli, Seattle, WA.
       Thank you for your attention to these appointments.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     Democratic Leader.
```
