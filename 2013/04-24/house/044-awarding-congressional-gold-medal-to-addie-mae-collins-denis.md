AWARDING CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL TO ADDIE MAE COLLINS, DENISE McNAIR, 
                  CAROLE ROBERTSON, AND CYNTHIA WESLEY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the vote on the 
motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 360) to award 
posthumously a Congressional Gold Medal to Addie Mae Collins, Denise 
McNair, Carole Robertson, and Cynthia Wesley to commemorate the lives 
they lost 50 years ago in the bombing of the Sixteenth Street Baptist 
Church, where these 4 little Black girls' ultimate sacrifice served as 
a catalyst for the Civil Rights Movement, as amended, on which the yeas 
and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Alabama (Mr. Bachus) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
