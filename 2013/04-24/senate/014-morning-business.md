# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with equal time 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the Republicans controlling the first half.
```
