# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business for debate only until 4 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The President pro tempore is recognized.
```
