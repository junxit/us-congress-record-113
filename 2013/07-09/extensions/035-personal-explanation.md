and pass H.R. 1092);

  ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 290 (on agreeing to H.Res. 274);
  ``yes'' on rollcall vote No. 291 (on agreeing to the Grayson 
Amendment to H.R. 1613);
  ``yes'' on rollcall vote No. 292 (on the motion to recommit H.R. 1613 
with instructions);
  ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 293 (on passage of H.R. 1613);
  ``yes'' on rollcall vote No. 294 (on the motion to suspend the rules 
and pass H.R. 1864);
  ``yes'' on rollcall vote No. 295 (on agreeing to the Hastings of 
Florida Amendment to H.R. 2231);
  ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 296 (on agreeing to the Flores Amendment

with instructions); and
  ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 304 (on passage of H.R. 2231).
