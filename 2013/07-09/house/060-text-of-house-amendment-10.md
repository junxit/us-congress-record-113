# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Turner, Michael R. (T000463) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                         Offered by: Mr. Turner

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to reduce the nuclear forces of the United States in 
     contravention of section 303(b) of the Arms Control and 
     Disarmament Act (22 U.S.C. 2573(b)).
```
