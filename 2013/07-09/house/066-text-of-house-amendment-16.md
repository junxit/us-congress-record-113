**Speaking:** Castor, Kathy (C001066) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                   Offered By: Ms. Castor of Florida

       Amendment No. 16: Page 22, line 8, before the period, 
     insert the following:
     : Provided, That the amount made available under this heading
```
