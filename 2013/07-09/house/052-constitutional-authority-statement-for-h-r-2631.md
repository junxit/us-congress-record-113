# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2631

**Measures:** H.R. 2631

```
           By Mr. BARLETTA:
       H.R. 2631.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18. This is the `necessary and 
     proper clause' that grants Congress the authority to make all 
     laws necessary for enforcing the Constitution.
```
