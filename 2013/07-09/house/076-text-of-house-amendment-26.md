# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Barrow, John (B001252) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                   Offered By: Mr. Barrow of Georgia

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to implement, administer, or enforce any authority, 
     in any preceding provision of this Act, to use funds for the 
     purchase or hire of motor vehicles.
```
