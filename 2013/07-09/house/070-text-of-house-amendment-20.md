# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mike (K000376) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                 Offered By: Mr. Kelly of Pennsylvania

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to develop or submit a proposal to expand the 
     authorized uses of the Harbor Maintenance Trust Fund 
     described in section 9505(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986.
```
