# CERTIFICATE OF ELECTION

```
                        CERTIFICATE OF ELECTION

  The VICE PRESIDENT. The Chair lays before the Senate a Certificate of 
Election to fill the vacancy created by the death of Senator Frank 
Lautenberg of New Jersey. The certificate, the Chair is advised, is in 
the form suggested by the Senate. If there is no objection, the reading 
of the certificate will be waived and it will be printed in full in the 
Record.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:

                          State of New Jersey


                        CERTIFICATE OF ELECTION

       To the President of the Senate of the United States:
       This is to certify that on the sixteenth day of October, 
     2013, Cory Booker, was duly chosen by the qualified electors 
     of the State of New Jersey, a Senator for the unexpired term 
     ending at noon on the 3rd day of January, 2015, to fill the 
     vacancy in the representation from said State in the Senate 
     of the United States caused by the death of Frank Lautenberg.
       Given, under my hand and the Great Seal of the State of New 
     Jersey, this twenty-eighth day of October two thousand and 
     thirteen.
       By the Governor:
                                                   Chris Christie,
                                                         Governor.
     [State Seal Affixed]

                          ____________________
```
