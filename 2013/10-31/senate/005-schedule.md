# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of Senator 
McConnell, the Senate will proceed to executive session to consider the 
nomination of Congressman Watt to be Director of the Federal Housing 
Finance Agency. The time until noon will be equally divided between the 
two leaders or their designees. At noon, Senator-elect Booker will 
become a U.S. Senator. Following the swearing in, there will be a 
cloture vote on the Watt nomination.
```
