# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, November 21, 
2013, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bills:

       S. 252. An act to reduce preterm labor and delivery and the 
     risk of pregnancy-related and complications due to pregnancy, 
     and to reduce infant mortality caused by prematurity, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 1545. An act to extend authorities related to global 
     HIV/AIDS and to promote oversight of United States programs.
```
