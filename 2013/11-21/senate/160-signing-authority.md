# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Thursday, November 21, through 
Monday, December 9, Senators Warren, Kaine, and Rockefeller be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
