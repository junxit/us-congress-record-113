# CHUCK BAROCH

```
                              CHUCK BAROCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2013

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Chuck Baroch for receiving the 2013 Golden Mayor's Award for 
Excellence.
   This award recognizes extraordinary contributions to the Golden 
community and is presented to Chuck Baroch for his long service leading 
the Golden Civic Foundation, which supports economic development and 
nonprofit organizations in Golden, and for his 13 years on Golden City 
Council, the last six as mayor. He enhanced relationships with the 
Colorado School of Mines and represented Golden well in the disputes 
over the ``Northwest Parkway,'' as well as contributed to the city's 
sustainability efforts. He brought an inclusive and gentlemanly manner 
to his interactions.
   I extend my deepest congratulations to Chuck Baroch for this well 
deserved recognition by Mayor Marjorie Sloan and the City of Golden. 
Thank you for your dedication to our community.

                          ____________________
```
