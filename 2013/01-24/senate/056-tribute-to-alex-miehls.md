# TRIBUTE TO ALEX MIEHLS

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ALEX MIEHLS

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Alex Miehls, a 
2012 intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard work he 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Alex is a graduate of University of Canton in Canton, OH. Alex also 
served Ohio as the vice chairman of the Ohio College Republican 
Federation. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted 
to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Alex for 
all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
