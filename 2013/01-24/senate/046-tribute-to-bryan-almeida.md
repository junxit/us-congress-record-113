# TRIBUTE TO BRYAN ALMEIDA

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BRYAN ALMEIDA

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Bryan Almeida, a 
2012 intern in my Washington, DC, office for all of the hard work he 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Bryan is a rising junior at the George Washington University. 
Currently, he is majoring in political communications and minoring in 
statistics. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted 
to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Bryan 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come. ●
```
