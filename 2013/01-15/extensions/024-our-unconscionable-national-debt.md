# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

```
                    OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 15, 2013

  Mr. COFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, On January 3, 2009, the day I took office, 
the national debt was $10,627,961,295,930.67.
  Today, it is $16,432,643,996,680.64. We've added 
$5,804,682,700,749.97 to our debt in 4 years. This is a $5.8 trillion 
in debt our nation, our economy, and our children could have avoided 
with a Balanced Budget Amendment. We must stop this unconscionable 
accumulation of debt.

                          ____________________
```
