# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

```
                    OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 19, 2013

  Mr. COFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, on January 20, 2009, the day President 
Obama took 
office, the national debt was $10,626,877,048,913.08.
  Today, it is $16,736,188,026,047.86. We've added 
$6,109,310,977,134.78 to our debt in 3 years. This is $6 trillion in 
debt our nation, our economy, and our children could have avoided with 
a balanced budget amendment.
```
