# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on December 13, 
2013, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 2871. To amend title 28, United States Code, to modify 
     the composition of the southern judicial district of 
     Mississippi to improve judicial efficiency, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2922. To extend the authority of the Supreme Court 
     Police to protect court officials away from the Supreme Court 
     grounds.
```
