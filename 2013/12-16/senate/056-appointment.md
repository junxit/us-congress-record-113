# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Public Law 106-398, as amended by Public Law 108-
7, and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, in consultation 
with the Chairmen of the Senate Committee on Armed Services and the 
Senate Committee on Finance, reappoints the following individuals to 
the United States-China Economic Security Review Commission: William A. 
Reinsch of Maryland for a term beginning January 1, 2014 and expiring 
December 31, 2015, and The Honorable Carte P. Goodwin of West Virginia 
for a term beginning January 1, 2014 and expiring December 31, 2015.
```
