# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 5:30 p.m. with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I would suggest the absence of a quorum 
and ask unanimous consent that the time be equally divided for all 
quorum calls.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CARPER. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
