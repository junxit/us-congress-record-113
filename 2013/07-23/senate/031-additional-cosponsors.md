                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 101

  At the request of Mr. Vitter, the name of the Senator from Wisconsin

governments, and for other purposes.

Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 234, a bill to
