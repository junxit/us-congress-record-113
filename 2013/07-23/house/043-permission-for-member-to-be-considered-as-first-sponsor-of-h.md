# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1012

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1012

be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1012, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Markey of Massachusetts, for the purposes

gentlewoman from California?
```
