# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1012

**Speaking:** Capps, Lois (C001036) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1012

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1012

  Mrs. CAPPS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1012, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Markey of Massachusetts, for the purposes 
of adding cosponsors and requesting re-printings pursuant to clause 7 
of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
