# SENATE BILLS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

```
                 SENATE BILLS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills of the Senate of the following 
titles:

           June 3, 2013:
       S. 982. An Act to prohibit the Corps of Engineers from 
     taking certain actions to establish a restricted area 
     prohibiting public access to waters downstream of a dam, and 
     for other purposes.
           June 13, 2013:
       S. 622. An Act to amend the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act to reauthorize user fee programs relating to new 
     animal drugs and generic new animal drugs.

                          ____________________
```
