# Text of House Amendment 40

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 40: At the end of the bill, (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for ``Energy 
     Programs--Fossil Energy Research and Development'', and 
     increasing the amount made available for ``Corps of 
     Engineers-Civil--Flood Control and Coastal Emergencies'', by 
     $10,000,000.
```
