```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2642, FEDERAL 
           AGRICULTURE REFORM AND RISK MANAGEMENT ACT OF 2013

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-149) on the resolution (H. Res. 295) providing
```
