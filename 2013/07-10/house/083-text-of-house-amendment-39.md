# Text of House Amendment 39

**Speaking:** LaMalfa, Doug (L000578) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                        Offered By: Mr. LaMalfa

       Amendment No. 39: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to regulate activities identified in subparagraphs 
     (A) and (C) of section 404(f)(1) of the Federal Water 
     Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1344(f)(1)(A), (C)).
```
