# Text of House Amendment 38

**Speaking:** Nugent, Richard B. (N000185) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2609

```
H.R. 2609

                         Offered By: Mr. Nugent

       Amendment No. 38: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by a private entity to bring an action against the 
     United States or its agents.
```
