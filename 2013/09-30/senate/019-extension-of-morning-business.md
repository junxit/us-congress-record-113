# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Stabenow, Debbie (S000770) [D-MI]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Ms. STABENOW. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the time 
for morning business, with debate only, be extended until 9:30 p.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and 
that the majority leader be recognized at 9:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
