# DARREL THOMPSON

```
                            DARREL THOMPSON

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this is not the time for me to stand and 
speak about the loss of a staff member whom I feel so strongly about.
  The man I am talking about is Darrel Thompson, who, as most people 
know, has been with me for 10 years. He was Obama's campaign manager 
when he ran for the Senate, and he is a wonderful man. I am sorry it is 
not appropriate for me to take Senate time now.

                          ____________________
```
