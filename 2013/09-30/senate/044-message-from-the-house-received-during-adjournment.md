# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 59

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2013, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on September 29, 2013, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the House agreed to the amendment of 
the Senate to the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 59) making continuing 
appropriations for fiscal year 2014, and for other purposes, with 
amendments, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
