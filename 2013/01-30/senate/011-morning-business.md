# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for 2 hours, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
majority controlling the first half.
  The Senator from Rhode Island.
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Madam President, I am delighted to see the Presiding 
Officer in that seat. I ask unanimous consent to speak for up to 20 
minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
