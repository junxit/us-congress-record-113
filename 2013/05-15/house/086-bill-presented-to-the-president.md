# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 360

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on May 15, 2013, she 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill:

       H.R. 360. To award posthumously a Congressional Gold Medal 
     to Addie Mae Collins, Denise McNair, Carole Robertson, and 
     Cynthia Wesley to commemorate the lives they lost 50 years 
     ago in the bombing of the Sixteenth Street Baptist Church, 
     where these 4 little Black girls' ultimate sacrifice served 
     as a catalyst for the Civil Rights Movement.

                          ____________________
```
