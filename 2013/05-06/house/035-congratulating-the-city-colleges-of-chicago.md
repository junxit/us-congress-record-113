# CONGRATULATING THE CITY COLLEGES OF CHICAGO

```
              CONGRATULATING THE CITY COLLEGES OF CHICAGO

  (Mr. DANNY K. DAVIS of Illinois asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. DANNY K. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, on Friday of last week, 
I had the opportunity to attend the City Colleges of Chicago's 
graduation, seven city colleges with more than 4,000 graduates.
  So I congratulate the mayor of the city of Chicago; the chairman of 
the board of the City Colleges, Dr. Paula Wolff; the chancellor, Cheryl 
Hyman; and all of the faculty and staff. This was the highest number of 
individuals who have ever graduated from the City Colleges of Chicago 
at one time. I congratulate them, all of the administration and all of 
those who made it happen.

                          ____________________
```
