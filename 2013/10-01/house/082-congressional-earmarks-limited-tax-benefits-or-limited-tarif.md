# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:


[[Page 14903]]

                   Offered By Mr. Rogers of Kentucky

       H.J. Res. 70, the National Park Service Operations, 
     Smithsonian Institution, National Gallery of Art, and United 
     States Holocaust Memorial Museum Continuing Appropriations 
     Resolution, 2014 Act, does not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as 
     defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                   Offered By Mr. Rogers of Kentucky

       H.J. Res. 71, the District of Columbia Continuing 
     Appropriations Resolution, 2014 Act, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                   Offered By Mr. Rogers of Kentucky

       H.J. Res. 72, the Veterans Benefits Continuing 
     Appropriations Resolution, 2014 Act, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
