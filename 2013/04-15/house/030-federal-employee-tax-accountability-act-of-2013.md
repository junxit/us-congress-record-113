                          personal explanation

  Mr. ROONEY. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 105 on final passage of H.R. 
249, I inadvertently voted ``nay.'' I would have voted ``aye,'' which 
is consistent with my past position on this legislation. In the 112th 
Congress, I voted ``aye'' on rollcall No. 538 on final passage of H.R. 
828, which is virtually identical to H.R. 249.
