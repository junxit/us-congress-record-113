  The SPEAKER. Without objection, 5-minute voting will continue.

  The SPEAKER. The unfinished business is the vote on the motion to 
suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 882) to prohibit the awarding

threshold unless the prospective contractor or grantee certifies in 
writing to the agency awarding the contract or grant that the 
contractor or grantee has no seriously delinquent tax debts, and for 
other purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

the bill, as amended.
