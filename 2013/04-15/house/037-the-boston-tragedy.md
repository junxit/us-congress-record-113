# THE BOSTON TRAGEDY

```
                           THE BOSTON TRAGEDY

  (Mr. GOHMERT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GOHMERT. Mr. Speaker, this was to be a happy and glorious day in 
Boston. Because of the explosions that were set off by evil people, at 
least two have been killed, we're told, and scores of others wounded.
  Our thoughts and our prayers go out for those who were wounded and 
injured and for the families of those who were killed. That will 
continue as the hurting continues, and may God help us to respond in a 
proper manner.

                          ____________________
```
