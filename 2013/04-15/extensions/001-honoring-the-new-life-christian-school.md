# HONORING THE NEW LIFE CHRISTIAN SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Delaney, John K. (D000620) [D-MD]

```
                 HONORING THE NEW LIFE CHRISTIAN SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN K. DELANEY

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 15, 2013

  Mr. DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the New Life 
Christian School, an educational institution in my district celebrating 
its 25th Anniversary on April 22, 2013.
   Since its founding in 1988, the New Life Christian School has served 
as an organization where our community's young adults can grow 
personally and academically. The school's students have acted as role 
models for their peers, and they should be proud of their academic 
accomplishments.
   I ask that you and my other distinguished colleagues help me in 
honoring the significant occasion of the New Life Christian School's 
25th Anniversary. The school is a model organization and will remain an 
inspiration in our community for many generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
