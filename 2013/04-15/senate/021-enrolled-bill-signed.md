# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2013, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 12, 2013, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 716. An act to modify the requirements under the STOCK 
     Act regarding online access to certain financial disclosure 
     statements and related forms.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2013, 
the enrolled bill was subsequently signed on April 12, 2013, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mr. Leahy).

                          ____________________
```
