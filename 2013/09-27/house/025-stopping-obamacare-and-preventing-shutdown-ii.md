# STOPPING OBAMACARE AND PREVENTING SHUTDOWN II

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
             STOPPING OBAMACARE AND PREVENTING SHUTDOWN II

  (Ms. FOXX asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, if there's one thing I know that hardworking 
taxpayers back home in my district need in these tough economic times, 
it's certainty. It's tough enough putting food on the table and putting 
gas in the car without worrying that an out-of-control Federal 
Government is going to make life harder for you and your family.
  That's why last week my colleagues and I in the House passed 
legislation that provides some of that certainty. We don't want our 
fellow Americans to see their insurance premiums shoot up or lose their 
insurance altogether because of the President's unworkable health care 
law. And we want our government to stay open.
  That's what the American people need, and that's what the House has 
acted to do. Now it's time for the Senate to act as well.

                          ____________________
```
