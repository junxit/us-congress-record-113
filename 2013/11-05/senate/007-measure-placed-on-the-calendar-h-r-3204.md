# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3204

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3204

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3204

  Mr. REID. I am told that H.R. 3204 is at the desk and ready for a 
second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Markey). The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.

       A bill (H.R. 3204) to amend the Federal Food, Drug, and

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings at this time regarding

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be
```
