# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 5, H.Res. 71, H.Res. 85, H.R. 1014

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       106. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Michigan, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 71 memorializing the Congress to pass H.R. 
     1014; to the Committee on the Budget.
       107. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 85 
     urging the support for continuation of the STARBASE program; 
     to the Committee on Education and the Workforce.
       108. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of Nevada, 
     relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 5 urging the Congress 
     to pass the Marketplace Fairness Act; to the Committee on 
     Ways and Means.
```
