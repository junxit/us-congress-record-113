  Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate consider the following nominations, Calendar Nos. 2, 3, 4, 5 and 
6, and all nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, 
Army, Marine Corps, and Navy; that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc; the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be 
in order to any of the nominations; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action, and that the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
