# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 p.m., recessed subject to the call of the 
Chair until 4:04 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Coons).
```
