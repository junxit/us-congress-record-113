                           VOTE EXPLANATIONS

commitments, I was unable to cast a vote relative to rollcall vote Nos.

be U.S. Circuit Judge for the Tenth District, yea on the motion to 
invoke cloture on the nomination of Charles T. Hagel to be Secretary of 
Defense, yea on the nomination of Jacob L. Lew to be Secretary of the 
Treasury, yea on the nomination of Katherine Failla to be U.S. District 
Court Judge for the Southern District of New York, nay on Senate 
amendment No. 25 related to elimination of funding for the National 
Security Working Group, and yea on the motion to invoke cloture on the 
nomination of

[[Page 2892]]

Caitlin Joan Halligan to be U.S. Circuit Judge for the District of 
Columbia Court.
