# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment or recess of the Senate from Friday, December 20 to Monday, 
January 6, the majority leader and Senators Warner and Rockefeller be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
