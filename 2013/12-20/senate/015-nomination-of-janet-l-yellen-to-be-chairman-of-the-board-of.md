# NOMINATION OF JANET L. YELLEN TO BE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD OF GOVERNORS OF THE FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

```
NOMINATION OF JANET L. YELLEN TO BE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD OF GOVERNORS 
                     OF THE FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all time on the 
Yellen nomination is yielded back. The vote will occur on this 
nomination on January 6, 2014.
  The clerk will report the nomination.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       Janet L. Yellen, of California, to be Chairman of the Board 
     of Governors of the Federal Reserve System for a term of four 
     years.
```
