# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 5 o'clock p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SESSIONS. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SESSIONS. I ask unanimous consent that I be allowed to speak as 
in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
