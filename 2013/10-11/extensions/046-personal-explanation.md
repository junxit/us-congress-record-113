# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2013

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent in the House 
Chamber for the following votes on October 8, 2013. Had I been present, 
I would have voted ``nay'' on H.J. Res 84, rollcall vote 530; ``nay'' 
on rollcall vote 531, and ``nay'' on rollcall vote 532.
  While I am a strong supporter of the Head Start program, I oppose the 
Republican piecemeal bills to fund the government. Vulnerable children 
and their families need and deserve critical government services that 
this legislation does not fund. I continue to stand ready to vote for a 
clean continuing resolution to end the Republican Shutdown and fund the 
entire Federal Government, including Head Start, the Community Services 
Block Grants, Child Care aid, and nutrition programs.
```
