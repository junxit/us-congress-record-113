# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           committee on commerce, science, and transportation

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be authorized to 
meet during the session of the Senate on October 11, 2013, at 1 p.m. in 
room 253 of the Russell Senate Office Building. The Committee will 
conduct a hearing entitled, ``The Impacts of the Government Shutdown on 
Our Economic Security.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
