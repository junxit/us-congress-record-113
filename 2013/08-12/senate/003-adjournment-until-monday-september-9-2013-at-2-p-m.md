# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2013,AT 2 P.M.

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 22

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2013,AT 2 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, and 
pursuant to the provisions of S. Con. Res. 22, the Senate stands 
adjourned until 2 p.m. on Monday, September 9, 2013.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:00 and 35 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, September 9, 2013, at 2 p.m.
```
