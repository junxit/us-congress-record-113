# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that during the adjournment or 
recess of the Senate from Wednesday, October 16, through Monday, 
October 28, the majority leader and Senators Kaine and Rockefeller be 
authorized to sign bills or joint resolutions.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S7560, October 16, 2013, in the first column, the 
following language appears: Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that 
during the adjournment or recess of the Senate from Wednesday, 
October 16, through Monday, October 28, the majority leader and 
Senators KING and ROCKEFELLER be authorized to sign bills or joint 
resolutions.
  
  The Record has been corrected to read: Mr. REID. I ask unanimous 
consent that during the adjournment or recess of the Senate from 
Wednesday, October 16, through Monday, October 28, the majority 
leader and Senators KAINE and ROCKEFELLER be authorized to sign 
bills or joint resolutions.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
