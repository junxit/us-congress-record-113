# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration en bloc of the following resolutions, 
which were submitted earlier today: S. Res. 272, S. Res. 273, and S. 
Res. 274.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the resolutions be agreed to, 
the preambles be agreed to, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table on each of these three matters, with no intervening action or 
debate on any of them.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions were agreed to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
