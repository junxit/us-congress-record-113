# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that from Friday, 
January 4, through Monday, January 21, the majority leader be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
