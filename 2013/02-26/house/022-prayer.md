# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, thank You for giving us another day.
  Be with each of us that we might be our very best and prove ourselves 
worthy of Your love and Your grace. Be with the Members of this 
people's House in their work and deliberations this day, that they 
might merit the trust of the American people and manifest the strength 
of our democracy to the nations of the world.
  Without You, O Lord, we can do nothing. With You and in You, we can 
establish a world of peace, goodness, and justice now and into the 
future.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
