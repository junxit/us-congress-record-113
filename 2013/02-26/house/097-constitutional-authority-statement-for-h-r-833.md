# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 833

**Measures:** H.R. 833

```
            By Mr. HUNTER:
        H.R. 833.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Specific authority is provided by Article I, section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution (clauses 12, 13, and 14), 
     which grants Congress the power to raise and support an Army; 
     to provide and maintain a Navy; and to make rules for the 
     government and regulation of the land and naval forces.
```
