# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.J. Res. 19

**Speaking:** Miller, Jeff (M001144) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 19

```
PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.J. Res. 19

  Mr. MILLER of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.J. Res. 19, a 
measure originally introduced by Representative Emerson of Missouri, 
for the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings 
pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
