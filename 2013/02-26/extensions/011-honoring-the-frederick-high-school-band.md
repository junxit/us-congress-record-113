# HONORING THE FREDERICK HIGH SCHOOL BAND

**Speaking:** Delaney, John K. (D000620) [D-MD]

```
                HONORING THE FREDERICK HIGH SCHOOL BAND

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN K. DELANEY

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 26, 2013

  Mr. DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Frederick High 
School Band, an organization in my district celebrating its 100th 
Anniversary on April 27, 2013.
  Since its founding at Frederick County's Boys High School in 1913, 
the Frederick High School Band has served as an organization where our 
community's young adults can grow personally and academically. The 
band's members have acted as role models for their peers, and they 
should be proud of their musical accomplishments.
  I ask that you and my other distinguished colleagues help me in 
honoring the significant occasion of the Frederick High School Band's 
100th Anniversary. The band is a model organization and will remain an 
inspiration in our community for many generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
