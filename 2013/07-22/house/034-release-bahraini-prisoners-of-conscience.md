# RELEASE BAHRAINI PRISONERS OF CONSCIENCE

```
                              {time}  1915
                RELEASE BAHRAINI PRISONERS OF CONSCIENCE

  (Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise to bring attention to the 
plight of Mahdi Abu Dheeb. Mahdi Abu Dheeb is the founder of the 
Bahrain Teachers Association and was arrested after taking part in pro-
democracy protests in 2011. For this so-called crime, he was tortured 
and sentenced to 5 years in prison by a military court.
  As a member of the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission, I call for the 
immediate release of Abu Dheeb and all of the prisoners of conscience. 
Mahdi Abu Dheeb is a nonviolent activist imprisoned for his beliefs. 
His release would send a message that the Bahraini government cares 
about freedom, prosperity, and justice for all of its citizens.
```
