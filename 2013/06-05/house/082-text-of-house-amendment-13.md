# Text of House Amendment 13

**Speaking:** Gingrey, Phil (G000550) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2217

```
H.R. 2217

                   Offered by: Mr. Gingrey of Georgia

       Amendment No. 13: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used in contravention of section 287(g) of the Immigration 
     and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1357(g)).
```
