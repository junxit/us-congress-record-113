# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 444, REQUIRE PRESIDENTIAL LEADERSHIP AND NO DEFICIT ACT

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 444, REQUIRE 
               PRESIDENTIAL LEADERSHIP AND NO DEFICIT ACT

  Mr. WOODALL, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 113-8) on the resolution (H. Res. 48) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 444) to require that, if the 
President's fiscal year 2014 budget does not achieve balance in a 
fiscal year covered by such budget, the President shall submit a 
supplemental unified budget by April 1, 2013, which identifies a fiscal 
year in which balance is achieved, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
