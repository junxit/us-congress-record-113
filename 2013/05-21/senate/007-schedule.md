# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 954

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Following leader remarks today the Senate will be in a 
period of morning business for 1 hour. The majority will control the 
first half, the Republicans the final half. Upon conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate will resume consideration of S. 954, the farm 
bill. I spoke to Chairman Stabenow last night. She indicated that she 
believes there is an opportunity to finish the bill, even this week. I 
certainly hope that is the case. The Senate will recess from 12:30 to 
2:15 today to allow for our weekly caucus meetings.
```
