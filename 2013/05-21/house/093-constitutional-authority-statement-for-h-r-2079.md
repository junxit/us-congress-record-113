# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2079

**Measures:** H.R. 2079

```
            By Mr. RADEL:
        H.R. 2079.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 12, 13, and 14, which grants 
     Congress the power to raise and support an Army, to provide 
     and maintain a Navy; and to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces.
```
