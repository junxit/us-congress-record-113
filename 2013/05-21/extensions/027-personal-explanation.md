# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DENNY HECK

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 21, 2013

  Mr. HECK of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today regarding my 
absence from the House yesterday, Monday, May 20, 2013. Because of this 
absence, I missed three votes on the House floor and would like to 
submit how I would have voted had I been in attendance. The votes were:
  Rollcall No. 161, on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and pass H.R. 
258, the Stolen Valor Act. I would have voted ``yea.''
  Rollcall No. 162, on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and pass H.R. 
1073, the Nuclear Terrorism Conventions Implementation and Safety of 
Maritime Navigation Act of 2013. I would have voted ``yea.''
  Rollcall No. 163, on Approval of the Journal. I would have voted 
``yea.''
```
