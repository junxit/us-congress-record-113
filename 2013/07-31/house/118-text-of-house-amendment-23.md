# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2610

```
H.R. 2610

                         Offered By: Mr. Gosar

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 421.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to 
     provide a grant under the Natural Experiment Grant Program.
```
