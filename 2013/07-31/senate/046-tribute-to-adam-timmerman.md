# TRIBUTE TO ADAM TIMMERMAN

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ADAM TIMMERMAN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Adam Timmerman, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Adam is a graduate of Sioux Falls Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, 
SD. Currently, he is attending University of Kansas, where he is 
majoring in environmental studies. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Adam for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
