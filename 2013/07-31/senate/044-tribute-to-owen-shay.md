# TRIBUTE TO OWEN SHAY

```
                          TRIBUTE TO OWEN SHAY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Owen Shay, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Owen is a graduate of Sunshine Bible Academy in Miller, SD. 
Currently, he is attending South Dakota State University, where he is 
majoring in history. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Owen for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
