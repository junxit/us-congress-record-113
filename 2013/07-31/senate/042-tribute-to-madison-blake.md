# TRIBUTE TO MADISON BLAKE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MADISON BLAKE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Madison Blake, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Madison is a graduate of Liberty High School in Liberty, MO. 
Currently, she is attending the University of Missouri, where she is 
majoring in health
```
