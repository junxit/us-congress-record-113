# TRIBUTE TO SAMUEL REULAND

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO SAMUEL REULAND

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Samuel Reuland, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Samuel is a graduate of White Lake High School in White Lake, SD. 
Currently, he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he is 
majoring in political science and history. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Samuel for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
