# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1300, H.R. 2094, H.R. 2754

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1300. An act to amend the Fish and Wildlife Act of 
     1956 to reauthorize the volunteer programs and community 
     partnerships for the benefit of national wildlife refuges, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Environment and 
     Public Works.
       H.R. 2094. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     increase the preference given, in awarding certain asthma-
     related grants, to certain States (those allowing trained 
     school personnel to administer epinephrine and meeting other 
     related requirements); to the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions.
       H.R. 2754. An act to amend the Hobby Protection Act to make 
     unlawful the provision of assistance or support in violation 
     of that Act, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.

                          ____________________
```
