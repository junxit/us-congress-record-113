# TRIBUTE TO ALAYNA ACKERMAN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ALAYNA ACKERMAN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Alayna Ackerman, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Alayna is a graduate of St. Thomas More High School in Rapid City, 
SD. Currently, she is attending University of South Dakota, where she 
is majoring in criminal justice and political science. She is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Alayna for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
