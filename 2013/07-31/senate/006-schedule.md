# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Following leader remarks the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Transportation, Housing and Urban Development 
appropriations bill. At about 10:45 there will be a rollcall vote in 
relation to the Paul amendment. As I have indicated to him and others, 
we will probably move to table that. That will be up to the two 
managers of the bill, but I understand that is what they are going to 
do--or someone will do.
  Following disposition of the Paul amendment, the Senate will proceed 
to executive session to consider the Jones nomination to be Director of 
the ATF. We will do this vote just as quickly as I can work out an 
appropriate time with the Republican leader.
  Yesterday I filed cloture on the THUD bill. As a result, the filing 
deadline for all first-degree amendments on that bill is 1 p.m. today.

                          ____________________
```
