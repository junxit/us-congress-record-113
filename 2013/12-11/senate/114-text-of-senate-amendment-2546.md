# Text of Senate Amendment 2546

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 2546. Mr. REID (for Mr. Whitehouse) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 263, designating the week of September 23 through 
September 29, 2013, as ``National Estuaries Week''; as follows:

       In the seventh whereas clause of the preamble, strike 
     ``extreme weather events'' and insert ``hurricanes and 
     storms''.
       In the ninth whereas clause of the preamble, insert 
     ``some'' before ``bays in the United States''.

                          ____________________
```
