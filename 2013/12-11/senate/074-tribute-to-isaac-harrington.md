# TRIBUTE TO ISAAC HARRINGTON

```
                      TRIBUTE TO ISAAC HARRINGTON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Isaac Harrington, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Isaac is a graduate of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, SD and the 
University of Notre Dame, where he majored in computer science. He is a 
hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Isaac for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
