# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3696

**Measures:** H.R. 3696

```
            By Mr. McCAUL:
        H.R. 3696.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make all laws necessary and proper 
     for executing powers vested by the Constitution, as 
     enumerated in Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United 
     States Constitution.
```
