# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Environment and the 
Economy began a hearing on the following legislation: ``Federal and 
State Partnership for Environmental Protection Act of 2013''; 
``Reducing Excessive Deadline Obligations Act of 2013''; and ``Federal 
Facility Accountability Act of 2013''. The hearing will reconvene on 
Wednesday, May 22, 2013.
U.S. CONTRIBUTION TO THE FIGHT AGAINST MALARIA
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on Africa, Global Health, 
Global Human Rights, and International Organizations held a hearing 
entitled ``The U.S. Contribution to the Fight Against Malaria''. 
Testimony was heard from Rear Admiral Tim Ziemer, U.S. Global Malaria 
Coordinator, President's Malaria Initiative; and Colonel Peter J. 
Weina, M.D., Deputy Commander, Walter Reed Army Institute of Research, 
Department of Defense.
EYES IN THE SKY: THE DOMESTIC USE OF UNMANNED AERIAL SYSTEMS
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Crime, Terrorism, Homeland 
Security and Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Eyes in the Sky: 
The Domestic Use of Unmanned Aerial Systems''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE TARGETING CONSERVATIVE GROUPS
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a hearing on Internal 
Revenue Service targeting conservative groups. Testimony was heard from 
Steve Miller, Acting Commissioner, Internal Revenue Service; and J. 
Russell George, Treasury Inspector General, Tax Administration.

Joint Meetings

  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
