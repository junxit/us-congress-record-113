# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to extend the 
period of morning business for debate only until 5 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and that the 
majority leader be recognized following morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
