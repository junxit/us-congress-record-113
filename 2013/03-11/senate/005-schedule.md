# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Following leader remarks, the Senate will be in morning 
business until 5 p.m. today. Following that morning business, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to consider the nomination of 
Gary Taranto to be a U.S. circuit judge for the Federal Circuit and the 
nomination of Andrew Gordon to be a U.S. district judge for the 
District of Nevada. At 5:30 there will be at least one rollcall vote--
on the Taranto nomination. We hope the Gordon nomination will be 
confirmed by voice.
  We hope to begin consideration of the continuing appropriations bill 
which we received from the House. It is going to be filed immediately. 
We need to complete action on this bill by the end of the week.
```
