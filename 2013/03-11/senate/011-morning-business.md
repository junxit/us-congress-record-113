# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 5 p.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
