# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 9:02 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, without amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 18. Concurrent resolution providing for the 
     use of the catafalque situated in the Exhibition Hall of the 
     Capitol Visitor Center in connection with memorial services 
     to be conducted in the United States Senate Chamber for the 
     Honorable Frank R. Lautenberg, late Senator from the State of 
     New Jersey.

  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 671. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to submit to 
     Congress an annual report on claims for disabilities incurred 
     or aggravated by military sexual trauma, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2216. An act making appropriations for military 
     construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, related 
     agencies for the fiscal year ending and September 30, 2014, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
