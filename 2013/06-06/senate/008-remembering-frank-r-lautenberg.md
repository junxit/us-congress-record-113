# REMEMBERING FRANK R. LAUTENBERG

```
                    REMEMBERING FRANK R. LAUTENBERG

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this afternoon the Senate will pay its final 
respects to a friend and a respected colleague--and that is an 
understatement--Frank Lautenberg. Frank will lie in repose in the 
Chamber where he spent three decades of his professional life.
  Senator Lautenberg was one of the most effective and productive 
Senators to serve in the Senate and, as we learned yesterday, one of 
the most humorous. His leadership as well as his laughter and kindness 
will be missed.
```
