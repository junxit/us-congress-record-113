# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that at a time to 
be determined by me in consultation with the Republican leader, the 
Senate proceed to executive session to consider nominations Nos. 47 and 
49; that there be 30 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual 
form; that following the use or yielding back of that time, the Senate 
proceed to vote with no intervening action or debate on the nominations 
in the order listed, the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order; that any related statements be printed in 
the Record; that President Obama be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
