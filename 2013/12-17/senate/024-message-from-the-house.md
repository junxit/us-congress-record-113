# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 11:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered

tempore (Mr. Thornberry) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3458. An act to treat payments by charitable 
     organizations with respect to certain firefighters as exempt 
     payments.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Leahy).
```
