    AUTHORITY TO EXTEND THE UNITED STATES-REPUBLIC OF KOREA NUCLEAR

motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 2449) to authorize 
the President to extend the term of the Agreement for Cooperation 
between the Government of the United States of America and the 
Government of the Republic of Korea Concerning Civil Uses of Nuclear 
Energy for a period not to exceed March 19, 2016, on which the yeas and 
nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

gentleman from California (Mr. Royce) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
