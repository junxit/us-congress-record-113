  On page S6519, September 17, 2013, in the first column, under 
the heading SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS, the 
following language appears: By Mr. RUBIO: S. Res. 233. A 
resolution authorizing expendures by the Committee on Veterans' 
Affairs; from the Committee on Veterans' Affairs; to the Committee 
on Rules and Administration.
  
  The Record has been corrected to read: By Mr. SANDERS: S. Res. 
233. A resolution authorizing expenditures by the Committee on 
Veterans' Affairs; from the Committee on Veterans' Affairs; to the 
Committee on Rules and Administration.
