# NAVY YARD TRAGEDY

```
                           NAVY YARD TRAGEDY

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I wish to say a brief word about 
yesterday's tragic and senseless violence at the Washington Navy Yard.
  The men and women who protect our Nation and the men and women in 
uniform and the thousands who serve the Department of Defense make 
enormous sacrifices for us. Facing a workplace gunman should not have 
been one of them. Those who have died, their wounded, their families, 
and loved ones are in our thoughts and in our hearts today.

                          ____________________
```
