# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican Leader, the Senate will be in a period of morning business 
for 1 hour. The majority will control the first half and the 
Republicans the final half.
  Following morning business the Senate will resume consideration of 
the Defense authorization bill. We will debate the sexual assault issue 
for up to 6 hours today. I hope we will reach an agreement on the 
ability to vote on those two amendments. We have worked very hard on 
arriving at a point where we can debate this issue. I hope we can do 
that. I think it would be very appropriate to have that issue resolved 
as quickly as possible.
```
