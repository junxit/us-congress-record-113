# IN RECOGNITION OF MS. JAMIE ROSHON

**Speaking:** Meehan, Patrick (M001181) [R-PA]

```
                   IN RECOGNITION OF MS. JAMIE ROSHON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PATRICK MEEHAN

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 20, 2013

  Mr. MEEHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of the 
country's finest early childhood educators. Ms. Jamie Roshon is a 
preschool teacher at the Pottstown KinderCare Learning Center in 
Montgomery County, Pennsylvania. Jamie is being honored this year with 
the Knowledge Universe Early Childhood Educator Award. She is one of 
only 10 preschool educators in the nation being honored with this 
award.
   At the Pottstown KinderCare Learning Center, Jamie is dedicated to 
teaching children aged two and three years old. Throughout her thirteen 
year tenure at the Pottstown Center, she has become an invaluable asset 
to her students, their families, and her fellow faculty members. This 
honor testifies to how Jamie's efforts each day in the classroom are 
making a profound difference in our children's lives.
   Mr. Speaker, I thank Ms. Jamie Roshon for her excellent work 
enriching the lives of young children, and I congratulate her on this 
well-deserved honor.

                          ____________________
```
