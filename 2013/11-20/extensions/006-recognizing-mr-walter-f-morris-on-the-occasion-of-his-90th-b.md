# RECOGNIZING MR. WALTER F. MORRIS ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 90TH BIRTHDAY

```
 RECOGNIZING MR. WALTER F. MORRIS ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 90TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANIEL WEBSTER

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 20, 2013

  Mr. WEBSTER of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Mr. 
Walter F. Morris on the occasion of his 90th birthday. Mr. Morris is a 
Central Florida resident and veteran of World War II. During the war, 
Mr. Morris bravely served as a B-17 navigator, flying 30 missions over 
Europe.
  World War II veterans, regarded as ``The Greatest Generation,'' have 
demonstrated heroic commitment to preserving the freedom and prosperity 
of our nation. Their sacrifices and their families' sacrifices defeated 
the forces of tyranny and advanced freedom to millions of people. We, 
the United States and future generations of Americans, owe World War II 
veterans a debt of gratitude that can never be repaid but is remembered 
and honored in our daily lives.
  It is my distinct pleasure, as a representative of the people of 
Central Florida, to recognize Mr. Morris and all members of America's 
Armed Forces for their dedication of service to our country.

                          ____________________
```
