# HONORING THE MARTIN'S MILL LADY MUSTANGS

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

```
                HONORING THE MARTIN'S MILL LADY MUSTANGS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JEB HENSARLING

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2013

  Mr. HENSARLING. Mr. Speaker, today I would like to honor the Martin's 
Mill Lady Mustangs basketball team, which won the Texas Class A 
Division I High School Basketball Championship on March 2, 2013. This 
victory marked the third time since 2006 the Lady Mustangs have 
captured the title.
   I would like to especially recognize team members Alyssa Pate, 
Brianne Miller, Hannah Celsur, Cassidy McCoy, Taylor Munns, Haily 
Jenkins, Bailey Caldwell, Cheyenne Brown, Madison Daniel, Meagan 
Weatherford, and Hailey Hawes. I would also like to recognize Head 
Coach Doug Barncastle; Assistant Coaches Laura Jenkins and Don Tarrant; 
and Managers Hailey Celsur, Jacee Greenlee, Jillian Jones, Megan 
Lafleur, and Adrianna Weatherford.
   On behalf of the Fifth District of Texas, I am honored to 
congratulate the Martin's Mill Lady Mustangs--both players and 
coaches--for their talent, dedication, and exceptional performance. I 
am confident that this is an accomplishment these young ladies will 
remember for the rest of their lives.

                          ____________________
```
