# CONGRATULATING FORT BEND CHRISTIAN ACADEMY

```
               CONGRATULATING FORT BEND CHRISTIAN ACADEMY

  (Mr. OLSON asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, Fort Bend Christian Academy rules. Yesterday, 
the Eagles won two State titles.
  The day started with the Lady Eagles winning their fourth State 
championship in softball, the fourth title in 7 years. Behind Coach 
Ferguson Carroll and Elizabeth Fox's 12 strikeouts, the Lady Eagles 
crushed Fort Worth Christian 4-0.
  Right after that game ended, the Fort Bend Christian men won their 
first State baseball title with a close 12-11 victory over Midland 
Christian. Coach Roman and the team never quit, scoring four runs in 
the top of the sixth inning to take the lead for good.
  There's an old saying in naval aviation: don't mess with an eagle 
unless you know how to fly. The Fort Bend Christian men and women are 
Eagles who know how to fly. Those Eagles are Texas State champs.

                          ____________________
```
