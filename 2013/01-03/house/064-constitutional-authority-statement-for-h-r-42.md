# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 42

**Measures:** H.R. 42

```
            By Mrs. BACHMANN:
        H.R. 42.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article One, Section Eight, wherein it states ``Congress 
     shall have power . . . to raise and support Armies.''
```
