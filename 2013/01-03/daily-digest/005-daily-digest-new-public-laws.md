Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2006 to clarify that a period of

as a translator, interpreter, or in a security-related position in an

residence and physical presence in the United States for purposes of 
qualifying for naturalization. Signed on December 28, 2012. (Public Law

revise certain administrative authorities of the District of Columbia 
courts, and to authorize the District of Columbia Public Defender 
Service to provide professional liability insurance for officers and 
employees of the Service for claims relating to services furnished 
within the scope of employment with the Service. Signed on December 28, 
2012. (Public Law 112-229)
  S. 2170, to amend the provisions of title 5, United States Code, 
which are commonly referred to as the `Hatch Act', to scale back the 
provision forbidding certain State and local employees from seeking 
elective office, clarify the application of certain provisions to the 
District of Columbia, and modify the penalties which may be imposed for 
certain violations under subchapter III of chapter 73 of that title. 
Signed on December 28, 2012. (Public Law 112-230)
  S. 2367, to strike the word ``lunatic'' from Federal law. Signed on

234)
