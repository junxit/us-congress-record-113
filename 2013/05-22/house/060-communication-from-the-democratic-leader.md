# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, May 17, 2013.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. Capitol, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to section 4(c) of House 
     Resolution 5, 113th Congress, I am pleased to re-appoint The 
     Honorable James P. McGovern of Massachusetts as Co-Chair of 
     the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
