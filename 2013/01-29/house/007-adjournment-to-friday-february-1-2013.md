# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2013

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2013

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, when the House adjourns 
today, it shall adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. on Friday, February 1, 2013; 
and further when the House adjourns on that day, it shall adjourn to 
meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, February 4, 2013.
  There was no objection.
```
