# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 30, 2013

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 30, 2013

  Mr. BROWN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, January 30, 2013; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning 
business for 2 hours with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each, with the majority controlling the first hour and the 
Republicans controlling the final hour; further, that at 2:30 p.m. 
Senator Kerry be recognized for up to 30 minutes for the purpose of 
delivering his farewell address.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
