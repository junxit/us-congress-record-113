# PROVIDING FOR MEMBERS ON THE PART OF THE SENATE OF THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

**Measures:** S.Res. 88

```
PROVIDING FOR MEMBERS ON THE PART OF THE SENATE OF THE JOINT COMMITTEE 
     ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY

  Mr. MERKLEY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to consideration of S. Res. 88, which was submitted earlier 
today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The assistant bill clerk read as follows:
  A resolution (S. Res. 88) providing for members on the part of the 
Senate of the Joint Committee on Printing and the Joint Committee of 
Congress on the Library.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. MERKLEY. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to 
and the motion to reconsider be laid on the table, with no intervening 
action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 88) was agreed to.
  (The resolution is printed in today's Record under ``Submitted 
Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
