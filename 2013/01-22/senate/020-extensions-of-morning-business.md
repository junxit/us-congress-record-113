# EXTENSIONS OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Landrieu, Mary L. (L000550) [D-LA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     EXTENSIONS OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the period of 
morning business be extended until 6 p.m. today, and that all 
provisions of the previous order remain in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. LANDRIEU. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Donnelly). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
