# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of January 23 through January 25, 2013

                             Senate Chamber

  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Foreign Relations: January 23, to hold hearings to 
examine Benghazi, focusing on the attacks and lessons learned, 9 
a.m., SH-216.
  January 24, Full Committee, to hold hearings to examine the 
nomination of John F. Kerry, of Massachusetts, to be Secretary of 
State, 10 a.m., SH-216.
  Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: January 24, 
to hold hearings to examine the state of America's mental health 
system, 10 a.m., SD-430.
  Committee on the Judiciary: January 23, to hold hearings to 
examine the nominations of Nelson Stephen Roman, and Analisa 
Torresto, both to be a United States District Judge for the Southern 
District of New York, Raymond P. Moore, to be United States District 
Judge for the District of Colorado, Derrick Kahala Watson, to be 
United States District Judge for the District of Hawaii, and Claire 
R. Kelly, of New York, to be a Judge of the United States Court of 
International Trade, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: January 24, to hold closed 
hearings to examine certain intelligence matters, 2:30 p.m., SH-219.
```
