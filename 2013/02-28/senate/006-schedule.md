# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will be in morning business for 1 hour. The Republicans will control 
the first half, the majority the final half. Following morning 
business, the Senate will resume consideration of the American Family 
Economic Protection Act.
  At a time to be determined today, there will be two cloture votes on 
the motions to proceed to S. 388 and S. 16, which are the Democratic 
and Republican sequestration bills. Senators will be notified when the 
votes are scheduled. I will work that out with Senator McConnell.
```
