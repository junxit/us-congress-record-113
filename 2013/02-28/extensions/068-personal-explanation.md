```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. YVETTE D. CLARKE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 28, 2013

  Ms. CLARKE. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained in my district 
and missed the vote on Monday, February 25, 2013. Had I been present, I 
would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall No. 47, H.R. 667--To redesignate
```
