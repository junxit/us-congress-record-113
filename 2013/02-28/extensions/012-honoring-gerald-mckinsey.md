# HONORING GERALD McKINSEY

```
                        HONORING GERALD McKINSEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 28, 2013

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of one of 
my constituents, Gerald McKinsey of Greensburg, Indiana.
  Gerald was a life-long resident of Greensburg, working in 
manufacturing at the local Honda automotive facility and, before that, 
at Gecom. On a personal note, my brother Rich and I have very fond 
memories of summer days spent on sports, bikes, and video games with 
Gerald and his brother, Jeff. Their friendships, and the friendship of 
their entire family, were a very important part of our childhood. Those 
memories will never be forgotten.
  I ask the entire 6th District to keep Gerald's mother, Faye, his 
daughter, Kayla, and son, Keegan, along with the entire extended 
McKinsey family in your thoughts and prayers.

                          ____________________
```
