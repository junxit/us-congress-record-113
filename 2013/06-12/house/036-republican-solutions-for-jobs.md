# REPUBLICAN SOLUTIONS FOR JOBS

```
                     REPUBLICAN SOLUTIONS FOR JOBS

  (Mr. CHABOT asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CHABOT. Madam Speaker, according to last week's jobs report, 
nearly 12 million Americans--12 million of our fellow citizens--are out 
of work. That's simply unacceptable.
  The American people deserve better than a constant parade of job-
destroying regulations coming out of Washington. They deserve better 
than a government that continues to spend us further into debt.
  The American people deserve solutions that will create jobs today, 
and that's what the House Republican plan is all about. We have a plan 
to grow our economy and secure the future for all Americans, to expand 
opportunity, not government.
  We want to rein in massive government spending and reform the Tax 
Code to make it simpler and fairer for all Americans. That's the House 
Republican plan. It's one of growth, prosperity, and unlimited 
opportunities for all Americans.

                          ____________________
```
