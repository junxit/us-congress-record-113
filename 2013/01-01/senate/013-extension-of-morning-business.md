# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
period for morning business for debate only be extended until 5 p.m., 
with Senators to speak up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask to speak for 15 minutes but 
probably not that long.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
