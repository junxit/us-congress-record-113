# WAIVER OF PARCHMENT PRINTING

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 147

```
                      WAIVER OF PARCHMENT PRINTING

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to H. Con. 
Res. 147.

[[Page S8634]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 147) waiving the 
     requirement that measures enrolled during the remainder of 
     the One Hundred Twelfth Congress be printed on parchment.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to and the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 147) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
