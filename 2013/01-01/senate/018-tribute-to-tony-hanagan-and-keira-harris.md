# TRIBUTE TO TONY HANAGAN AND KEIRA HARRIS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                TRIBUTE TO TONY HANAGAN AND KEIRA HARRIS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, Tony Hanagan and Keira Harris are two 
former pages who returned to the Senate, graciously volunteering to 
sacrifice some of their Christmas vacation to help here on the Senate 
floor this past weekend. Tony and Keira have worked tirelessly to 
complete work typically performed by 14 pages. We appreciate their help 
during the Senate's recent late nights. We thank them for their great 
effort and impeccable service to the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
