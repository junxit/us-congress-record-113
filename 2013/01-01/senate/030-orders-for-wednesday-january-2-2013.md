# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 2, 2013

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 2, 2013

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 p.m. tomorrow, 
January 2, 2013; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to a period 
of morning business until 1:30 p.m. for debate only with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
