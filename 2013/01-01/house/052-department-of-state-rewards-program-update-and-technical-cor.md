# DEPARTMENT OF STATE REWARDS PROGRAM UPDATE AND TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT OF 2012

**Measures:** S. 2318

```
 DEPARTMENT OF STATE REWARDS PROGRAM UPDATE AND TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS 
                              ACT OF 2012

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on

[[Page H7560]]

suspending the rules and passing the bill (S. 2318) to authorize the 
Secretary of State to pay a reward to combat transnational organized 
crime and for information concerning foreign nationals wanted by 
international criminal tribunals, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Florida (Ms. Ros-Lehtinen) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
