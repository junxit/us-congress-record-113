# CORRECTING AND IMPROVING THE LEAHY-SMITH AMERICA INVENTS ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 6621

```
      CORRECTING AND IMPROVING THE LEAHY-SMITH AMERICA INVENTS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate amendment to the bill 
(H.R. 6621) to correct and improve certain provisions of the Leahy-
Smith America Invents Act and title 35, United States Code.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Smith) that the House suspend the rules and 
concur in the Senate amendment.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the Senate amendment was concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
