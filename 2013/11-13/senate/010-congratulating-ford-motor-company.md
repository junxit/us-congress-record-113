# CONGRATULATING FORD MOTOR COMPANY

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   CONGRATULATING FORD MOTOR COMPANY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, Ford Motor Company has a proud 100-year 
history of manufacturing in Kentucky. Today the company announces a new 
model to be constructed in its Louisville assembly plant, further 
employing yet another generation of Kentuckians.

[[Page S7967]]

  I congratulate Ford on this development and applaud its continued 
excellence in manufacturing in the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
  I yield the floor.
```
