**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, as if in executive

Kaplan nominations be modified to be after consultation with Senator
```
