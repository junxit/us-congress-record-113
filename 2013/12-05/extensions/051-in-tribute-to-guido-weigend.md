# IN TRIBUTE TO GUIDO WEIGEND

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

```
                      IN TRIBUTE TO GUIDO WEIGEND

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID SCHWEIKERT

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 5, 2013

  Mr. SCHWEIKERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Guido 
Weigend for his long-term dedication at the Office of Strategic 
Services. His many years of service have impacted us all. I would also 
like to thank him for dedicating the majority of his life to the 
advancement of our nation's youth through his role at Arizona State 
University. We are in sincere gratitude for the vision, guidance, and 
leadership he has provided our country.
```
