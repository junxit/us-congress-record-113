# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 27, 2013

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 30

```
        CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 27, 2013

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 12 noon, on Friday, December 27, 2013, 
unless it receives a message that the House has adopted S. Con. Res. 
30, in which case the Senate stands adjourned until 11:45 a.m., on 
Friday, January 3, 2014.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 and 46 seconds p.m., conditionally 
adjourned until Friday, December 27, 2013, at 12 noon.
```
