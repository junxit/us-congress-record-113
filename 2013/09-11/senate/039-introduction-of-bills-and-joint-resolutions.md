# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. FEINSTEIN (for herself, Ms. Collins, and Mrs. 
             Hagan):
       S. 1494. A bill to amend the Child Care and Development 
     Block Grant Act of 1990 to improve child safety and reduce 
     the incidence of preventable infant deaths in child care 
     settings; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.

       S. 1495. A bill to direct the Administrator of the Federal 
     Aviation Administration to issue an order with respect to 
     secondary cockpit barriers, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. KIRK:
       S. 1496. A bill to enhance taxpayer accountability at 
     public transportation agencies; to the Committee on Banking, 
     Housing, and Urban Affairs.
```
