# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Ms. JACKSON LEE. Madam Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 9 o'clock and 33 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 434, 
the House adjourned until Monday, December 16, 2013, at 11 a.m., as a 
further mark of respect to the memory of the late Nelson Mandela, 
former President of the Republic of South Africa.
```
