# HONORING THE CENTRAL CATHOLIC RAMS

**Speaking:** Blumenauer, Earl (B000574) [D-OR]

```
                   HONORING THE CENTRAL CATHOLIC RAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EARL BLUMENAUER

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2013

  Mr. BLUMENAUER. Mr. Speaker, victories on the field are much sweeter 
when achieved by a team, dedicated in unity to achieving its goals. 
This week the Central Catholic Rams won the OSAA Class 6A Championship, 
defeating their archrival Jesuit 38-28. It was a fitting end to a 14-0 
season and showed what can be done with teamwork and effort.
  In a truly dominant year, both during the regular season and 
playoffs, the young men of Central Catholic, their coaches, and their 
supporters inspired their fellow students and the community through 
their exemplary actions on and off the field. This is a season that can 
inspire great pride as the 2013 Rams join the 1952 and 1953 
championship teams in the halls of glory.
  Go Rams!

                          ____________________
```
