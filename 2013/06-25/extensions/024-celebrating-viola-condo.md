# CELEBRATING VIOLA CONDO

```
                        CELEBRATING VIOLA CONDO

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2013

  Ms. FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Viola 
Condo of West Palm Beach, Florida, who turns 100 years old on July 7, 
2013.
  Viola immigrated to this country from Italy in 1920, when she was 7 
years old, and she has lived in the Sunshine Park Community of West 
Palm Beach since 1976. A member of Saint Juliana's Parish Church, Viola 
is active in the community. She still sings with the Church Choir. She 
has also knitted 526 children's sweaters for World Vision, an 
organization dedicated to reducing world poverty and hunger.
  Viola is truly an exceptional woman whom I am proud to represent in 
Florida's 22nd District. I know I join with her friends and family in 
celebrating this wonderful milestone. I wish her good health and 
continued success in the coming year.

                          ____________________
```
