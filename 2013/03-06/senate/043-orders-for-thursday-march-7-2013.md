# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 7, 2013

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 7, 2013

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Thursday, March 7, 2013; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and following any leader remarks, the Senate resume 
executive session and consideration of the Brennan nomination; further, 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2 p.m. to allow for caucus 
meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
