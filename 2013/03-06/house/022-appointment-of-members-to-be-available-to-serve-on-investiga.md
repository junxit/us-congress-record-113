# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BE AVAILABLE TO SERVE ON INVESTIGATIVE SUBCOMMITTEES OF THE COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BE AVAILABLE TO SERVE ON INVESTIGATIVE 
                SUBCOMMITTEES OF THE COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to clause 5(a)(4)(A) of rule X, and the order of 
the House of January 3, 2013, of the following Members of the House to 
be available to serve on investigative subcommittees of the Committee 
on Ethics for the 113th Congress:
  Mr. Latham, Iowa
  Mr. Thornberry, Texas
  Mr. Forbes, Virginia
  Mr. Bishop, Utah
  Mrs. Blackburn, Tennessee
  Mr. Latta, Ohio
  Mr. Olson, Texas
  Mr. Gardner, Colorado
  Mrs. Roby, Alabama
  Mr. Messer, Indiana
```
