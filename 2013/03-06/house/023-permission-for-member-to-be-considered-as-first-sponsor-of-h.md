# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 313

**Speaking:** Farenthold, Blake (F000460) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 313

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 313

  Mr. FARENTHOLD. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 313, a bill 
originally introduced by Mrs. Emerson of Missouri, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting printings pursuant to clause 7 of rule 
XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
