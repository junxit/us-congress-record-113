# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      AFRICAN AFFAIRS SUBCOMMITTEE

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on June 18, 2013, at 10 a.m., to hold an African Affairs 
subcommittee hearing entitled, ``Examining Prospects for Democratic 
Reform and Economic Recovery in Zimbabwe.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee
```
