# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 15, H.Res. 290

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

     Representatives of the State of Illinois, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 290 urging the Congress, the Department of the

     Albracht's heroic actions at Firebase Kate; to the Committee 
     on Armed Services.
       126. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of Ohio, 
     relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 15 urging the 
     Subcommittee on Communications and Technology to hold regular 
     hearings regarding the nationwide broadband public safety 
     network; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
```
