# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, APRIL 30, 2013

```
                 ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, APRIL 30, 2013

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Tuesday, 
April 30, 2013; and that the order of the House of January 3, 2013, 
regarding morning-hour debate not apply on that date.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Washington?
  There was no objection.
```
