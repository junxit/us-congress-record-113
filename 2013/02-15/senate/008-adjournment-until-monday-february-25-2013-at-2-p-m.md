# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 25, 2013, AT 2 P.M.

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 15

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 25, 2013, AT 2 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 2 p.m., Monday, February 25, 2013, 
pursuant to the provisions of H. Con. Res. 15.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:02 and 26 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, February 25, 2013, at 2 p.m.
```
