# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the order of the Senate of January 3, 2013, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on March 25, 2013, during the adjournment of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the House has agreed to the following concurrent resolution, without 
amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 11. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate and an 
     adjournment of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
