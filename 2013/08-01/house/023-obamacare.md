# OBAMACARE

```
                               OBAMACARE

  (Mr. MULLIN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)

numbers by the Congressional Budget Office, ObamaCare is now going to 
cost the American taxpayers nearly $1.4 trillion.

single day, I must ask my colleagues who support this: Can America
```
