# SENATE RESOLUTION 216--ELECTING LAURA C. DOVE, OF VIRGINIA, AS SECRETARY FOR THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 216

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 216--ELECTING LAURA C. DOVE, OF VIRGINIA, AS 
                SECRETARY FOR THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 216

       Resolved, That Laura C. Dove of Virginia be, and she is 
     hereby, elected Secretary for the Minority of the Senate,
```
