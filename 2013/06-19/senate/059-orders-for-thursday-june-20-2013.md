# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 20, 2013

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 20, 2013

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Thursday, 
June 20, 2013; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, and 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; and that the time until 11:30 a.m. be equally divided and 
controlled between the majority and minority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
