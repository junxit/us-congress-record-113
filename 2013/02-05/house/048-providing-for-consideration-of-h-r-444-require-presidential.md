  Mr. WEBER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 34 I missed the vote

present, I would have voted ``yea.''


                          personal explanation

  Mr. CICILLINE. Mr. Speaker, on the Legislative Day of February 5, 
2013, upon request of a leave of absence, a series of votes were held. 
Had I been present for these rollcall votes, I would have cast the 
following votes: On Ordering the Previous Question for H. Res. 48, 
Providing for consideration of H.R. 444, to require that, if the 
President's fiscal year 2014 budget does not achieve balance in a 
fiscal year covered by such budget, the President shall submit a 
supplemental unified budget by April 1, 2013, which identifies a fiscal 
year in which balance is achieved, and for other purposes (rollcall No. 
33)--I vote ``nay.'' On Agreeing to the Resolution H. Res. 48, 
Providing for consideration of H.R. 444, to require that, if the 
President's fiscal year 2014 budget does not achieve balance in a 
fiscal year covered by such budget, the President shall submit a 
supplemental unified budget by April 1, 2013, which identifies a fiscal 
year in which balance is achieved, and for other purposes (rollcall No. 
34)--I vote ``no.''
