# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

business be extended until 11:30 a.m., and at 11:30 a.m. the Senate

  Mrs. MURRAY. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
