# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1120, PREVENTING GREATER UNCERTAINTY IN LABOR-MANAGEMENT RELATIONS ACT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1120, 
    PREVENTING GREATER UNCERTAINTY IN LABOR-MANAGEMENT RELATIONS ACT

  Ms. FOXX, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 113-32) on the resolution (H. Res. 146) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1120) to prohibit the National Labor 
Relations Board from taking any action that requires a quorum of the 
members of the Board until such time as Board constituting a quorum 
shall have been confirmed by the Senate, the Supreme Court issues a 
decision on the constitutionality of the appointments to the Board made 
in January 2012, or the adjournment sine die of the first session of 
the 113th Congress, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
