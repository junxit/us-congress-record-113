# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2505

**Measures:** H.R. 2505

```
            By Mrs. NEGRETE McLEOD:
        H.R. 2505.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 8, ``Congress Shall have the 
     power to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, among the 
     several States, and with Indian Tribes.''
       and;
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, ``Congress shall have the 
     Power To make all Laws which shall be necessary ad proper for 
     carryng into Execution the foregoing Powers, and all other 
     Powers vested by the Constitution in the Government of the 
     United States, or in any Department of Officer thereof.''
```
