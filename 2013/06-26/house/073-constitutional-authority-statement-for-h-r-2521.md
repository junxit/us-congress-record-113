# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2521

**Measures:** H.R. 2521

```
            By Mr. BEN RAY LUJAN of New Mexico:
        H.R. 2521.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the power of the Congress to regulate Commerce, as 
     enumerated by Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United 
     States Constitution.
```
