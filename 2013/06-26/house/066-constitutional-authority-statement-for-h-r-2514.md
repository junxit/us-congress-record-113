# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2514

**Measures:** H.R. 2514

```
            By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
       H.R. 2514.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 7 of Section 9 of Article I of the Constitution, in 
     that all funds belonging to the Treasury may not be withdrawn 
     except according to law.
```
