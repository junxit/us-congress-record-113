# RECOGNIZING THE VOLUNTEERS FOR THE RAINBOW THERAPEUTIC RIDING CENTER

**Speaking:** Connolly, Gerald E. (C001078) [D-VA]

```

                        HON. GERALD E. CONNOLLY

                        Wednesday, June 26, 2013

  Mr. CONNOLLY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the volunteers 
with the Rainbow Therapeutic Riding Center.
   Established in 1985, the Rainbow Therapeutic Riding Center offers 
equestrian activities to help Prince William County area citizens who 
are facing mental health or physical challenges. In an age where 
technological breakthroughs dominate medicine and where urbanization 
dominates our surroundings, the Rainbow Center has worked diligently to 
preserve horse-riding not only as a simple form of leisure, but also as 
a pleasurable means of facilitating the improvement of physical and 
mental health for many of our citizens.
```
