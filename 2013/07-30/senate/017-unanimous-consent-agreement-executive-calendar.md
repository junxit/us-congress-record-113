# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
motion be withdrawn and that at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader, notwithstanding rule XXII, in consultation with Senator 
McConnell, the Senate proceed to executive session to consider the 
following nomination: Calendar No. 220; that there be 2 hours for 
debate equally divided between the proponents and opponents; that 
following the use or yielding back of time, the Senate proceed to vote, 
with no intervening action or debate on the nomination; that the motion 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order; that 
any related statements be printed in the Record; and that President 
Obama be immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate 
then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
