# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 2218

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 2218

  Mr. REID. I am told H.R. 2218 is at the desk and is due for a second 
reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for a second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2218) to amend subtitle D of the Solid Waste 
     Disposal Act to encourage recovery and beneficial use of coal 
     combustion residuals and establish requirements for the 
     proper management and disposal of coal combustion residuals 
     that are protective of human health and the environment.

  Mr. REID. I now object to any further proceedings at this time.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
