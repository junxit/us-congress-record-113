# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
recess until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus meetings and that 
the time during the recess be counted postcloture, with the time 
charged equally to both sides.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:40 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Ms. 
Baldwin).
```
