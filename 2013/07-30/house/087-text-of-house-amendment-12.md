# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Grayson, Alan (G000556) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2610

```
H.R. 2610

                        Offered By: Mr. Grayson

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       SEC. ___. None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be made available to any airline that reduces the 
     benefits of its frequent flyer program without 180 days prior 
     notice.
```
