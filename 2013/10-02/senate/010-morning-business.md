# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for debate until 12 
noon, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
```
