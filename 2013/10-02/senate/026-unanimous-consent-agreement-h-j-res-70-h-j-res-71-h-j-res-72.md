# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 70, H.J. RES. 71, H.J. RES. 72, H.J. RES. 73; AND H.R. 3230

```
UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 70, H.J. RES. 71, H.J. RES. 72, 
                      H.J. RES. 73; AND H.R. 3230

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that if the Senate 
receives from the House any of the following joint resolutions or bills 
by 11 a.m. on Thursday, October 3, those measures be considered to have 
received their second reading and objection to further proceedings 
considered to have been heard under the provisions of rule XIV during 
Thursday's session: H.J. Res. 70, H.J. Res. 71, H.J. Res. 72, H.J. Res. 
73; and H.R. 3230.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
